# Receive and ship a serialized pack-out

Use an isolated local company. Enable **Take the serial number from the part** before creating the job. The sales part and production part both use serial tracking. Leave the sales part’s serial sequence blank. Give it one pack-out operation and one planned production part per sales unit. Receive two production units with the labels `SN-7` and ` SN-7 ` (one leading and one trailing space). Record their physical tracked-entity IDs and create a sales-part job for two units. For the volume and resale checks, seed at least 1,000 older consumed serials for the sales SKU. Use FIFO, an active accounting period after cutover and two separate draft stock sales shipments.

1. In MES, select an open sales output and open its planned production part’s **Issue → Scan** tab.
   - Scan the padded part’s traveler QR or enter its physical ID. Press Enter. Verify the dialog stays open and displays `␠SN-7␠`, the source ID, job, operation, and sales output ID. Check that Enter sent no Issue request.
   - Rename the source in the local fixture while the dialog remains open. Press **Issue ␠SN-7␠**. Verify the request carried the original spaces and the server reports the serial changed. Check for zero new consumption and pairing rows, unchanged issued quantity, and a blank output serial.
   - Reload, rescan the QR, verify the new serial and same destination, and press the Issue button bearing that serial. Check for exactly one issue and one active pairing. Use **Unconsume** (Give Back) before Log. Verify the input is available again and the output’s inherited serial is blank.
   - Restore the source’s padded label in the fixture. Scan the literal padded serial and press Enter. Verify a single issue with both spaces preserved, then Log it. Give Back must now explain that the logged unit cannot be returned. The sales unit and its source keep separate IDs.
2. In ERP, open the job’s **Complete** dialog. Fail the job receipt snapshot request once in the browser. Verify the dialog offers Retry, cannot submit a receipt, and the job has no sales receipt. Restore the request and retry. Confirm the receipt shows `␠SN-7␠` and its hidden `expectedSerials` field contains the original spaces. Submit a copy of this form with the plain serial and check for a rule error (422) and zero receipts. Submit the displayed form with the optional bin empty. Verify one receipt for that sales output and a job still open for its second piece.
3. Issue the plain production serial and Log the second piece. Verify two quantity-one sales receipts, each with the corresponding sales output ID.
4. On a one-piece stock sales shipment, open the serial picker. Identify both choices by their displayed names. The plain option reads `SN-7 · <sales output ID>`. The padded option reads `␠SN-7␠ · <sales output ID>`. Pick the plain one and reload. Once the available tracking numbers control is visible, scan the literal padded label, blur the field to save and reload. Replay the captured first pick. Check for a conflict (409) and confirm the stored slot still identifies the padded unit.
5. Post the shipment. Check that the sales shipment ledger debits exactly one padded sales unit and that the plain unit remains available. Check the padded unit's physical layer was relieved and the posted COGS equals its receipt cost.
6. Open **More options → Void** and confirm **Void Shipment**. Verify the padded unit is available again, its shipment slot is clear, its restored layer has quantity one at its original cost, and COGS reversed. On the fresh draft choose the padded unit by its menu label, reload and post. Check the second sale relieved that restored layer and posted the original COGS. The plain unit retains its quantity and cost. Remove the fixture company and test employee, close the private browser context, and release the test servers.

The database regressions in `shipment-serial-selection.test.ts` and `job-unit-pairing-cost.test.ts` cover replaying the old shipment selection and shipping the cheaper of two units first.

## Return and resell one packed serial

Use a synthetic company with FIFO and active accounting. Pack one $22 and one $40 unit of the same sales SKU. On one sales shipment, assign the $22 unit to an ordinary sales order line and the $40 unit to a warranty replacement line. Post the shipment. The sale cost ledger should identify both units. In the posted journal check the ordinary line’s $22 COGS and the warranty line’s $40 expense, including each line’s shipment reference.

1. Link a one-piece sales return to the $22 sale line and create its receipt. Have an older anonymous $77 layer open for the SKU. On the ERP receipt choose **Returned serial number 1**, select the $22 unit and confirm **Post Receipt**. Check that the same physical ID is On Hold with a quantity-one $22 layer. The posted receipt debits finished goods and credits COGS by $22.
2. Open the linked sales return line, select **Disposition → Use As Is**, and verify that the returned unit becomes Available. On a fresh sales shipment open **Show available tracking numbers**, select that unit and reload. Before posting, reopen the original shipment and check both serials on their original lines. Reopen the draft and check the returned serial in its editable slot. Confirm **Post Shipment**, then reopen both shipments and verify all three read-only fields.
3. Check the fresh shipment’s physical debit, its posted $22 COGS, the return layer’s remaining quantity of zero, the $77 layer’s remaining quantity of one, and the $40 unit’s consumed status. The native cases in `serialized-shipment-cost.test.ts` include the older pooled-sale history.

## Legacy serial return and second return

In a synthetic FIFO company, stock two serials of one SKU at $22 and $40. Give the $40 serial a null `itemId` and valid Item provenance for the SKU **before** selling it. Pick and post both units on separate shipment lines. Open the linked return receipt for the $40 line, select that physical unit in **Returned serial number 1**, and post. Verify the receipt's +1 item movement, $40 physical layer and posted $40 inventory debit.

Return the $22 serial, release it with **Use As Is**, resell it on a fresh shipment and take it back on a second linked receipt. Attempt to void the first receipt. Both receipts must still be Posted, the latest receipt must still own the on-hand serial, and the ledgers and journal must be unchanged. Void the second receipt and verify its own physical and financial reversal. The native four-method replay is in `serialized-return-ownership.test.ts`.
