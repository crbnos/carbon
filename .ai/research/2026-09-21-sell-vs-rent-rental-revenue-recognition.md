# Sell vs. Rent Manufactured Units — Rental Fleet Accounting & Revenue Recognition: Best Practices Survey

> Date: 2026-09-21
> Prepared for: the revenue recognition build (crbnos/carbon#1048) with the "build a vehicle, then sell it or rent it" use case as the design driver; touches #1041 (fixed assets: make/self-constructed), #1056 (leases: lessor side) and #1060 (program tracker).
> Method: four parallel web surveys (standards + SEC filings; SAP; NetSuite + Dynamics 365; Odoo + rental point solutions) and three read-only sweeps of the Carbon codebase. Every claim carries a source; items the surveys could not confirm against a primary page are marked **unverified**.

## Summary

A manufacturer that rents out a unit it built is a **lessor**, not a seller: under ASC 842 and IFRS 16 the rental is an operating lease whenever the customer controls an identified asset, and the short-term exemption exists only for lessees. The unit therefore leaves inventory **at cost, with no revenue**, into a long-lived "rental fleet" asset class (Tesla's "Operating lease vehicles", United Rentals' "Rental equipment"), keeps depreciating whether or not it is on rent, and earns **straight-line lease income** billed on a cycle. A returned unit is either re-rented or moved **back to inventory at net book value** and sold as a used unit with gross revenue and cost of sales, because selling ex-fleet units is part of ordinary business (ASC 606, not the net gain/loss presentation of ASC 610-20). Sales-type leases (rent-to-own with a bargain purchase option, or a term covering most of the economic life) are a sale at commencement and are the boundary between "rent" and "sell". Every ERP surveyed reaches the same shape with different vocabulary: SAP settles production orders to an asset and bills rental contracts through periodic billing plans; Dynamics 365 Finance has a dedicated *Inventory to Fixed Asset* journal with serial tracking; NetSuite creates fixed assets from inventory adjustments and bills subscriptions with ratable revenue plans; Odoo keeps rented stock company-owned in an internal rental location and defers revenue by date range on invoice lines; rental point solutions add the operational vocabulary (on rent / off rent, cycle billing, dollar utilization). Carbon today has none of it: no inventory→fixed asset path, no rental document, no recurring invoicing, no deferred-revenue writer, and no "at customer" state for a serialized unit.

## Competitors Surveyed

- **US GAAP / IFRS primary guidance (PwC Viewpoint, Deloitte DART, KPMG, EY, Grant Thornton) and SEC filings (Tesla, United Rentals, Hertz, Oshkosh/JLG, Terex, Alta Equipment)** — the accounting the design must produce; the filings show how real manufacturer-lessors present it.
- **SAP S/4HANA** — reference enterprise pattern: production order → asset settlement, SD rental contracts with periodic billing, PM equipment categories for owned-but-rented units, Contract & Lease Management lease-out accounting.
- **NetSuite** — the mid-market system companies IPO on; Fixed Assets Management asset proposals from inventory transactions, SuiteBilling + Advanced Revenue Management, rental SuiteApps.
- **Microsoft Dynamics 365 Finance & Supply Chain / Business Central** — the closest "inventory-to-fixed-asset" primitive (F&SC) and the simplest deferral + recurring-invoice primitives (BC).
- **Odoo** — the one general ERP with a first-class Rental app; shows the operational lifecycle and the "stock stays company-owned" inventory pattern.
- **Equipment-rental point solutions (Texada, Point of Rental, Wynne RentalMan, InspHire)** — fleet asset model, contract lifecycle, cycle-billing conventions, utilization KPIs, rental purchase options.

## Key Consensus Patterns

### 1. A rental of your own product is a lease; the accounting is the lessor model, not ASC 606
- **Standards**: a contract is a lease when it conveys an identified asset and the right to control its use (ASC 842-10-15-10/-15-17/-15-20). Rights "limited to operating or maintaining the asset" do not convey control. United Rentals and Hertz classify even single-day rentals as operating leases under Topic 842 — duration never removes a contract from lease scope. The ≤12-month short-term election lives in ASC 842-20 (lessee) only; a lessor always applies full classification. Non-lease components (maintenance) may be combined with the lease under the lessor practical expedient (ASC 842-10-15-42A) only when the pattern of transfer matches and the lease component would be operating; the predominant component then decides ASC 606 vs lease accounting. IFRS 16 keeps the dual lessor model (operating vs finance) even though it collapsed the lessee model.
- **SAP**: Contract & Lease Management ("Lease-Out Accounting", RE-FX based) implements IFRS 16 / ASC 842 lessor valuation for real-estate and non-real-estate assets. SAP's own "Meier Yellow Industries" reference architecture (an excavator maker renting part of its fleet) instead treats rental income "as a service regulated by IFRS 15" via Subscription Billing — a documented shortcut, not the standards' answer.
- **Rationale**: the customer directs the use of a specific vehicle; that is the definition of a lease. Modelling rentals as an ASC 606 service arrangement would be wrong for an audit-ready manufacturer and is the reason the existing rev-rec spec (#1048) is not sufficient for this use case on its own.

### 2. Fleet units leave inventory at cost and live in their own PP&E class
- **Tesla** (10-K, "Operating lease vehicles"): vehicles delivered under leasing programs move out of inventory into a separate balance-sheet line carried at cost less accumulated depreciation, depreciated straight-line over the lease term to estimated residual value (charged to cost of automotive leasing revenue). **Synthesized from several filings; wording not confirmed against one filing.**
- **United Rentals**: "Rental equipment" is its own PP&E class, recorded at cost, straight-line over 2–20 years to a 0–50% salvage (weighted ~12%), **depreciated whether or not it is out on rent**. Oshkosh/JLG carries "Equipment on operating lease to others"; Alta Equipment splits "rent-to-sell" (depreciated as a percentage of rental revenue) from "rent-to-rent" (straight-line). **Filing details search-synthesized.**
- **Rationale**: the unit is no longer held for sale in the ordinary course; inventory valuation (LCNRV, FIFO layers) stops applying and depreciation starts. The transfer is a reclassification, never a sale.

### 3. ERPs provide an explicit "inventory → fixed asset" primitive, usually serial-aware
- **Dynamics 365 Finance**: the *Inventory to Fixed Asset(s)* journal (Fixed assets → Journal entries) exists "for a legal entity that produces or constructs its own fixed assets", supports serial/batch selection (Reservation function), posts an Acquisition per the FA posting profile, and since 10.0.44 pushes inventory-closing cost adjustments to the asset. Purchase-order acquisition is a second path (one PO line = one asset).
- **NetSuite FAM**: any Inventory Adjustment, Inventory Transfer or Assembly Build posting to a GL account flagged "Asset Account" spawns an **Asset Proposal** that a user accepts to create the asset "in proportion to the reduced inventory". No one-click convert; RSM and a build-then-rent case study document exactly this pattern (the latter **unverified**, page 403).
- **SAP**: two paths — settle the production/internal order to an Asset under Construction then to the final asset (receiver categories AUC → FXA; CO88/KO88, AIAB/AIBU), or issue existing stock to an asset with movement type **241** "GI for asset" (asset account assignment mandatory). The FI line Dr Asset / Cr Inventory for 241 is the expected outcome per community threads, **not confirmed on SAP Help**.
- **Business Central**: acquisition only via purchase invoice, FA G/L journal or the acquisition wizard — no item-journal path (a real gap vs F&SC). **Odoo**: stock → asset is a forum workaround (negative manual valuation, then "Create Asset" on the journal item) — **not a supported flow**.
- **Rationale**: the primitive must consume the inventory layer (quantity and cost) and create the asset in one posting, or the unit is double-counted. Serial awareness is what lets the fleet record answer "which vehicle".

### 4. The rental contract is a time-based document with a periodic billing engine, separate from the quantity-based sales order
- **SAP**: SD rental contract type **MV** (item category **MVN**) with a **periodic billing plan** (next-billing-date rule, horizon; VF04 creates the invoices as dates fall due, prorating partial periods). PM equipment category **X** marks equipment that stays on the owner's books and is given to others on rent, with a serial number and its own maintenance plans.
- **Odoo Rental**: Quotation → Confirmed → Picked-up → On Rent → Returned, with pickup/return validation, late-return fees (duration rounds up, delay rounds down), padding time before re-rent, and serials assigned only on confirmed orders. Pricing is a list of (unit of time, duration, price) lines and Odoo always picks the cheapest applicable line. **No periodic invoicing inside Rental** — long rentals pair a zero-priced rental product with a Subscription product (forum guidance).
- **Business Central ISV (Abakion Rental Management)**: Sales Orders for invoicing + Transfer Orders moving stock between Home / Rental / Return locations, rental period tracked shipment→return, a recurring invoicing batch per period. **NetSuite**: no native rental order type; SuiteBilling subscriptions + ARM revenue plans, rental SuiteApps (SuiteWorks, NetScore, Rand Group) add fleet availability, contracts, cycle billing.
- **Point solutions (Texada, Point of Rental, Wynne, InspHire)**: reservation → delivery / on rent → off rent → return / check-in → invoice; fleet unit statuses **Available / On Rent / In Repair / Sold**; rates by day / week / 4-week or month with best-rate selection; **cycle billing** either calendar-month (12 invoices/yr, prorate 29–31-day months) or 28-day (13 invoices/yr), with Texada applying one rate tier per contract and retroactively truing up when a rental crosses a tier.
- **Rationale**: a sales order is quantity-complete when shipped and invoiced; a rental is open-ended and bills repeatedly. Every system that tried to bend the sales order (Odoo, BC ISV) had to bolt a subscription or batch engine beside it.

### 5. Straight-line lease income; advance billing is deferred and released monthly
- **Standards**: fixed operating-lease payments are recognized straight-line over the lease term (PwC LG 4-16 example: $500/$550/$600 monthly payments recognized at $550/month, the difference in a straight-line rent receivable); variable payments (mileage, hour-meter overage) in the period earned; initial direct costs deferred over the term; a **refundable** security deposit is not a lease payment (a liability), a **non-refundable** one is a fixed payment.
- **Odoo**: invoice lines carry Start/End dates; the deferral engine books the line to deferred revenue and releases by Months / Full Months / Days via a daily cron. **BC**: deferral templates on the item or G/L account spread every posted sale over N periods. **D365 F&SC**: Subscription billing owns billing schedules and deferral schedules together (Revenue recognition module deprecated in 10.0.30). **SAP**: SD time-related revenue recognition type A (VF44) recognizes evenly between accrual start/end dates independent of billing; RE-FX has a straight-lining accrual engine for lease-outs (**program name unverified**).
- **Rationale**: when billing period = accounting period and the amount is level, invoice posting *is* the recognition and needs no deferral; deferral machinery is needed for advance/upfront billing and uneven payments. This is the same "Straight Line by date range" method the existing rev-rec spec designs — rentals are its natural first consumer.

### 6. A returned unit is sold gross when selling used units is ordinary business
- **Standards**: ASC 606 governs "the sale of a car by a car dealer" and other ordinary-course asset sales (gross revenue + cost of sales at NBV); ASC 610-20 (net gain/loss) is for incidental, non-customer asset sales. **United Rentals** reports "Sales of rental equipment" as a revenue line with the equipment's net book value as cost of sales; **Tesla** reclassifies returned lease vehicles to used-vehicle inventory at NBV and sells them gross inside "Services and other" revenue (**search-synthesized**).
- **NetSuite / BC / D365**: all three sell a fixed asset through an invoice (NetSuite Disposal–Sale auto-creates the invoice; BC posts disposal gain/loss from a sales-invoice FA line; F&SC via free-text invoice only) — i.e. the **net** presentation Carbon already has for its Fixed Asset sales-order line.
- **Rationale**: a manufacturer whose used-unit sales are routine should present them gross, which "return to inventory at NBV, then sell normally" produces with existing sales flows; the net fixed-asset sale stays right for genuine one-off disposals.

### 7. Rent-to-own is where a rental becomes a sale
- **Standards**: any of the five criteria (ownership transfer, bargain purchase option reasonably certain, term ≥ ~75% of remaining life, PV ≥ ~90% of fair value, specialized asset) makes the lease **sales-type**: derecognize inventory, recognize revenue and cost of sales at commencement (selling profit), then interest income over the term; direct financing defers the selling profit.
- **Industry (RPO / rent-to-own conversions)**: a share of each rent payment (often 50–100%, front-loaded and capped at 6–12 months) accrues as earned equity credited against the purchase price at conversion; where the option price is fair market value the arrangement can remain a "true lease" and the asset stays on the lessor's books until conversion. **Internal journal mechanics for the conversion were not documented by any vendor reached — unverified.**
- **Rationale**: the classification test is what tells the system "this rental is really a financed sale"; v1 can compute it as a warning and treat an exercised purchase option as a sale on the exercise date.

## Answers to Research Questions

1. **Is renting a manufactured vehicle a lease or a revenue contract?** A lease (ASC 842 lessor / IFRS 16 lessor) — identified asset + customer control, regardless of duration; ASC 606 applies only to bundled non-lease components or when the supplier keeps deployment control (Deloitte DART 3.4, PwC 2.2/2.4, United Rentals & Hertz 10-Ks).
2. **How does a built unit get onto the balance sheet as a rental asset, and back?** Reclassify from inventory to a dedicated fleet PP&E class at cost (no revenue); depreciate to residual whether on rent or idle; on return, either re-rent or reclassify to inventory at NBV (Tesla) and sell gross (United Rentals). ERP primitive: D365 F&SC *Inventory to Fixed Asset* journal, NetSuite asset proposal from an inventory adjustment, SAP movement 241 / order settlement to asset.
3. **How do ERPs model the rental contract and its billing?** A time-based contract document with start/end (or open-ended), rate by period, and a periodic billing plan / cycle-billing engine that proposes invoices as periods fall due (SAP MV + billing plan / VF04; Odoo Rental + Subscriptions; Texada cycle billing; BC ISV recurring batch). Fleet units carry an Available / On Rent / In Repair / Sold status.
4. **How is rental revenue recognized?** Straight-line over the term for fixed payments; variable charges when earned; advance billing deferred (liability) and released monthly; refundable deposits are liabilities, never revenue (PwC 4.5, Deloitte DART ch. 6). Same-period level billing needs no deferral.
5. **How is a returned unit sold — gross or net?** Gross (ASC 606) when used-unit sales are ordinary business, with NBV as cost of sales; net gain/loss (ASC 610-20) only for incidental disposals. Carbon's existing Fixed Asset sales-order line is the net path.
6. **What terminology and KPIs should Carbon adopt?** "Rental agreement" (SAP: rental contract; point solutions: contract), "on rent / off rent", "rental period" and "cycle billing" (calendar-month vs 28-day), fleet status Available / On Rent / In Maintenance / Sold, **time utilization** (rented days ÷ available days, healthy 60–75%) and **dollar utilization** (rental revenue ÷ fleet original cost, ~55–65% for national chains), "rental purchase option (RPO)".

## Competitor-Specific Details

### SAP S/4HANA
- Order settlement to asset: receiver categories AUC then FXA in the settlement profile; AIAB distributes / AIBU settles AuC line items; KO88 (single order) / CO88 (production/process orders collectively). Movement type 241 issues unrestricted stock to an asset with a mandatory asset account assignment. Documentation pages were JS-rendered; mechanics corroborated across community threads, static mirrors and KBAs.
- SD rental contract type MV / item category MVN, periodic billing plan (next-billing-date rule, horizon), VF04 / RVFPLA01 creates invoices as dates fall due with proration. Equipment category **S** = sold to customer; **X** = owned, rented out, serialized, own maintenance plans.
- Asset sale with customer: ABAON (manual revenue) or an SD order line with account-assignment category "AS" pointing at the asset so billing posts the retirement (**label currency unverified**).
- Revenue: SD time-related recognition (type A) via VF44; RE-FX straight-lining for lease-outs; RAR's lease scope boundary **unverified**. Meier Yellow Industries reference architecture: rental as subscription (IFRS 15) via SAP Subscription Billing + S/4 Sales Order Management.

### NetSuite
- FAM is a SuiteApp; assets come from **Asset Proposals** raised by transactions (vendor bill, journal, inventory adjustment/transfer, item receipt, assembly build) hitting an asset-flagged account. Disposal–Sale enters customer, sale item and amount; NetSuite creates the invoice and posts gain/loss to an Asset Sale Gain/Loss account. Lease Accounting chapter is lessee-shaped; lessor depth **unverified / likely absent**.
- SuiteBilling (flat/usage/tiered recurring charges) + ARM (revenue element → arrangement → recognition plan, e.g. 12 ratable monthly entries). No native rental transaction; rental businesses use SuiteApps (SuiteWorks Rental Management, NetScore Rental, Sererra RentalSeries, Rand Group) for fleet availability, serialized custody, return inspection and cycle billing.

### Microsoft Dynamics 365
- **Finance & Supply Chain**: *Inventory to Fixed Asset(s)* journal for self-produced assets with serial/batch reservation; PO acquisition (one line = one asset); FA sale only via free-text invoice (transaction type "Disposal – sale"); **Asset leasing is lessee-only** ("accounting … on behalf of lessee"); Revenue recognition deprecated in 10.0.30 → **Subscription billing** (billing schedules + deferral schedules, straight-line or event-based).
- **Business Central**: FA acquisition via purchase invoice / FA G/L journal / wizard (no item-journal path); disposal via sales invoice FA line with auto gain/loss (must be the last FA entry; partial disposal needs the reclassification journal first); **deferral templates** on item or account; **Recurring Sales Lines** on the customer card; rental via AppSource ISVs (Abakion: Sales Orders + Transfer Orders between Home/Rental/Return locations + recurring invoicing batch).

### Odoo (v17/v18)
- "Can be Rented" flag; rental price lines by unit of time; cheapest applicable line always wins; late fees and padding time; lifecycle Quotation → Confirmed → Picked-up → On Rent → Returned; with Rental Transfers on, stock moves through an internal "Customer/Rental" location so ownership stays with the company (**location detail from secondary sources**); serials assigned on confirmed orders only.
- Deferred revenue: Start/End dates per invoice line, automatic or manual entry generation, Months / Full Months / Days methods, daily cron. Assets: asset models, "Automate Assets" on fixed-asset accounts from vendor bills, "Manage Items" splits a bill line into N assets. Stock → asset only via forum workaround.

### Rental point solutions
- **Texada**: fleet depreciation posts to GL; Cycle Billing applies one rate tier per contract with retroactive true-up; Monthly (calendar, prorated) or 28-Day cycles. **Point of Rental**: quote → reservation → contract → return → billing; live status buckets Available / On Rent / In Repair / Sold; telematics-triggered inspections. **Wynne RentalMan**: up to five depreciation methods per item, immediate GL posting. **InspHire**: asset tracker with live GPS, hire & sales order processing.
- Rate conventions: weekly ≈ 3–4.5× daily, monthly ≈ 10–14× daily; rate-factor pricing ~4–6% of machine cost per month; half-day/4-hour minimums for specialty gear. Fleet disposal mechanics **not documented** on vendor pages reached; advance-vs-arrears timing in cycle billing **unverified**.
- KPIs: time utilization, dollar utilization (ARA 2011 "Rental Market Metrics"; Rouse benchmarking), ROI per unit — a unit can be busy but unprofitable.

## Carbon Baseline (verified in code, 2026-09-21)

What exists:
- **Fixed assets** (`fixedAsset`, `fixedAssetClass`, `depreciationRun`, `fixedAssetDisposal`): acquisition from PO receipt / purchase invoice (Dr class asset account / Cr GR-IR or AP), manual registration (Dr asset / Cr retained earnings), manual depreciation runs, scrap disposal, and a two-step sale through a **Fixed Asset sales-order line** (`$fixedAssetId.sell.tsx` drafts a sales order priced at NBV; `post-shipment` parks NBV in the write-off clearing account; `post-sales-invoice` books proceeds against NBV to the class's **gain / loss on disposal accounts** — already the net presentation). `fixedAsset` has **no `itemId`, `trackedEntityId`, quantity or storage unit** — only free-text `serialNumber` and `locationId`.
- **Job completion** (`complete_job_to_inventory`, newest in `20260916131445`): Dr Finished Goods / Cr WIP at actual job cost, `itemLedger` Assembly Output rows (one per serial), `costLedger` Output layer, `itemCost` update for Average/FIFO. Serialized units are `trackedEntity` rows whose location is answered from `itemLedger`, not from the entity.
- **Sales lifecycle**: `salesOrderLineType` `Comment | Part | Material | Tool | Service | Consumable | Fixture | Fixed Asset` (no `ADD VALUE` history — additive target); Service lines are filtered out of shipments and exempted from status rollups; COGS posts at shipment for inventory items and at job completion for Non-Inventory services; revenue posts in full at invoice posting to `accountDefault.salesAccount` (4010) with shipping to 4050 and tax to 2210.
- **Accounting**: natural-balance-signed `journalLine.amount`; 26 `journalEntrySourceType` values (no revenue-recognition, lease or rental value); ERP-side `getOrCreateAccountingPeriod(source: "accounting")` may post into Locked periods, edge functions cannot; close checklist registers auto-checks by inserting a `periodCloseTaskDefinition` row **and** adding an evaluator to `computePeriodReadiness` (a key with no evaluator fails closed); `POSTING_POLICY` in `@carbon/ee` is total over source types, so a new source type must get a sync policy or the package fails typecheck.
- **Account defaults**: 2160 Deferred Revenue is seeded but **unmapped and unwritten**; 2110 Customer Prepayments is mapped as `prepaymentAccount` but **no posting path writes it**; gain/loss on disposal are 4140 / 6320.
- **Scheduled jobs**: ten daily/hourly Inngest crons; **no monthly cron**; `scheduled/mrp.ts` is the per-company fan-out precedent (one `step.run` per company).

What does not exist (all confirmed by grep):
- Any path from inventory (or a job) to a fixed asset — capitalizing a built unit today leaves it in Finished Goods and adds it against equity a second time.
- Any rental, lease, consignment, customer-owned or "at customer" concept on `location`, `storageUnit`, `trackedEntity` (statuses `Available | Reserved | On Hold | Consumed | Rejected | Scrapped`), `salesOrderLine` or `item`; the `Customer` tracked-entity attribute is declared but never written.
- Recurring or scheduled invoicing, subscription/billing schedules, time-based units of measure (seeded UoM are physical only), per-duration pricing, sales-order deposits (`payment` has no document FK; `invoiceSettlement` can only target an invoice or memo), auto-reversing / recurring / prepaid journal machinery, revenue schedules or a deferred-revenue writer.
- Implementation of the three governing specs: `.ai/specs/2026-07-04-revenue-recognition.md` (#1048), `.ai/specs/2026-07-04-lease-accounting.md` (#1056, whose lessor operating slice is exactly this use case) and the make/self-constructed slice of `.ai/specs/archived/2026-07-04-fixed-assets-completeness.md` (#1041, being re-specced by a community contributor).

## Recommended Approach for Carbon

1. **Treat rentals as operating leases (lessor), not ASC 606 arrangements** — separate rental-income revenue account, straight-line recognition, fleet asset stays in PP&E and depreciates (standards; Tesla; United Rentals). Compute the sales-type classification test on the agreement and surface it as a warning; leave sales-type / direct-financing accounting and RPO conversion mechanics to #1056 or a later phase.
2. **Add the missing bridge first: "Capitalize from inventory"** — a posting that consumes a serialized unit's inventory layer (`itemLedger` −1, `costLedger` consumption) and creates/activates a `fixedAsset` in a fleet class at the unit's carrying cost (Dr class asset account / Cr Finished Goods), linking `fixedAsset.itemId` + `trackedEntityId` (D365 F&SC Inventory-to-Fixed-Asset journal; NetSuite asset proposal; SAP 241). Add the mirror **"Return to inventory at NBV"** (Tesla) so a returned unit is sold gross through the normal sales flow, while the existing Fixed Asset sales-order line remains the net disposal path. Design the primitive so the CIP/make work on #1041 can reuse the same item↔asset link.
3. **Introduce a time-based Rental Agreement document rather than bending the sales order** (SAP MV contract; Odoo/BC ISV precedent shows the sales order needs a subscription engine beside it anyway): customer, fleet units (one serialized asset per line), start / end or open-ended, rate per period, billing cycle (calendar-month default, prorated; advance or arrears), refundable deposit, variable charges, purchase option; lifecycle Draft → Reserved → On Rent → Off Rent/Returned → Closed / Cancelled; a monthly Inngest job proposes Draft invoices per due cycle (propose-only, matching the program's close-automation posture) using a new **`Rental`** invoice line type that posts to the rental-income account.
4. **Make rentals the first consumer of the revenue-recognition core** — activate `accountDefault.deferredRevenueAccount` → 2160, a per-line recognition schedule keyed by service period, a monthly recognition run posting an accounting-source journal, and the "Recognize revenue for the period" close task, exactly as #1048 designs its Straight Line method; advance-billed rental invoices defer and release monthly, same-period level billing recognizes at posting. Arrangements, SSP allocation and cost-to-cost POC layer onto the same schedule/run substrate in later phases.
5. **Custody and traceability**: on capitalization the serial is consumed *into the fixed asset* (tracked-entity status `Consumed` plus a Fixed Asset attribute, reactivated on return-to-inventory like the RMA flow) rather than parked in a rental inventory location — keeps the inventory tie-out exact. Derive the operator-facing fleet status (Available / On Rent / In Maintenance / Sold) from agreements and asset status instead of storing it; track the customer location on the active agreement.
6. **Deposits** ride the payment-deposit branch #1048 already designs (Cr Customer Prepayments 2110), extended to rental agreements, applied to the final invoice or refunded on return.
7. **Reports** in v1: fleet register with on-rent status, rental revenue by unit, deferred-revenue waterfall (shared with #1048), and time + dollar utilization — the two KPIs every rental operator benchmarks.
