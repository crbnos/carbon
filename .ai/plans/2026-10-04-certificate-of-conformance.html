6. Write the issue/render server module and its unit test

Depends on: Tasks 4, 5

The server-only module loads the data, renders the PDF and issues the CoC. Do not export it from quality/index.ts.

issueCertificateOfConformance does these steps in order:

  1. Refuse unless the shipment is Posted and its source is a Sales Order.
  2. Refuse if the shipment already has an Issued CoC.
  3. Take the next number from the certificateOfConformance sequence.
  4. Read the signer name from user.fullName.
  5. Take signedAt from now(timeZone) in the company time zone.
  6. Render the PDF in issued mode.
  7. Compute the SHA-256 contentHash of the PDF bytes.
  8. Upload the PDF and add a document row for the shipment.
  9. Insert one certificateOfConformance row. No later update follows.

The data load uses one Promise.all and one .in() query for customer part numbers. It never queries in a loop.
