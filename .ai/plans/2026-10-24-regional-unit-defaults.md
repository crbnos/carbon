# Regional unit-of-measure defaults

Branch `feat/regional-unit-defaults`. Goal: a company outside the US starts with the units it measures in. Every company holds one master list of standard units, and the active flag decides which units the pickers offer.

## Findings

- `seedCompany` gives every new company the same 17 units (`unitOfMeasures`, `packages/database/src/seed-data.ts`): count and packaging units, Gallon, Ounce, Pound, Ton, Yard, Inch, Foot, Liter and Meter.
- A metric company gets no Kilogram, Gram, Tonne, Millimeter, Centimeter, Square Meter, Cubic Meter or Milliliter. It gets seven imperial units it does not use. Metric companies create KG, MM and M2 by hand before their first item.
- `insert_company_related_records` (`20250725212215_metric-default.sql`) set `companySettings.useMetric` from the company's country. The event-system port (`20260410031811_remaining-interceptors.sql`, latest body in `20261004194527_event-attachments.sql`) inserts `companySettings` with only its id.
- So every company created since 2026-04-10 starts imperial. Material dimensions, label sizes and calibration temperatures show imperial units.
- The old rule was `countryCode = 'US' OR baseCurrencyCode = 'USD'`. Onboarding defaults the base currency to USD, so a non-US company that keeps that default was imperial.
- Units have a CSV import (`unitOfMeasure`), but no list of standard units to import from.
- The demo datasets reference LB, INCH, FOOT, GL and YD. A seed without them would make a template fail on its foreign keys.

## Decisions

- D1. One catalog of standard units (`unitOfMeasureCatalog`), one name and code per unit (27 units). Metric codes are SI symbols with their case: `g`, `kg`, `t`, `mm`, `cm`, `m`, `m2`, `m3`, `mL`, `L`. Count and imperial codes stay as they are (`EA`, `LB`, `INCH`). Square Foot (`FT2`) and Cubic Foot (`FT3`) are new, to match Square Meter and Cubic Meter. Names keep the existing spelling (Meter, Liter). The metric ton takes the name Tonne, so nobody confuses it with the US Ton.
- D2. Every company holds the whole catalog. The units of the company's measurement system are active; the units of the other system are inactive. An imperial company also keeps Meter and Liter active, because the old list had them. So an imperial company starts with the 17 old units and Square Foot and Cubic Foot active.
- D3. `seedCompany` inserts the whole catalog and sets `active` from `companySettings.useMetric`, which the company-insert interceptor writes. One rule decides material dimensions and units.
- D4. The interceptor sets `useMetric` again, country first: imperial for the US and its territories (US, PR, GU, VI, AS, MP, UM), metric for every other country. A company with no country falls back to its base currency (USD imperial, else metric). The migration leaves existing companies alone: their setting may be a choice.
- D5. Existing companies: migration `20261024044646_units-of-measure-master-list` inserts each catalog unit that the company lacks. It skips a unit when the company has one with the same code or name, ignoring case and spaces. So a company keeps Meter as `MT` and Liter as `LT`. The migration does not change the units that the company has. A company is metric when `useMetric` is on, or when its country (else its base currency) is outside the US. The second test covers the companies that the April regression left on imperial.
- D6. Demo datasets: `ensureUnitsOfMeasure` (beside `ensureSequences`) makes each catalog unit that the dataset uses active. It also adds back a unit that the company deleted. The validator checks dataset unit codes against the catalog.
- D8. The active flag hides a unit from the unit picker only. The picker keeps the unit that the record uses. Tables and documents still read the names of inactive units. The Units of Measure list shows an Active column. Turn On and Turn Off act on the selected rows through `items.updateUnitsOfMeasureActive` (`update: parts`, published to MCP and the API). Each (`EA`) stays active.
- D9. Codes keep their case. The `unit_of_measure_enforce_unique_code` interceptor refuses a code that another unit of the company has in another case. It checks only when the code changes, so a pair that already exists can still be edited.
- D7. Out of scope: unit conversions and a unit dimension model (the parked conversion design), translated unit names, a base-currency default from the country in onboarding, the billing address rows the same April port stopped creating (reported separately).

## Deviations from the decisions

- The catalog lives in its own module, not in `seed-data.ts`. The module has no imports, and `seed-data.ts` carries the whole chart of accounts.
- Revision 2 (2026-10-24, after review) replaced the first design. The first design seeded a metric list or an imperial list, used uppercase codes (`KG`, `MT`) and added missing units through an Add Standard Units drawer. The review asked for SI codes, lowercase codes, a backfill for existing companies, and one master list that the active flag controls.
