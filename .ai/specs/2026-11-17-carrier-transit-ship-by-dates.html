Carrier transit time and ship-by dates

Carbon has no transit time, so an order's arrive-by date equals its latest ship-by date, and MES shows no ship-by date. This spec adds transit days to each shipping method, date math between the two dates, a stored job.shipByDate, and delivery dates on sales shipments. The order gets a correct arrive-by date, operators see the days left to ship, and users can check the transit days against real deliveries.

The problem

A customer asks for goods in 10 days. Production needs 3 days, and the carrier needs 5 days. So the goods must ship by day 5.

No shipping method holds a transit time today. So the quote to order conversion sets the arrive-by date to the latest line ship-by date. Operators on MES see only the "Due" text from job.dueDate, which does not follow the line after Carbon creates the job. When a sales shipment posts, Carbon records only shipment.postingDate, so nobody can check the transit days.

The glossary already says that the arrive-by date and the transit days drive a ship date back-calculation. No code does this calculation yet.

Example

Jane Doe converts a quote for PART-001 on Tuesday 2026-11-17. The quoted lead time is 3 days. The shipping method "Ground" has 5 transit days. The line ship-by date is Friday 2026-11-20. Today Carbon sets the arrive-by date to Friday 2026-11-20 too. With 5 transit days, the goods arrive on Friday 2026-11-27, so the arrive-by date is 5 business days early.

How it works

1. The business-day calendar

  1. A business day is Monday to Friday, minus each holiday row of the company.
  2. addBusinessDays(date, days, holidays) steps forward one business day at a time, days times.
  3. subtractBusinessDays(date, days, holidays) steps back one business day at a time, days times.
  4. If days is 0, both functions return the date unchanged.
  5. businessDaysBetween(start, end, holidays) counts the business days after start, up to and including end.

The 3 pure functions go into packages/utils/src/date.ts. They use @internationalized/date and never JavaScript Date. Plant shifts do not change the calendar, because a plant that works on Saturday does not make the carrier deliver on Saturday.

Try the date math

Enter a date and the transit days. The calculator uses the date as the posting date for the estimate, and as the arrive-by date for the ship-by date. It skips Saturday and Sunday. It has no holidays, like the spec's examples. Carbon also skips the company's holiday rows.

FormulaResult
The entered date is aFriday
Estimated delivery date = posting date + transit days (addBusinessDays)Friday 2026-11-27
Last business day on or before the arrive-by dateFriday 2026-11-20
Ship-by date = that business day − transit days (latestShipByDate)Friday 2026-11-13

Each step skips Saturday and Sunday.

2. The transit days lookup

One building block, resolveTransitDays(db, lane) in sales.service.ts, finds the transit days. The lane holds the company, the shipping method, the ship-to address, the plant and the ship date.

  1. If the shipping method is null, the function returns null.
  2. If a customerLocationTransitTime row matches the ship-to address and the shipping method, the function returns its transitDays. The source is "Ship-to override".
  3. If shippingMethod.transitDays is not null, the function returns it. The source is "Shipping method".
  4. Otherwise the function returns null.

Null means "unknown". If the transit days are unknown, Carbon calculates no date and shows no warning.

The ship-to address, the holidays and the unused lane fields

The ship-to address of an order comes from resolveSalesOrderShipTo in packages/ee/src/rules/sales/server.ts. For a drop shipment, it is the drop-ship location.

The holidays come from getHolidayDateSet in sales.service.ts. It is the Kysely sibling of getHolidayDates in production.service.ts. Quote to order and shipment posting read the holidays inside a Kysely transaction.

In v1 the function ignores the plant and the ship date. Each caller passes them now, because a carrier API needs the origin ZIP code and the ship date.

3. Date math on the sales order

EventWhat Carbon does
Quote to orderArrive-by date = the latest line ship-by date + transit days. If the transit days are unknown, the arrive-by date is the latest line ship-by date, as today.
A user adds a line with no promisedDateIf the order has an arrive-by date and known transit days, Carbon sets promisedDate = arrive-by date − transit days (latestShipByDate).
A user changes the arrive-by date, the shipping method or the ship-to addressCarbon calculates a new ship-by date for each line. If 1 or more lines differ, Carbon asks "Recalculate the ship date on N lines?". Carbon changes no line without a yes.
A line's ship-by date + transit days falls after the arrive-by dateThe line shows a "Late to arrive" warning with the estimated arrival date.

Carriers deliver on business days. So latestShipByDate in sales.utils.ts counts the transit days back from the last business day on or before the arrive-by date. An order due on a Saturday ships in time for Friday. With 0 transit days, the line ships on the arrive-by date.

Example

The arrive-by date is Saturday 2026-11-21, and the transit days are 1. The last business day on or before it is Friday 2026-11-20. So the ship-by date is Thursday 2026-11-19.

The recalculation changes only a line whose salesOrderLineType is in DELIVERY_CHECKED_LINE_TYPES and whose sentComplete is false.

The prompt uses one resource route, x+/sales-order+/$orderId.ship-by-dates.tsx:

  1. The client saves the header field, as today.
  2. When the save succeeds, the client loads the route.
  3. The loader returns the lines whose new ship-by date differs.
  4. If 1 or more lines differ, the client opens the confirm modal.
  5. "Yes" posts to the route action.
  6. The action calls recalculateSalesOrderShipByDates.
Quote to order: which lane it uses

The lane uses quoteShipment.shippingMethodId and the new order's customerLocationId, because quoteShipment has no ship-to address. receiptRequestedDate keeps its current fallback.

How "Late to arrive" joins the delivery date check

The delivery date check asks "can the line be ready by its ship-by date?". The "Late to arrive" warning asks "does the ship-by date leave enough transit time?". The two checks stay separate, and each one keeps its own icon. 3 rules join them:

  1. createSalesOrderLine sets the default promisedDate before it calls deliveryDateForSave. So the check runs on the default date.
  2. recalculateSalesOrderShipByDates runs the check on each line it changes, with capacity: "defer".
  3. The "Late to arrive" icon sits next to DeliveryDateFlag in SalesOrderExplorer.tsx.

4. job.shipByDate

  1. When Carbon inserts a job, the trigger job_set_ship_by_date copies the line's promisedDate into shipByDate.
  2. The same trigger runs on an update of salesOrderLineId or shipByDate. So it replaces a direct write of shipByDate with the line's date.
  3. If salesOrderLineId is null, the same trigger sets shipByDate to null.
  4. When a line's promisedDate changes, the trigger sales_order_line_sync_job_ship_by_date updates shipByDate on each job of that line and company, where the value differs.
  5. If a user deletes the line, the foreign key job_salesOrderLineId_fkey sets salesOrderLineId to null.
  6. Then job_set_ship_by_date clears shipByDate.

No application code writes job.shipByDate. job.dueDate does not change. The scheduler, the priority order and the overdue flags keep using dueDate.

Why plain triggers, not event-system interceptors

The 2 triggers are plain triggers in the migration. An interceptor in packages/database/src/event-system/handlers cannot change NEW, and the job trigger must change it. Both triggers run as their owner. So a sales user who moves a line also moves the job's copy.

6. Estimated and actual delivery

  1. When postShipment posts a shipment whose sourceDocument is 'Sales Order', it calls resolveTransitDays.
  2. If the transit days are known, postShipment sets estimatedDeliveryDate = addBusinessDays(postingDate, transitDays, holidays).
  3. A Posted sales shipment shows a Mark Delivered button.
  4. The button opens a modal with one date field, Delivered on. The default is the company's today.
  5. The service function refuses a date in the future and a date before postingDate.
  6. The service function saves deliveredDate and sets deliveredSource to 'Manual'.
  7. Later, a user can change the date or clear it. A clear sets both columns to null.

If a user voids the shipment, estimatedDeliveryDate and deliveredDate stay on the voided row. The statistics skip that row.

The shipment header shows "Arrives {date} (estimated)" before delivery and "Delivered {date}" after it. The shipments list gets 2 columns, Estimated Delivery and Delivered. getShipments selects * from shipment, so its query does not change.

7. Measured transit statistics

The shipping method drawer shows one line for the last 90 days. The spec gives this example:

Drawer line

Last 90 days: 42 shipments, average 3.4 business days. 88% by the estimate, 93% by the promised date. Use 4 days

NumberHow Carbon gets it
ShipmentsPosted, not voided shipments from a sales order, with this shippingMethodId, a deliveredDate, and a postingDate in the last 90 days
AverageThe mean of businessDaysBetween(postingDate, deliveredDate), to one decimal
By the estimateThe share of rows with deliveredDate ≤ estimatedDeliveryDate. It measures the carrier and the transit days. It skips rows with no estimate.
By the promised dateThe share of rows with deliveredDate ≤ the order's receiptPromisedDate. This is the on-time delivery (OTD) to the customer. It skips orders with no arrive-by date.

An early delivery counts as on time in both rates. Use N days rounds the average up and saves it as shippingMethod.transitDays. Carbon never changes transitDays without this click. If fewer than 5 shipments match, the drawer shows "Not enough deliveries yet" and no button. Transit overrides show no statistics in v1.

Decisions

Dates and their meaning

What does salesOrderLine.promisedDate mean? The ship-by date.human

Why: the user decided it. It matches the glossary: "When this specific line is promised to ship".

Which column is the arrive-by date? salesOrderShipment.receiptPromisedDate.human

Why: the column exists, and the glossary gives it this meaning (Q1). Quote to order sets it to the latest line ship-by date + transit days.

Who sets the line ship-by date? A default on new lines, a confirm prompt on header changes, and a "Late to arrive" warning.human

Why: Carbon never overwrites a date that a user typed without a yes (Q2).

Does job.dueDate become the ship-by date? No. A separate job.shipByDate holds it.human

Why: the user decided it. Planners keep editing dueDate. A later change can switch the scheduler to shipByDate.

Rejected: job.dueDate as the ship-by date.

How does Carbon keep job.shipByDate? A stored, read-only column. 2 triggers are its only writers.human

Why: the MES RPCs can sort and filter on a stored column with no extra join (Q3).

Transit days

Where do transit days live? shippingMethod.transitDays, plus customerLocationTransitTime per ship-to address and shipping method.human

Why: transit depends on the destination. SAP routes and Epicor Ship To do the same (Q4).

How many shipping methods per order? One, on salesOrderShipment.human

Why: the user decided it. It matches the current schema.

Rejected: a shipping method per line.

Business days or calendar days for transit? Business days: Monday to Friday, minus holiday rows.human

Why: the user decided it. Carriers quote ground service in business days. Business Central, SAP and Epicor count working days.

Rejected: calendar days.

Which calendar counts transit days? One company calendar.

Why: it is the smallest useful model. Business Central's carrier and customer calendars can follow if users ask for them.

Rejected: one calendar per carrier or per customer.

What do unknown transit days hold? Null, not 0.

Why: 0 means same-day (will-call). Null means "not set", so Carbon calculates nothing.

Rejected: 0 — it means same-day.

Where do the holidays come from? getHolidayDateSet (Kysely) in sales.service.ts.

Why: getHolidayDates in production.service.ts takes a Supabase client. The callers run inside a Kysely transaction.

Handling time per location? Out of scope.

Why: the coworker's example needs only make time + transit.

The ship countdown

Which screens show the ship countdown? MES operation cards, MES jobs list and MES assigned view.human

Why: the user chose these 3 screens (Q5). Picking lists and ERP screens are out of scope for v1.

Does the countdown also go on the assigned list view and picking lists? The assigned list view yes. Picking lists no.assumed

Why: the assigned list view shows the same rows as the assigned board (Q5). Q5 excludes picking lists.

What unit does the countdown use? Calendar days to the plant's today.

Why: operators read "3 days" as calendar days. It matches the existing "Due" text.

Where does the assigned view get the plant's today? The loader returns it.

Why: the countdown must change at the plant's midnight, not at the device's midnight.

Delivery and statistics

How does Carbon learn the actual delivery? An estimate at posting, a manual Mark Delivered date, and statistics per shipping method. No auto-tuning.human

Why: it builds the history that the carrier integration extends (Q6). The user also chose statistics per shipping method only, not per override (Q6b).

Rejected: statistics per transit override.

Which source does the delivered date record? deliveredSource: 'Manual' or 'Carrier'.assumed

Why: SAP fills the proof of delivery date from VLPOD or an IDoc. A carrier date must not overwrite a date that a user typed.

Which on-time rates show on the shipping method? 2 rates: by the estimate and by the promised date. Early counts as on time.assumed

Why: on-time shipping and OTD measure different things. A low OTD with a high estimate rate points at the transit days.

Do the shipment header and the shipments list show the delivery dates? Yes.assumed

Why: without them, a user cannot see the estimate that the statistics use.

How does the delivery date check relate to "Late to arrive"? They stay separate. createSalesOrderLine sets the default ship-by date before the check runs.

Why: the check answers "ready by the ship-by date". The new warning answers "the transit fits".

How does a carrier integration plug in? resolveTransitDays takes the whole lane. recordShipmentDelivery is the only writer of the delivered date.human

Why: the user asked for one PR with no carrier integration, but with an easy path to add one. The lane holds every input a carrier API needs.

Conventions

Multi-tenancy? customerLocationTransitTime has companyId, PK ("id", "companyId") and id('ctt').

Why: Heuristic 1 of the spec.

Service shape? Building blocks take db. New operations are defineServiceFn operations in sales.server.ts and inventory.server.ts.

Why: Heuristic 2 and .claude/rules/module-files.md.

RLS? customerLocationTransitTime: company("sales", { read: "sales_view" }) in the authz manifest.

Why: Heuristic 3. It copies the rule of customerItemRentalRate.

Permissions? Transit days on the shipping method and Mark Delivered: update: inventory. Transit overrides: update: sales, plus create: sales for a new row, and delete: sales.

Why: Heuristic 4. The manifest rule shippingMethod: company("inventory"), the customer Shipping tab (update: sales) and shipment posting use these scopes today.

Forms? ValidatedForm + validator(zodSchema) + a route action.

Why: Heuristic 5.

Module layout? The existing sales and inventory module files. No new module.

Why: Heuristic 6.

Backward compatibility? Every new column is nullable. The API gains fields and loses none. Existing orders keep their dates.

Why: Heuristic 7.

Terms

Ship-by date
The date a line must leave the plant, stored in salesOrderLine.promisedDate. The glossary calls it the Promised Date of the line.
Arrive-by date
The date the order must reach the customer, stored in salesOrderShipment.receiptPromisedDate. The glossary calls it the Receipt Promised Date.
Shipping method
The carrier and the service that a shipment goes out on.
Transit days
The business days from the ship-by date to the arrive-by date, from shippingMethod.transitDays or a transit override.
Transit override
The transit days for one ship-to address and shipping method pair, stored in customerLocationTransitTime.transitDays.
Business day
A day from Monday to Friday that is not a holiday row of the company.
Lane
The input of resolveTransitDays: the company, the shipping method, the ship-to address, the plant and the ship date.
Estimated delivery date
shipment.postingDate + transit days, stored in shipment.estimatedDeliveryDate.
Delivered date
The date the customer received the goods, stored in shipment.deliveredDate.
Ship countdown
The calendar days from the plant's today to job.shipByDate, shown as a label on 3 MES screens.
Plant's today
Today's date in the time zone of the location.
OTD (on-time delivery)
The share of deliveries that reach the customer on or before the arrive-by date.
Seam
One of the 2 functions that a later carrier integration plugs into: resolveTransitDays and recordShipmentDelivery.
