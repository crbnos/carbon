Certificate of Conformance

Aerospace, defense and medical customers need a signed Certificate of Conformance (CoC) with each shipment, and Carbon cannot make one. An authorized quality user issues a CoC from a posted Sales Order shipment, and Carbon freezes the signed PDF with a hash. The customer gets a correct certificate, and an auditor sees who released the parts and when.

The picture

All 10 messages, in order
  1. The quality user posts the shipment with "Issue CoC" checked and a title.
  2. The post route invokes post-shipment.
  3. post-shipment returns Posted.
  4. The route calls issueCertificateOfConformance with the shipment id, the signer and the title.
  5. The issue function asks the renderer for a PDF with the signature block.
  6. The renderer returns the PDF bytes.
  7. The issue function uploads the PDF and calls upsertDocument.
  8. The issue function inserts one Issued row with the hash.
  9. Later, the quality user voids the shipment.
  10. post-shipment sets the CoC to Voided in the same transaction.

The problem

Lockheed Martin Appendix QX §1.5, Boeing PO terms and RTX flowdowns require a CoC with each shipment. AS9100D and ISO 9001 clause 8.6 also require a record of who authorized release. Carbon has no CoC today.

  • Users type each CoC in Word and copy part numbers, revisions, lots and the customer PO by hand. A typo in a lot number breaks traceability.
  • Carbon keeps no record of who signed a CoC, or when. An auditor cannot trace a shipment to the person who released it.
  • No customer setting tells the shipping clerk that a customer needs a CoC. The clerk learns about it only when the customer rejects the delivery.

The precision demo dataset already shows the gap. Its final-inspection procedure says "attach the CMM report to the certificate of conformance", but Carbon cannot make that certificate.

Example

Customer ACME requires a CoC. Jane Doe posts a shipment with 2 lines: one line with lot L-100, one line with serials S1 and S2. Today she types the CoC in Word. With this spec, the post modal pre-checks "Issue CoC". After the post, the shipment shows CoC COC000001, signed by Jane Doe.

How it works

  1. The template customizer gets a new document type, certificateOfConformance, with 6 blocks.
  2. The 6 blocks are header, details, parties, line items, statement and signature.
  3. The quality module gets a certificateOfConformance table. One row is one issued CoC for one shipment.
  4. The customer shipping settings (customerShipping) get a "Certificate of Conformance required" switch.
  5. On a posted Sales Order shipment, a user with quality_create can Issue a CoC.
  6. Issue renders the PDF with the signature block, uploads it, and then inserts the row.
  7. ShipmentPostModal gets an "Issue Certificate of Conformance" section. The customer flag pre-checks it.
  8. The post route issues the CoC right after a successful post.
  9. Any user who can view the shipment can Preview the CoC at any status.
  10. The preview shows a "PREVIEW — NOT ISSUED" banner in the signature block, and no signature.
  11. A user with quality_delete can Void a CoC with a reason, and then issue a new one.
  12. When a user voids the shipment, post-shipment voids its CoC in the same transaction.
  13. A new list page, Quality → Certificates, shows each CoC with filters for customer, status and date.
The issue function, step by step

issueCertificateOfConformance lives in the new quality/certificate-of-conformance.server.ts. It does these 8 steps:

  1. Refuse unless the shipment is Posted, is a Sales Order shipment, and has no Issued CoC.
  2. Get the next certificateId with getNextSequence.
  3. Take signedAt from now(companyTimeZone).
  4. Render the PDF in issued mode.
  5. Compute the SHA-256 hash with node:crypto.
  6. Upload the PDF to storage.
  7. Call upsertDocument.
  8. Insert the row.

The function writes the row with one insert and never updates it afterwards. So a half-issued row cannot exist.

The default statement and its merge fields

The statement block is rich text. Its merge fields are {{company.name}}, {{customerPo}}, {{salesOrderId}}, {{shipmentId}}, {{certificateId}} and {{customer.name}}.

The default text, quoted exactly:

{{company.name}} certifies that the items listed above were manufactured, inspected and tested in accordance with the requirements of purchase order {{customerPo}}, the applicable drawings and specifications, and conform to those requirements. Supporting records are on file and available upon request.

Decisions

Where does the requirement live? A flag on customerShipping; item level waits.humanassumed

Why: E2 uses a customer flag, and customerShipping holds the other shipping defaults. The human approved the new column (Q1). Q6 deferred the item level autonomously.

Rejected: a new 1:1 customerQuality table — it adds a join and a form route for one flag. No flag in v1 — it removes the warning. An item-level column — item is a production-critical table.

When can a user issue a CoC? Only when the shipment is Posted.assumed

Why: Before posting, a user can still edit shipped quantity and tracking, so the CoC could go stale. Preview works at any status (Q2).

Block posting, or warn? Warn, never block.assumed

Why: A CoC can exist only after posting, so a block would deadlock. A user who cannot sign must still ship. ProShop and E2 also do not block (Q3).

Community or commercial? Community (AGPL).assumed

Why: Every quality feature today is community. A customer often needs a CoC to sell at all, so it is basic, not an upgrade (Q4).

What is a CoC keyed to? One shipment, one section per shipment line.

Why: SAP keys to the delivery item and ProShop to the packing slip. Lockheed asks for one CoC per shipment. Shipped quantity and lots are the truth.

Which source documents in v1? Sales Order shipments only.

Why: Only a customer shipment needs a CoC. Purchase returns and transfers do not go to a customer.

Stored record or live render? Freeze the signed PDF and store a SHA-256 hash.

Why: AS9100 8.6 requires retained evidence. A live render drifts when the template or a user name changes. itarCertification.docHash is the precedent.

Rejected: render on each download — the output drifts.

Which statuses? Issued → Voided, no Draft.

Why: The preview replaces a draft. A Draft row needs its own edit flow and adds no value.

How are CoCs numbered? Sequence certificateOfConformance, prefix COC, size 6.

Why: Customers and auditors quote the number. Inventory counts use the same sequence pattern.

How many live CoCs per shipment? One, by a partial unique index.

Why: Reissue means void the old CoC, then issue a new number. The voided row stays as the audit trail.

Who can sign? A user with quality_create; the name is read-only.

Why: The existing permission is the authorization, so RBAC does not change. A read-only name stops a user from signing as somebody else. The signer must tick an attestation checkbox.

Signature image? Not in v1.assumed

Why: Lockheed QX accepts "Electronically signed by" with the printed name. An image needs its own storage and upload flow (Q7).

Which statement text? One company statement in the template.assumed

Why: Paperless Parts uses one company statement. Research found no verified per-customer statement field in any product (Q5).

Rejected for v1: a per-customer override column on customerShipping.

What if issue fails at post? Post first, then issue; keep the post.

Why: The packing slip PDF uses the same pattern in $shipmentId.post.tsx. The route shows an error toast, and the user issues again from the shipment page.

How is issue atomic? One insert with every column, after render and upload.

Why: The Supabase client has no transaction. A failed insert leaves only an unused file and a sequence gap, and both are harmless.

What happens on a shipment void? The void sets the CoC to Voided in its Kysely transaction.

Why: Otherwise an Issued certificate could remain for parts that never shipped.

Where is the PDF stored? Under the opportunity path, like the packing slip.

Why: The CoC then appears in the sales order files next to the packing slip. The path is ${companyId}/opportunity/${opportunityId}/${certificateId}.pdf.

If the sales order has no opportunity, the path is ${companyId}/certificate-of-conformance/${certificateId}.pdf.

Which module owns it? Quality, with a new certificate-of-conformance.server.ts.

Why: A CoC is a quality release record. The issue code renders PDFs and uses the service-role storage client, so it stays out of the browser bundle.

Which permission for each action? Each action follows the module that owns its data.

Preview: view: "inventory". Issue: create: "quality". Void: delete: "quality". List: view: "quality". Customer flag: update: "sales".

RLS comes from certificateOfConformance: company("quality") in authz/manifest.ts.

Is the change backward compatible? Yes, additive only.

Why: The change adds a table, an enum, a column with a default and a template type. documentTemplateTypeSchema grows from 11 to 12 values.

Cert package with material certs and inspection reports? Out of scope for v1.assumed

Why: Carbon has no material-cert entity yet. A follow-up spec can add a certificateOfConformanceAttachment table (Q8).

Email the CoC to the customer? Not in v1.assumed

Why: The PDF is a document on the shipment, so a user can attach it by hand. Carbon does not email the packing slip either (Q9).

Retention period? No setting; Carbon never deletes a CoC.assumed

Why: A void keeps the row and the document. The ON DELETE RESTRICT foreign key to the shipment stops a cascade delete (Q10).

Seed CoCs in the demo datasets? Not in v1.assumed

Why: The datasets cannot write a frozen PDF to storage, so a seeded row would point at a missing file (Q11).

Data model

  • A partial unique index on (companyId, shipmentId) WHERE status = 'Issued' allows one live CoC per shipment.
  • A check constraint requires voidedAt and voidedBy exactly when the status is Voided.
  • The shipment and customer foreign keys use one column and ON DELETE RESTRICT, because both primary keys are ("id") alone.
  • The migration adds a certificateOfConformance sequence for each company. seed-data.ts gets the same sequence for new companies.
  • The authz/manifest.ts entry supplies the RLS. The migration has no policy SQL.

Done when

  • A user switches on "Certificate of Conformance required" for customer ACME. The value saves and reloads.
  • A user posts an ACME shipment with lot L-100 on one line and serials S1, S2 on the other. The modal pre-checks "Issue CoC", and the shipment then shows Issued COC000001.
  • The downloaded PDF shows the customer PO, both part numbers with revisions, and the shipped quantities. It also shows the lot and serials, the filled statement, and the signer, title and date.
  • The SHA-256 of the downloaded PDF equals contentHash on the row.
  • After a change to the company statement in Templates, the COC000001 PDF stays the same, byte for byte.
  • On a Draft shipment, Preview shows "PREVIEW — NOT ISSUED" and no signer. The card disables Issue.
  • A user without quality_create posts an ACME shipment. The post succeeds, and the modal and the card show the warning.
  • The issue action refuses a second Issue on a shipment with an Issued CoC. The partial unique index also refuses it.
  • A user voids COC000001 with reason "Wrong title" and issues COC000002. The card shows both, with the correct status.
  • A shipment void sets its Issued CoC to Voided with reason "Shipment voided", in the same transaction.
  • A CoC PDF request for a CoC of another company returns 404.
  • Quality → Certificates lists both CoCs and filters by status and customer.
  • The scoped typecheck, pnpm run lint, the quality and documents unit tests, pnpm db:check:datasets and pnpm db:check:backups pass.

Terms

Certificate of Conformance (CoC)
A signed statement that the parts in a shipment conform to the purchase order, the drawing and the specifications.
AS9100D clause 8.6
The quality-standard clause that requires a record of who authorized the release of products.
Posted shipment
A shipment that Carbon has posted. After posting, nobody can edit its lines.
Void
The action that cancels a posted shipment or an issued CoC. A voided CoC stays as the audit trail.
Batch tracking
Identical units share one tracked entity and one batch (lot) number.
Serial tracking
Each physical unit gets its own tracked entity and unique number.
Content hash
The SHA-256 hex value of the PDF bytes, stored in contentHash, that proves the file did not change.
Template customizer
The Templates screen where a company edits the blocks of each PDF document type.
Merge field
A placeholder such as {{customerPo}} that Carbon replaces with a value when it renders the statement.
quality_create
The existing permission that marks a user as authorized to sign a CoC.
