# Employee time clock and the Quality dashboard

> Clock in and out with breaks, flags, and overtime; see quality at a glance on a new dashboard; add non-taxable add-ons to quotes, orders, and invoices.

Employees can now clock in and out in Carbon, with breaks, flags, and overtime tracked on the time card. The Quality dashboard brings issues, inspections, and open actions onto one screen. Quotes, sales orders, and sales invoices accept non-taxable add-ons.

- Converting a quote to a sales order generates and attaches the sales order PDF.
- The sales order summary shows invoiced and paid amounts.
- The item master gained an exploded bill of materials view.
- BOM exports include cost estimates, including the cost of subassemblies.
- Demand forecast is shown on the inventory table, and planning tables sort by quantity to order.
- Tracked entities can be updated and deleted, and you're warned if you forget to issue a tracked part.
- Shipment lines accept a readable id.
- Inventory adjustments carry a comment.
- Drawers have a new floating look.
- Academy: new Issues and Gauges & Calibrations videos.

- Invoicing partial shipments no longer miscounts.
- Quote lines take the operation minimum cost from the method operation.
- Notes, assignee, and custom fields respect record locks.
- The payment dropdown is disabled by invoice status and permissions.
- Deactivating and reactivating a user no longer poisons the permissions cache.
- Inventory quantities no longer show duplicate rows across companies.
- The issues view's containment status column renders correctly.
