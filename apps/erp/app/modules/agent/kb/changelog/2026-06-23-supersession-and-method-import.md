# Picking lists, label printing, and item supersession

> Pick against a list, print labels, phase out a part in favor of its successor, import methods from CSV, and customize document templates.

Picking lists turn demand into a pick against real storage units. Label printing ships. Item supersession records that a part is being phased out and which successor replaces it, so planning and job creation pick up the change. Bills of materials and bills of process can be imported from CSV, and document templates now have a customizer.
