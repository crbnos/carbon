# Inbound inspections, pricing rules, and storage units

> Inspect what you receive, price by rule, and model your warehouse as storage units and types.

Inbound inspections put received goods through an inspection before they're available. Pricing rules set prices by rule instead of by hand. Inventory storage has been rebuilt around storage units and storage types, so a location's bins, shelves, and racks are modeled the way your warehouse works.

- Negative adjustments for batch- and serial-tracked parts.
- Duplicate a pricing list.
- Due dates can be edited inline on schedule job cards, and empty work centers can be hidden.
- Receipts can be created when line items have no price.
- Purchase order delivery dates are editable regardless of status.
- Purchase invoice status "Submitted" is now "Open".
- The BOM combobox reveals its dropdown on hover, sizes to its content, and shows tooltips for truncated names.
- The language dropdown lists every available locale.
- Audit configuration can name the fields recorded on create.
- MCP: search and call tools.

- Active make methods can no longer be overwritten.
- Backflushing serial parts includes their children.
- Serial and batch tracked entities are restored on job creation.
- Customer part ids are deduplicated on quotes and RFQs.
- Active quotes no longer auto-close.
- Jobs auto-complete correctly, and method type reads Make to Order rather than Make.
- Purchase invoice line prices update on the right trigger.
- Sign-in failures show consistent messages.
- Paperless Parts no longer overwrites data.
