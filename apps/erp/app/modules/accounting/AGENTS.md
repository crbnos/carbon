# Accounting Module

Chart of accounts, journal entries, general ledger, fiscal periods, currencies, payment terms, cost centers, dimensions, financial reporting (trial balance, balance sheet, income statement), fixed assets with depreciation, intercompany transactions, and external accounting sync (Xero, QuickBooks Online, Rillet).

The reports hub lives inside the accounting module at `/x/accounting/reports` (with the module sidebar; single "Reports" sidebar item), while the report pages themselves are full-screen in their own namespace `apps/erp/app/routes/x+/reports+/` (`/x/reports/{balance-sheet,income-statement,executive-pnl,trial-balance,inventory-valuation,revenue-waterfall,rental-utilization,lease-net-investment}` — bare-outlet layout, no module sidebar; `/x/reports` redirects to the hub). Balance sheet and income statement are multi-period: a Columns filter (Monthly default / Quarterly / Yearly) buckets the selected range fiscal-aware via `computeReportPeriodBuckets` (`@carbon/utils`) and the `accountTreeBalancePeriodSeries` RPC returns per-bucket `balanceAtDate`/`netChange` in one snapshot-bounded journal scan. Trial balance stays single-period (Beginning/Debit/Credit/Ending derived from netChange by account class).

**Inventory Valuation** (`inventory-valuation.tsx`, gated on `view: "accounting"`) is an inventory-subledger report that lives here (not in the inventory module): it renders the `InventoryValuationWorkbench` from `~/modules/inventory/ui` (on-hand value by location/item, as-of date, with a GL tie-out and a **Reconcile** action → `inventory-valuation.reconcile` posting via `createInventoryReconciliationJournal`, gated on `create: "accounting"`). Data/UI live in the inventory module; only the route + hub registration are here.

The **Executive P&L** (`executive-pnl.tsx`) shares the income statement's loader (same period series, consolidation, and translation) but renders a condensed summary instead of the account tree: `computeExecutivePnl` (`ui/Reports/executivePnl.ts`) rolls income-statement leaves up by `accountType` into Revenue / Cost of Sales / Operating Expenses / Other Income / Other Expense / Tax, then derives Gross Profit, Operating Income, and Net Income with margin-of-revenue percentages. A class-based catch-all keeps Net Income tied out to the income-statement root; `ExecutivePnlSummary` renders the fixed lines (no search, no drill-down).

## Key Domain Concepts

- **Chart of Accounts** — hierarchical account tree. Accounts have `class` (Asset/Liability/Equity/Revenue/Expense), `incomeBalance` (Balance Sheet/Income Statement), and `accountType`. Group accounts contain children; leaf accounts post transactions. Scoped by `companyGroupId`.
- **Journal Entries** — double-entry bookkeeping in the `journal` table. `amount` uses natural balances: positive Asset/Expense is debit; positive Liability/Equity/Revenue is credit. Convert by account class before summing debit-signed amounts. Lines carry dimensions and cost center allocations. Statuses (`journalEntryStatus`): a manual entry is Draft → Posted → Reversed; an automatic posting is born `Provisional` before the company's accounting cutover and `Posted` after it; the enable turns a Provisional journal into `Posted` (dated on or after the cutover) or `Superseded` (dated before it). `JournalEntryStatus` labels Superseded "Before cutover". `getJournalEntries` leaves Superseded journals out unless a status filter asks for them.
- **The accounting cutover** (`.ai/specs/implemented/2026-10-08-accounting-cutover.md`) — every posting path writes its journal and its sub-ledger rows (cost layers, finished-goods and asset cost, deferral schedules, contract entries, intercompany rows) for every company. `companySettings.accountingCutoverDate` decides only the journal status; `hasAccountingCutover(settings)` (`accounting.utils.ts`) is the ERP's one test for it. `companySettings.accountingEnabled` still exists, but no code reads it. `accountingActivatedAt` / `accountingActivatedBy` record the enable; the `check_accounting_config_locked` trigger then refuses any change to the three cutover columns, to `company.baseCurrencyCode` and to `fiscalYearSettings.startMonth`. A new company has no cutover: `seed-company` leaves it Provisional (`.ai/specs/2026-10-12-chart-of-accounts-import.md`), so its chart can be imported before anything is Posted, and it goes through the wizard. A company with no cutover sees the wizard instead of the gated pages: `AccountingBetaGate` overlays Reports, Intercompany, Journals, Periods and Depreciation Runs with a "Set up accounting" link (Fixed Assets and rental agreements stay open — a depreciation run or disposal is refused on the server instead), and `x+/accounting+/_index.tsx` redirects to the wizard. A Provisional journal has no accounting period, so it never locks the fiscal calendar (`getFiscalCalendarCommitted` counts Posted and Reversed only) and never blocks a period delete.
- **Journal status lists** (`@carbon/database/accounting-posting`) — every journal reader filters by one of three lists. `GL_JOURNAL_STATUSES` (Posted, Reversed): balances, trial balance, reports, period snapshots, tie-outs, provider sync. `DOCUMENT_JOURNAL_STATUSES` (Provisional, Posted, Reversed): a reader that follows one document's chain — void builders, GR/IR, WIP sums, intercompany lookups. `OPEN_ITEM_JOURNAL_STATUSES` (Provisional, Posted): payment control lookups and the memo, charge and reimbursement void checks. Superseded is in none. The `journal-status-filter` check (`@carbon/checks`) fails a `status <> 'Draft'` journal filter in TS and in migrations from `20261009005144`. The AR/AP readers (`get_ar_open_by_customer`, `get_ap_open_by_supplier`, `get_ar_tie_out`, `get_ap_tie_out`) keep `= 'Posted'` and also accept `sourceType 'Opening Balance'` (`20261009014620`).
- **Stand-in lines** — before the cutover, a posting that needs an empty nullable account default posts the line to `retainedEarningsAccount` and names the default it wanted in `journalLine.accountDefaultRole` (`resolveDefaultAccount`, `@carbon/database/journal-posting-status`). A Provisional journal counts nowhere, so the stand-in moves no balance. The enable re-points every stand-in line dated on or after the cutover to its default and clears the role. After the cutover an empty default fails the posting. See → Journals and the accounting cutover below.
- **Manual accounting work is refused before the cutover** with `ACCOUNTING_NOT_STARTED` ("Set up accounting before you post journals, runs or period closes."): `requireAccountingCutover` (`accounting.service.ts`) in `postJournalEntry`, `reverseJournalEntry`, `lockAccountingPeriod`, `closeAccountingPeriod` and `createDepreciationRun`; `requireGroupAccountingCutover` (every company of the group) in `runIntercompanyMatching` and `generateEliminations`; a `readAccountingCutoverDate` test (`@carbon/database/journal-posting-status`) at the top of `postDepreciationRun`, `postDisposal` and `postRevenueRecognitionRun` (`accounting.service.ts`); and `assertAccountingCutover` (`shared.service.ts`, the same read) in the `propose-revenue-recognition-run` and `recalculate-revenue-recognition-run` postings.
- **The enable wizard** — `x+/accounting+/activation.tsx` (layout, `view: accounting`, redirects to Periods once the company has a cutover; the cutover date rides the `?cutover=` search param (`ACTIVATION_CUTOVER_PARAM`), read by `getActivationCutover` in `accounting.service.ts`, which defaults to the first day of the current month) with six steps, each its own route: `activation.chart.tsx` (links to the chart of accounts import; Next keeps Carbon's chart), `activation.readiness.tsx` (the date picker — the first day of the current month or of one of the 3 before it, `CUTOVER_MAX_PERIODS_BACK` — and the six checks of `getActivationReadiness`: every `accountDefault` account column set to an active account, except a default with a fallback (`hasDefaultFallback`), which may stay empty, and `migrationClearingAccount` an Equity account that is not a group; fiscal year settings and base currency; a valid cutover date; no Draft or Pending receipt, shipment or invoice and no Draft payment or memo dated before the cutover; no open job created before the company's first Provisional or Superseded journal; no Posted `Opening Balance` journal. Each `ActivationCheck` carries `reasons`, codes (`ActivationCheckReason`: `no-fiscal-year-settings`, `no-base-currency`, `migration-clearing-wrong-kind` and the four `CutoverDateReason` codes) that `ReadinessChecklist` translates; `detail` says the same in English for the enable's refusal), `activation.inventory.tsx` (`getCutoverInventoryValuation`: on-hand at the cutover per item and its unit cost — the replayed layer value for FIFO / LIFO, standard cost for Standard, `itemCost.unitCost` for Average, which is the only one the user edits — with each item's value, the value per inventory account and the total, valued as the opening journal values them), `activation.fixed-assets.tsx` (`getCutoverFixedAssets`: the assets on the books the day before the cutover — acquired before it, and not disposed or disposed on or after it, except an asset whose disposal leaves the books with no journal (`getAssetsLeavingWithoutJournal`: a sale, return to inventory or lease commencement posted before the reset) — with accumulated depreciation as of that day, the asset's own less what Posted runs booked for months on or after the cutover (`getDepreciationAfterCutover`), since the enable writes those runs' journals again; intent `save-asset` writes the accumulated depreciation as of the day before the cutover through `updateCutoverAccumulatedDepreciation`, which stores it plus that later depreciation), `activation.trial-balance.tsx` (the prior system's trial balance as of the day before the cutover, kept as ONE Draft `Opening Balance` journal with no period by `saveOpeningTrialBalance`; one debit-signed balance per account in the editor (a credit is negative; the save still sends a debit and a credit); intents `save-tb` (the editor), `import-tb` (CSV `accountNumber, debit, credit`, saves nothing when a row has an error), `pull-tb` (only when the active accounting integration passes `isTrialBalanceProvider` — Xero, QuickBooks Online, Rillet: `pullTrialBalanceFromProvider` (`@carbon/ee/accounting`) reads the provider's trial balance as of the day before the cutover and maps it through the account mappings, else by account number; it saves what maps and returns the rest, unmapped or mapped to several Carbon accounts, for the editor to list), `ignore-invoice` (`setCutoverInvoiceIgnored`) and `apply-carbon` (`applyCarbonToOpeningTrialBalance`: every control account set to Carbon's opening total, every other row kept, and what that leaves out of balance — the Migration Clearing total — added to the `retainedEarningsAccount` row, so Migration Clearing totals zero; the button is enabled while the total is not zero). The CSV parser is the pure, tested `trial-balance-csv.ts` (`parseTrialBalanceCsv`, `mapTrialBalanceAccounts`): it returns error codes the editor translates, refuses a duplicate account number, and refuses an amount with a comma (`ambiguous-amount`) instead of stripping it, since `1,234` is a thousand in one convention and one and a fraction in another; `getMigrationClearing` shows, per control account, the trial balance, Carbon's opening total and the difference, and each account opens to the items behind its total. Before the cutover another system kept the books, so an invoice open in Carbon may be settled there: a sales or purchase invoice (`isIgnorableOpenItem`) can be ignored — unless Carbon itself settles it on or after the cutover (`settledFromCutoverIds`: a payment or memo application not in effect the day before, or a Paid status dated on or after it), whose journal credits the control account and needs the opening line — which stores it in `accountingCutoverIgnoredInvoice`, takes it out of `items` into `ignoredItems`, and has the enable mark it Paid the day before the cutover. No other item can be ignored: leaving it out of the opening journal would misstate what reads it later) and `activation.enable.tsx` (the summary and the typed company name; it invokes `activateAccounting` (`accounting.service.ts`, ported from the `activate-accounting` server function) and redirects to Periods). The summary lists, under "Documents posted before Carbon kept journals", each family with legacy documents and how many (`getLegacyDocumentCounts`): documents posted on or after the cutover that have no journal, which the enable journals with today's accounts and costs (see → Legacy documents below). The reads live once, in Kysely, in `@carbon/database/accounting-cutover-reads` (server-only; one module per step under `packages/database/src/accounting-cutover/`). The wizard routes import them from there directly and pass `getDatabaseClient()`; `activate-accounting` calls the same functions with its transaction. The pure planners (`planInventoryReset`, `recostOutbound`, `buildOpeningJournalLines`) are `@carbon/database/accounting-cutover`. Settings → Accounting (`x+/settings+/accounting.tsx`) shows "Accounting since {date}" or a "Set up accounting" button; there is no switch.
- **Opening lines** — the enable posts one `Opening Balance` journal, dated the day before the cutover, against `accountDefault.migrationClearingAccount` (seeded as 3400 "Migration Clearing" for new companies). A control-account line copies the keys its chain reader matches (`documentType`, `documentId`, `documentLineReference`, description), so a payment, a purchase invoice's GR/IR walk or a job close after the cutover finds it as if the original posting had written it. A PO line invoiced before the cutover for more than it received opens its GR/IR accrual (`purchase-invoice:<poLineId>`, `accrual` true, the open quantity at the invoices' accrual unit cost), which a receipt after the cutover clears at the invoice's cost. A document partly settled before the cutover gets two lines: its original amount, and the amount settled before the cutover under "<description> (settled before cutover)", which the readers ignore. Every other account comes from the trial balance only. Deferred revenue opens from the Deferral rows dated on or after the cutover of invoices posted before it that were still deferred the day before (Planned, or Posted by a run whose journal the reset deleted, which the enable writes again), on each row's debit account. Lease net investment opens per rental agreement from the lease rows of each `Sale` line commenced before the cutover (the last `rentalLeaseScheduleLine.closingNetInvestment` before the cutover, else `initialNetInvestment`), not from a commencement journal, which a lease commenced before the reset does not have. Migration Clearing must total zero within 0.01 before the enable. A void of a document dated before the cutover is in → Journals and the accounting cutover below.
- **Fiscal Year Settings** — configurable start month for fiscal and tax years. Accounting periods auto-created via `getOrCreateAccountingPeriod`.
- **Dimensions** — analytical tags on journal lines (Location, Department, Project, etc.). Entity-type dimensions resolve values from their source table; Custom dimensions use `dimensionValue`.
- **Cost Centers** — hierarchical organizational units for cost allocation via `parentCostCenterId`.
- **Fixed Assets** — capital assets with depreciation. Supports straight-line, declining balance, MACRS, and units-of-production methods. Depreciation runs generate journal entries: one `depreciationRunLine` per asset per month, each month's journal in that month's period, and **Reverse Run** (`reverseDepreciationRun`) undoes the latest posted run — journals, accumulated depreciation and the run's status (see the rule). The **fleet bridge** links an asset to an `item` / `trackedEntity` / `workCenter`: a serialized unit is capitalized from stock or built by a Make to Asset job (`complete_job_to_inventory`), returned to stock at net book value, or accumulated as **Construction in Progress** (`fixedAssetClass.isConstructionInProgress`, status `Under Construction`, cost rows in `fixedAssetCipCost`) until capitalized into an in-service class. Construction in progress is built but **hidden** until it returns with projects: `CONSTRUCTION_IN_PROGRESS_ENABLED = false` (`accounting.models.ts`) filters CIP classes out of `getFixedAssetClasses` / `getFixedAssetClassesList` (every class list and picker), hides Attach Job / Capitalize and the CIP cost card (`isCipClass` in `x+/fixed-asset+/$fixedAssetId.tsx`), the Under Construction status filters (`visibleFixedAssetStatuses` / `visibleFleetStatuses`) and Complete To → Asset Under Construction (`JobForm`). Engines and schema are untouched; flip the constant to bring it back. Every inventory ↔ asset move is one Posted `fixedAssetTransfer` document written by `postAssetTransfer` (`accounting.service.ts`, ported from the `post-asset-transfer` server function) (journal `sourceType 'Asset Transfer'`, off by default in the sync policy). Fleet status is derived by the `fleetAssets` view, never stored. See `.claude/rules/fixed-asset-lifecycle.md`.
- **Revenue recognition** — `revenueRecognitionSchedule` is the one "what to post, when" table: `type` Deferral / Accrual / Interest / Amortization, `status` Planned / Posted, each row carrying its own `debitAccountId` / `creditAccountId`, linked to a `salesInvoiceLineId`, a `rentalAgreementLineId`, a `customerContractLineId` (contract rows, see Contract revenue below) or a lessee `leaseId` + `leaseScheduleLineId` (see Lessee leases below). `postSalesInvoice` (`invoicing.service.ts`) writes Deferral rows for every company (every **Service** line with service dates; every Rental rent line). There is no revenue recognition flag. Service dates belong to Service lines only: every other item type is a physical good, earned when it ships, so the sales order / invoice line forms show the date fields only on a Service line, the line validators refuse them on any other type (the invoice validator also admits `Rental`, whose lines carry their billing period), `upsertSalesOrderLine` / `upsertSalesInvoiceLine` null them on any other type, and posting ignores dates left on a non-Service line. A **run** (`revenueRecognitionRun` / `revenueRecognitionRunLine`) has a `type` (`runType`): **Revenue Recognition** or **Lease** — see Run types below. It claims every Planned row of its type dated on or before its `periodEnd` and first runs its type's synthesizers (`runRowSynthesizers(type)`) in `proposeRevenueRecognitionRun` (`accounting.service.ts`, ported from the `propose-revenue-recognition-run` server function) (`apps/erp/app/modules/accounting/accounting.service.ts`, `{ periodEnd, type }`, permission `create: accounting`; the routes call it through `invokePosting`, the monthly job through `posting`). `postRevenueRecognitionRun` (`accounting.service.ts`) posts the run's journals (`sourceType 'Revenue Recognition'` or `'Lease Run'` by run type (`buildRecognitionJournals`, `@carbon/database` `run-journals.ts`), both `defaultEnabled: false` in the sync policy; one per month, below); deferral lines reference the invoice, rental Accrual/Interest lines `documentType 'Rental Agreement'` with Customer / Item / Location dimensions resolved from the agreement, lessee rows `documentType 'Lease'` + `documentLineReference` = the asset (`leaseLine`, from `RecognitionSources.leaseAssets`) with Supplier / the asset's Location dimensions. Inngest `revenue-recognition-proposal` (cron `0 12 1 * *`) only proposes Draft runs, one step per run type per company. A period may hold more than one run of a type (a later one picks up rows that fell due after the first posted) but one Draft per type at a time: the routes and the proposal refuse a second Draft, and the partial unique index `revenueRecognitionRun_one_draft_per_period` (`companyId, type, periodEnd` WHERE Draft) is the backstop. No run may end after the company's current month (`futureRunPeriodError`, `accounting.service.ts`; the proposal function repeats the check). Posting writes ONE journal per month the rows fall in, each in that month's period — the run's own period when the month is Closed (`resolveRunPostingPeriods` → `RunPostingPeriods`); `revenueRecognitionRun.journalId` names the journal of the run's own period, and the Documents panels read every journal through the schedule rows. **Reverse Run** (`reverseRevenueRecognitionRun`, route `$runId.reverse`) reverses those journals with their documents and dimensions (`reverseRunJournals`, each on its own date or today when Closed, `resolveReversalPeriods`), puts the rows back to Planned under the same run, clears what posting stamped (lease schedule lines, contract ledger entries, contract revenue months) and returns the run to Draft; `reverseJournalEntry` refuses a run's journal (`RUN_JOURNAL_SOURCES`, `accounting.models.ts`). A Draft is a snapshot: each run line copies its row's amount. **Recalculate** (`x+/revenue-recognition-run+/$runId.recalculate`, `recalculateRevenueRecognitionRun` (`accounting.service.ts`, ported from the `recalculate-revenue-recognition-run` server function), `update: accounting`) keeps the run's id and number, releases its rows BEFORE rerunning the synthesizers (a synthesizer that drops a held row would otherwise delete the run it emptied), then claims what is due by its `periodEnd` again; a run with nothing left due is deleted. The proposal's `periodEnd` must be a real month end (a 400 otherwise). `postRevenueRecognitionRun` refuses a run that is out of date — a held row's amount or status changed, or an unclaimed Planned row is due by its `periodEnd` — with `RunOutOfDateError`, whose message the post route shows. A write that deletes a held Planned row (an invoice void, or `synthesizeContractRevenue` dropping a contract row whose plan month was replaced) keeps Draft runs in step in its own transaction through `syncDraftRecognitionRuns` (`shared.service.ts`), deleting a run it empties. Close tasks "Recognize revenue for the period" (`autoCheckKey 'unposted-revenue-schedules'`) and "Post lease journals" (`'unposted-lease-schedules'`) ask the run of their type what it would do instead of copying its rules: `previewRevenueRecognitionRun` (`accounting.service.ts`, ported from the `preview-revenue-recognition-run` server function) (`{ periodEnd, type }`, `view: accounting`) runs the synthesizers and `selectDueScheduleRows` in a transaction that always rolls back and returns `{ count, amount }`. The check fails while that preview has rows, the preview failed, or a Draft run holds a Planned row due by the period end.
- **Run types** (`@carbon/database/run-type`; spec `.ai/specs/2026-10-11-lessee-leases.md` §6) — two runs on one engine, one client-safe descriptor: `runTypeFilter(type)` (Kysely), `runTypeOfScheduleRow(row)` (the same test on a row in hand), `RUN_SEQUENCE`, `RUN_LABEL` / `runLabelSentence` (messages); the ERP adds `RUN_PAGES` (`ui/RevenueRecognition/runPages.ts`: list path, New Run path, list title) for every "back to the run's list" and breadcrumb. `runTypeFilter(type)`: a **Lease** run claims every row with a `rentalAgreementLineId` or a `leaseId` (rental deferrals, rental accruals, sales-type lease interest, lessee interest and amortization) and synthesizes rental accruals; a **Revenue Recognition** run claims the rest (service-dated invoice lines, contracts) and synthesizes contract revenue. `accounting.service.ts` uses the same filter; post checks only its own type's unclaimed rows (the contract check only for revenue runs) and stamps lessee `leaseScheduleLine.journalId` / `postedAt`; reverse filters its Draft check on the type, clears lessee stamps, and releases rows of the OTHER type a legacy posted run held (deleting a run left empty). Readable ids come from `RUN_SEQUENCE` (`revenueRecognitionRun` / `leaseRun`, prefix `LR`). Lease runs: route `x+/accounting+/lease-runs.tsx` (+ `.new`); revenue runs `x+/accounting+/revenue-recognition-runs*`; both in the nav under Accounting → Closing. Both list routes share `loadPeriodRuns` / `createPeriodRun` (`period-runs.server.ts`) and `PeriodRunsPage`; a run of either type opens at `x+/revenue-recognition-run+/$runId` (post, reverse, repeat, recalculate, delete follow the run's type). `20261011163211_lessee-leases.sql` released rental rows from Draft revenue runs (posted history kept) and `20261011192957_lease-run-sync-policy.sql` copied each accounting integration's `Revenue Recognition` sync setting to `Lease Run`. `reverseJournalEntry` refuses a `Lease Run` journal like the other run sources (`RUN_JOURNAL_SOURCES`).
- **Rental accruals** — `synthesizeRentalAccruals` (the lease run's synthesizer) accrues earned-but-unposted rent of `Rental` (operating-lease) lines for the run's calendar month (Dr `contractAssetAccount` / Cr `rentalIncomeAccount`, both required or it throws "Set the Contract Assets and Rental Income account defaults before recognizing rental revenue"). It takes every non-adjustment `rentalBillingPeriod` of a `Rental` line (`On Rent` / `Returned`) that no POSTED invoice covers (`Pending`, or `Invoiced` onto a Draft/Pending invoice), clips it to the month and to `[deliveredAt, returnedAt]`, and writes one Accrual row per SLICE (`periodStart/End` = the slice, `scheduledDate` = the run's period end) with cumulative rounding from the period's first day. Any existing Accrual row overlapping a slice suppresses it (idempotent); a transaction advisory lock serializes concurrent proposals. Posting a Rental rent line credits Contract Assets for the unbilled Accrual rows of its period (Planned or Posted) and stamps `billedBySalesInvoiceLineId`. The close check counts un-accrued rent through the run preview (above), so it sees exactly what a run would accrue.
- **Contract revenue** (contracts Phase B, `.ai/plans/implemented/2026-09-22-revenue-recognition-rentals-and-contracts.md` Part IV D5) — `synthesizeContractRevenue` (the revenue recognition run's synthesizer, `runRowSynthesizers`) turns every Planned `customerContractRevenue` month starting on or before the period end, of a non-Draft contract, with no schedule row yet, into a Deferral row (Dr `deferredRevenueAccount` / Cr `salesAccount`) for the part the line's deferred pool covers and an Accrual row (Dr `contractAssetAccount` / Cr `salesAccount`) for the rest, at `get_exchange_rate(…, periodEnd)`; each row carries `customerContractLineId`, `customerContractRevenueId`, `contractAmount` and one `Recognition` entry in `customerContractLedgerEntry` (cascades with the row). A negative (catch-up) month yields negative rows. It first drops Planned contract rows whose plan month was replaced (`customerContractRevenueId` nulled). `postRevenueRecognitionRun` posts contract rows with `documentType 'Contract'` and Customer / Item / Project (line, else contract) dimensions, flips their plan months to `Recognized`, stamps the entries' `journalId`, and treats an unsynthesized due month or a held row with a nulled plan month as out of date. The close check counts unsynthesized due months through the run preview (above).
- **Sales-type leases** — a rental line classified `Sale` (a sales-type lease; `lessorClassification` values `Rental` / `Sale` / `Financing`) at activation (`postRentalAgreement`, `sales.service.ts`; see sales AGENTS → Rentals) posts its commencement and end-of-term residual return as journals of `sourceType 'Lease'` (`POSTING_POLICY` `Lease`, `defaultEnabled: false`; also in the hand-written `journalEntrySourceTypes` array with the `LuKeyRound` icon), lines `documentType 'Rental Agreement'` with Customer / Item / Location dimensions. Its effective-interest schedule is `rentalLeaseScheduleLine`; each line that `earnsInterest` (`lessor-lease` in `@carbon/utils`: not zero, not the one-unit drift an Advance lease closing on zero leaves on its last line) spawns one Planned `Interest` recognition row (Dr `netInvestmentInLeasesAccount` / Cr `leaseInterestIncomeAccount`) that the LEASE run posts under `'Lease Run'`, and `postRevenueRecognitionRun` then stamps the schedule line's `journalId` / `postedAt`; Reverse Run (`reverseRevenueRecognitionRun`) clears the stamps. Rent and Purchase Option invoice lines of a `Sale` line credit `netInvestmentInLeasesAccount`; a Purchase Option line also settles the rest of the line's NI against the schedule's closing balance (`purchaseOptionSettlement`, `invoicing.service.ts`: shortfall Dr `costOfGoodsSoldAccount` / Cr NI, excess Dr NI / Cr `leaseRevenueAccount`). A residual return moves the schedule's balance on the return date (`netInvestmentAt`) and keeps the Interest rows dated on or before it, so later runs still post them and NI closes to zero. A sales-type lease must run whole billing periods (`salesTypeRequirementError`, `lessor-lease` in `@carbon/utils`) so the billed rent equals the valued payment stream. Account defaults seeded 1160 Net Investment in Leases, 4070 Lease Revenue, 4150 Interest Income – Leases. Lease policy (the classification thresholds `companySettings.leaseMajorPartThresholdPercent` 75 / `leaseSubstantiallyAllThresholdPercent` 90, shared by lessor and lessee; the lessor `leaseDefaultDiscountRate` 6, label "Lessor Discount Rate (%)"; the lessee `leaseIncrementalBorrowingRate` 6, `leaseShortTermExpedient` true and `leaseReminderDays` 90) is edited on `x+/settings+/accounting.tsx` ("Lease Classification" card, intent `leasePolicy`, `update: settings`) through `updateAccountingSettings`; changes affect later activations only.
- **Net investment report** — `getLeaseNetInvestment(client, companyId, { asOf })`, report `x+/reports+/lease-net-investment.tsx` (`view: accounting`; hub card "Net Investment in Leases", category Close Reports; `path.to.leaseNetInvestment`). Per commenced `Sale` line live on `asOf` (Pending / On Rent, or Returned after `asOf`; Sold lines omitted; agreement `startDate` ≤ `asOf`): `initialNetInvestment`, posted principal (schedule lines with `periodDate` ≤ `asOf` whose Interest row has posted (`postedAt`) or that earn no interest, so have no row to post), current NI, next interest, undiscounted remaining payments by fiscal year (`fiscalYearSettings.startMonth`), and the last line's closing (option + residuals). Reads `rentalAgreementLine` and `rentalLeaseScheduleLine`, whose RLS is `sales_view` — an accountant without sales view sees an empty report. Principal counts once its Interest row is posted (a no-interest line: once its date passes), so it ties to 1160 only when the run and the rent invoices for the same months are both posted.
- **Lessee leases** (purchasing module, see purchasing AGENTS → Leases) — journals of `sourceType 'Lease'` (`POSTING_POLICY` `Lease`, also used by sales-type rentals) for commencement, opening balance, modification, termination, purchase and the cancel VOID, lines `documentType 'Lease'` (`journalLineDocumentType` value) + `documentLineReference` = the leased asset (`leaseLine`) with Supplier / the asset's Location dimensions; `getJournalEntryRelatedItems` links them, and `Lease Run` journals, back to their lease or run. Class totals per `fixedAssetClass`. One read per table, joined in TS.
- **Intercompany** — transactions between companies in a group. `runIntercompanyMatching` pairs them; `generateEliminations` creates reversing entries on the lowest-common-parent elimination entity, classified by `journal.eliminationKind`: `'IC Balance'` (reverse IC Receivable/Payable) and `'IC Revenue'` (reverse an intragroup sale's revenue + COGS and write the buyer's capitalized asset down to group cost — removes intragroup profit from consolidated income). The engine is **capture-driven, not GL reconstruction**: the postings (`post-sales-invoice`, `post-purchase-invoice`) record role-tagged references (`intercompanyEliminationLine`: `Control`/`Revenue`/`COGS`/`Capitalization`) when each side posts, and `generateEliminationEntries` reads them — so it writes down whatever account the BUYER capitalized (inventory OR a **fixed asset** — the latter is now in scope, was the negative-Finished-Goods bug). The writedown scales by the buyer's on-hand fraction (fixed assets: full until disposed); revenue/COGS reversal scale by the same fraction so the entry balances. `p_regenerate` reverses existing eliminations (reversing entries, never deletes) and re-derives — the workbench "Regenerate" button. Both halves of a trade record their matching amount in the trade's DOCUMENT currency rounded at internal `SCALE` — `calculateSalesIntercompanyAmount` (seller) and `round(intercompanyAmount)` (buyer). `generate_intercompany_matches` pairs them on exact NUMERIC equality with **no tolerance**, so rounding either half at settlement precision leaves every trade with sub-cent digits permanently `Unmatched` and eliminations never run. Investment/NCI and seller fixed-asset disposal (deferred gain) remain out of scope. See `.ai/specs/2026-08-17-intercompany-elimination-engine.md`.

Report loaders reject company selections outside the available company group (404) before balance reads.
- **Shipping revenue** — `accountDefault.salesShippingRevenueAccount` maps an active Revenue posting leaf distinct from Sales. - **Payments have THREE payee kinds, not two.** `payment.employeeId` is a reimbursement
  payout: always a Disbursement, never AR, never a refund, and it targets
  `invoiceSettlement.targetReimbursementId` only — no invoice target, no discount, no
  write-off, no prior credit. Its control account is
  `accountDefault.employeeReimbursementsPayableAccount`, falling back to `payablesAccount`
  at runtime. `journalEntrySourceType` gained `Charge` and `Reimbursement` (the former
  replacing `Card Transaction`); a Reimbursement journal's payable leg is what a payout
  clears. Before the cutover a target with no control line (a document posted before
  every company wrote journals) books the default control account instead. After the
  cutover a target with no control line, in its own journal or in the opening journal, is
  refused ("Target is missing its original control account") rather than debiting a
  liability that was never credited.
- **Payments and refunds** — the customer/supplier determines AR/AP independently of cash direction. Customer Disbursements and supplier Receipts refund posted balance-reducing memos through `invoiceSettlement.targetMemoId`, using current cash only (no invoice targets, discounts, writeoffs, or prior credits). Ordinary payments retain invoice targets, memo-source funding, and prior cash credits. Memo availability subtracts effective source and target principal; an effective posted refund blocks memo voiding. Aging and tie-out RPCs sign unallocated refunds opposite ordinary payments and use the applying payment's cash direction for FX consumption. Target memos consume exact `sourceAmount` principal and `appliedAmount` carrying base. Unknown memo principal retains the existing exclusion contract; it is not reconstructed or raised as a report error. Payment/invoice application histories page all rows, including embedded parent statuses and refund labels, before totaling.
- **Customer deposits** — a customer payment with `payment.rentalAgreementId` or `payment.salesOrderId` (CHECK `payment_deposit_document_check`: at most one; `paymentValidator` refines the same and refuses it on a supplier payment) is a deposit. `post-payment` books its unapplied cash on `accountDefault.prepaymentAccount` (2110 Customer Prepayments, class Liability) with description `CUSTOMER_DEPOSIT_DESCRIPTION` = "Customer Deposit" (`@carbon/database/accounting-posting`) instead of on-account AR credit; a Disbursement carrying the reference refunds from the same account. Applying it later goes through the existing prior-credit path: the source-control lookup matches either the on-account description or "Customer Deposit", and the release posts `CUSTOMER_DEPOSIT_APPLIED_DESCRIPTION` ("Customer Deposit (applied)") as a debit on the prepayment account. **A deposit funds only its own document**: `allocatePaymentFunding` (`@carbon/utils` payment-funding) gives each `FundingSource` an optional `scope` (`fundingScopeOf(payment)` → `{ type: "rentalAgreement" | "salesOrder", id, readableId? }`) and each `FundingRequest` the `rentalAgreementIds` / `salesOrderIds` its invoice bills (from `salesInvoiceLine`, one read per call: `loadSalesInvoiceDocumentIds` in `@carbon/database/deposit-scope`); a scoped prior source funds only requests that list its id, an unscoped one funds anything. A payment that is itself a deposit is refused on any other invoice ("A deposit for RA000001 can only be applied to that agreement's invoices", readable id via `loadDepositScope`). Source priority is unchanged (current payment, then prior sources by `postingDate`, `paymentId`) but the split is decided over all requests at once (`prioritizedTransport`), so a deposit's invoice never spends the ordinary cash another invoice needed and the outcome does not depend on request order — with no scoped source it is byte-identical to the old walk. `replaceInvoiceSettlements` and `post-payment` both apply it (post re-derives, so a crafted Draft cannot bypass it). `getAvailableOnAccountCreditSources` returns deposits in `sources` with their scope but leaves them out of `availableDocumentAmount` / `availableBaseAmount` (and `getAvailableOnAccountCredit`); `getOpenSalesInvoicesForCustomer` returns each invoice's `rentalAgreementIds` / `salesOrderIds`; `PaymentApplyTable` lists each deposit apart ("Deposit for RA000001 (its invoices only)"), sizes rows with `fundableDocumentAmounts`, and for a deposit payment lists only its document's invoices. The payment form's "Deposit for" / "Refund deposit for" combobox (`PaymentForm`, customer payments only) is fed by `getDepositDocuments` (`invoicing/invoicing.service.ts`, server-only, called by the loaders of `x+/payments+/new.tsx` and `x+/payments+/$paymentId.tsx`). It loads every rental agreement, every open sales order, and every sales order a Posted deposit Receipt already references (plus ids the caller must keep), each flagged `open` (open order / Draft or Active agreement). The form narrows to the payment's customer; a Receipt offers only `open` documents (plus its current one), a Disbursement also offers documents already holding a posted deposit, so a Closed agreement's or a finished order's deposit can be refunded. Deposits never touch revenue or recognition schedules.
- **Chart of accounts import** (`.ai/specs/2026-10-12-chart-of-accounts-import.md`) — `x+/accounting+/chart-import.tsx` (`update: accounting`), linked from the chart page's **Import** button only while the company has no cutover. Two CSVs, both required: the customer's chart (`parseChartCsv`, `account-import-csv.ts`, reads the header names other systems export and leaves out inactive rows; `chartTemplate`, served by `api+/accounting.chart-of-accounts[.]csv.tsx`, downloads the current chart in those columns) and the default accounts template (`defaultAccountsTemplate`, served by `api+/accounting.default-accounts[.]csv.tsx`; `parseDefaultsCsv`) with the customer's number for every `accountDefault` role and fixed asset class account.   defaults, resolutions }`, trimmed and size-capped, permission `update: accounting`) in one

## Journals and the accounting cutover

Every posting function writes its journal for every company. The company's
`companySettings.accountingCutoverDate` decides only the journal's status
(`.ai/specs/implemented/2026-10-08-accounting-cutover.md`). The `accountingEnabled` column still
exists, but no code reads it.

- **Status.** `journalPostingStatus(db, companyId)` (`@carbon/database/journal-posting-status`)
  returns `Provisional` when the company has no cutover and `Posted` when it has one.
  `postingStatusFor(cutoverDate)` is the same decision without a read, and
  `readAccountingCutoverDate` returns the date. All reads take `FOR SHARE`. The SQL
  posting functions (`complete_job_to_inventory`, `backflush_job_materials`) call
  `journal_posting_status(company_id)`.
- **The double read.** Only a posting function that resolves an accounting period
  BEFORE its transaction reads the status twice. It reads it once before the
  transaction, to decide whether to resolve the period. Inside the transaction it calls
  `assertPostingStatusUnchanged` (`@carbon/database/journal-posting-status`), which
  reads it again `FOR SHARE` and throws `POSTING_STATUS_CHANGED_ERROR` ("Accounting was
  just set up. Post the document again.") on a mismatch. A function that reads the
  status only inside its transaction needs no second read. `activate-accounting` takes
  `FOR UPDATE` on the same row, so no posting writes a Provisional journal after the
  enable commits.
- **No period before the cutover.** A Provisional journal has `accountingPeriodId`
  null, and no posting creates a period for it. Resolve a period only when the status
  is `Posted`.
- **Stand-in lines.** `resolveDefaultAccount(defaults, role, postingStatus)` picks the
  account for a line that needs one of the nullable defaults in `OPTIONAL_DEFAULT_ROLES`.
  A set default is used as is.
  - An empty default with a fallback in `DEFAULT_FALLBACKS` uses the fallback, before
    and after the cutover (for example `scrapAccount` →
    `inventoryAdjustmentVarianceAccount`, `salesReturnsAccount` → `salesAccount`). It
    never takes a stand-in, and the enable does not require it.
  - Only an empty default with no fallback takes a stand-in. Before the cutover it
    returns `retainedEarningsAccount` with `accountDefaultRole = role`, and the line
    stores that role in `journalLine.accountDefaultRole`. After the cutover it throws
    `MissingAccountDefaultError`: status 400, message "Set the <label> account in
    Accounting → Default Accounts.", with the label from `OPTIONAL_DEFAULT_LABELS`.
  - A void copies `accountDefaultRole` from each original line to its reversal.
  - A `revenueRecognitionSchedule` row never takes a stand-in. `post-sales-invoice`
    refuses when the deferral, contract asset or rental default it needs is empty, in
    both states. The invoice's own lines take stand-ins through `salesInvoiceStandIns`
    (`shared.service.ts`: `leaseRevenueAccount`,
    `netInvestmentInLeasesAccount`, `salesShippingRevenueAccount`).
- **Journal readers filter by a named status list** from
  `@carbon/database/accounting-posting`: `GL_JOURNAL_STATUSES` (Posted, Reversed) for
  balances, reports and tie-outs; `DOCUMENT_JOURNAL_STATUSES` (Provisional, Posted,
  Reversed) for a reader that follows one document's chain (void builders, GR/IR, WIP
  sums, intercompany lookups); `OPEN_ITEM_JOURNAL_STATUSES` (Provisional, Posted) for
  the payment control lookups and the memo, charge and reimbursement void checks.
  `PRE_CUTOVER_JOURNAL_STATUSES` (Provisional, Superseded) is not a reader's list: the
  enable wizard dates the start of the cost record from it. The
  `journal-status-filter` check (`@carbon/checks`) fails a `status <> 'Draft'` filter
  on a journal.
- **Opening lines.** The opening journal of the enable (`sourceType 'Opening Balance'`)
  copies the document keys and descriptions the original posting wrote. So the payment
  control lookups in `post-payment` accept `sourceType 'Opening Balance'` as well as the
  invoice, memo and `Payment` source types.
- **Voids of a document dated before the cutover** (`refuseVoidBeforeCutover`, `shared.service.ts`;
  `isBeforeCutover` in `@carbon/database/accounting-cutover-dates`):
  - A receipt or shipment void throws `INVENTORY_VOID_BEFORE_CUTOVER_ERROR` ("… Record a
    return or an inventory adjustment instead.").
  - A sales invoice void throws `SALES_INVOICE_VOID_BEFORE_CUTOVER_ERROR` ("… Issue a
    credit memo instead."). A purchase invoice void throws
    `PURCHASE_INVOICE_VOID_BEFORE_CUTOVER_ERROR` ("… Record a debit memo instead.").
  - A charge void throws `CHARGE_VOID_BEFORE_CUTOVER_ERROR` and a reimbursement void
    throws `REIMBURSEMENT_VOID_BEFORE_CUTOVER_ERROR` ("… Record a journal entry to
    correct it instead."). The date is `postingDate`, else `transactionDate` or
    `reimbursementDate`, as the posting uses.
  - A payment or memo void builds the document's posting again
    (`rebuildPaymentJournal` and `rebuildMemoJournal`, both in the ERP's
    `invoicing.service.ts`) and posts it negated,
    dated today, so it nets the opening lines. `assertNoMigrationClearing` refuses a
    rebuilt line on the Migration Clearing account. A customer credit memo that credits
    a contract or a rental agreement throws `MEMO_CREDIT_VOID_BEFORE_CUTOVER_ERROR`
    instead.
  - A time entry whose journal the enable superseded is not posted again.
    `post-production-event` refuses an event posted to GL with a line in a Superseded
    journal. `post-maintenance-event` refuses an entry whose expense changed (edited or
    deleted), and drops an unchanged one (`withoutSupersededEntries`,
    `resources.service.ts`). Both throw `TIME_ENTRY_BEFORE_CUTOVER_ERROR` ("…
    Record a journal entry to correct it instead.").
  - A company with no cutover has nothing before it, so every void works as before.
- **Zero-cost outbound movements keep their pair.** The adjustment core
  (`valueMovement`, `shared.service.ts`) skips the journal of an inbound
  movement at no value, but always writes the pair of an outbound one, at zero too.
  The enable's re-cost may give that movement a cost, and it adjusts the pair it finds.
- **Manual accounting work is refused before the cutover.**
  `assertAccountingCutover` (`shared.service.ts`) throws
  `ACCOUNTING_NOT_STARTED` ("Set up accounting before you post journals, runs or period
  closes."). `propose-revenue-recognition-run` and
  `recalculate-revenue-recognition-run` call it. The ERP service functions refuse the
  same work with the same message.

### `activate-accounting`

The one-way enable (`activateAccounting` in `apps/erp/app/modules/accounting/accounting.service.ts`,
input `{ cutoverDate, confirmation }`, permission `update: accounting`). The wizard's Enable
step calls the service function `enableAccountingCutover` (`accounting.server.ts`), which
runs it through `invokePosting`. It runs in one Kysely transaction. The steps use the
spec's section 5 numbering, as the code comments do:

1. Locks `companySettings` `FOR UPDATE`. Refuses when the company already has a cutover
   (`ACCOUNTING_ALREADY_SET_UP`) or when `confirmation` is not the company name. Runs
   `getActivationReadiness` and `getMigrationClearing`
   (`@carbon/database/accounting-cutover-reads`) again under the lock. Refuses on a
   failed check, or when Migration Clearing does not total zero
   (`isMigrationClearingZero`). The `account-defaults` check refuses an empty
   `migrationClearingAccount`, and one that is not an active Equity posting account.
   1a. Writes the journals of the legacy documents (`journalLegacyDocuments`, below).
2. Inventory reset (`resetAndRecostInventory`, `accounting.service.ts`).
   Closes the cost layers dated before the cutover and inserts one opening layer per
   item with stock (`planInventoryReset`). The unit cost comes from
   `getCutoverInventory`: a Standard item takes its standard cost, a FIFO or LIFO item
   the value its layers held at the cutover (`unitCostAtCutover` replays the layers
   dated before it), and an Average item `itemCost.unitCost`, the one cost the
   wizard's inventory step lets a user edit.
3. Re-cost. Replays the layers and outbound movements of FIFO and LIFO items dated on
   or after the cutover (`recostOutbound`), and books each document's difference as a
   Provisional "Cutover recost" journal, one per document and posting date. A
   `Revaluation` cost row (a serial recost) keeps its cost.
4. Sets every Provisional journal dated before the cutover to `Superseded`.
5. Sets every Planned `revenueRecognitionSchedule` row dated before the cutover to
   Posted with no journal.
6. Fixed assets: no write. The register already holds the values at the cutover.
7. Deletes the Draft trial balance journal and posts the opening journal
   (`buildOpeningJournalLines`), Posted, dated the day before the cutover. Before
   that, `markIgnoredInvoicesPaid` sets every invoice ignored on Migration Clearing
   (`clearing.ignoredItems`, rows of `accountingCutoverIgnoredInvoice` still open at
   the cutover) to `Paid` with `datePaid` the day before the cutover, with
   `app.sync_in_progress` on so no workflow, webhook or provider push fires. They are
   not in the opening journal, and the AR/AP readers treat a Paid invoice as closed.
8. `assignPeriods` gives each Provisional journal dated on or after the cutover the
   period of its date.
9. `repointStandInLines` moves each stand-in line to the default it names (or that
   default's fallback). Then `promoteJournals` promotes those journals to `Posted`.
   Both are in `accounting.service.ts`.
10. Closes every period that ends before the cutover and snapshots its balances
    (`snapshotAccountingPeriodBalances`). It does not call `closeAccountingPeriod`,
    which opens its own transaction.
    10a. `getCurrentAccountingPeriod` makes the period that holds today Active, as the
    first posting would.
11. Sets `accountingCutoverDate`, `accountingActivatedAt` and `accountingActivatedBy`.
    The `check_accounting_config_locked` trigger then refuses any change to them, to the
    base currency and to the fiscal year start month.

#### Legacy documents (step 1a)

A legacy document is posted (not Draft, Pending or Voided), dated on or after the
cutover, and has no journal line under its document keys in a journal of any status.
A company with accounting off wrote no journal, and the reset deleted the others'.
`journalLegacyDocuments` (`accounting.service.ts`) writes, for each one, the
journal its posting writes today: today's account defaults and item costs, in base
currency at the document's exchange rate, `Provisional`, dated the document's posting
date. The steps above then re-cost, period, re-point and promote it like any other
Provisional journal.

- **Detection** is one definition, `@carbon/database/legacy-documents`. Each function
  returns a query; the enable executes it, and `getLegacyDocumentCounts`
  (`@carbon/database/accounting-cutover-reads`) counts the same rows for the wizard's
  Enable step. Change the detection there, never in a builder.
- **Order** (`journalLegacyDocuments`): first the cost rows of movements that stored none
  (`writeLegacyMovementCosts`: sales order shipments, direct sales invoice lines, job
  issues and completions); then sales invoices, purchase invoices, memos, charges and
  reimbursements; then payments in posting order, because a payment reads the control
  line of what it settles; then purchase receipts, sales return receipts, sales
  shipments, return shipments, the adjustment core's movements, job issues and job
  completions; then depreciation runs, scrap disposals and revenue recognition runs
  (also in `accounting.service.ts`).
- **Movement costs.** `writeLegacyMovementCosts` (`accounting.service.ts`) costs an
  outbound FIFO or LIFO movement with no cost row against the layers open now
  (`relieveOpenLayers`: one locked read of the layers, then `replayReliefs`). A
  Standard item costs at its standard cost and an Average one at `itemCost.unitCost`.
  The enable then re-costs the FIFO and LIFO rows (step 3); the repair keeps what the
  open layers gave.
- **Memos and payments** are built by `rebuildMemoJournals` and `rebuildPaymentJournals`
  (`invoicing.service.ts`). The pre-cutover voids use the same code for one document
  (`rebuildMemoJournal`, `rebuildPaymentJournal`). The payment posting and its rebuild
  assemble the `buildPaymentJournal` input in one place (`assemblePaymentJournal`,
  `invoicing.service.ts`), and `apps/erp/test/database/post-payment/rebuild-journal.test.ts`
  pins a rebuilt journal to the posted one.
- **Every rebuilt journal stays out of provider sync.** The provider may already hold
  the original. So `insertProvisionalJournals` (`accounting.service.ts`) calls
  `keepOutOfProviderSync` for every journal it writes, in the enable's step 1a and in
  the repair. It writes an `Excluded` `accountingSyncOperation` per accounting
  integration with `errorCode` `CUTOVER_REBUILT` (`CUTOVER_REBUILT_SYNC_CODE`).
  Re-send in Sync Activity still pushes one.
- **Closed periods.** Before it writes a batch, `insertProvisionalJournals` refuses
  when a period that holds one of the batch's posting dates is Closed or Locked, or
  has `closedAt` set. The `InvalidInputError` names the period and says to reopen
  (Closed) or unlock (Locked) it. The enable never meets one; the repair can.
- **Not rebuilt** (no stored basis): labor and machine absorption of a legacy job, the
  offset of a serial recost or an asset cost adjustment, asset registrations and
  transfers, and the depreciation of an asset that left the books with no journal
  (`assetsLeavingWithoutJournal`).
- The result's `legacyJournals` counts the journals written per family
  (`LegacyJournalCounts`: `LegacyDocumentCounts` plus `movementCostRows`). A document
  whose journal has no lines gets none.

#### `journal-legacy-documents` (the repair after the enable)

A company enabled before step 1a existed, and a demo-template company (migration
20261009004448 backfilled its cutover), can still hold legacy documents. The
building block (`repairLegacyJournals`, `accounting.service.ts`; input `{}`, permission
`update: accounting`) runs step 1a again. The Settings service function in
`settings.server.ts` runs it through `invokePosting` as the caller. It runs in one transaction, under `companySettings` `FOR UPDATE`. It refuses a
company with no cutover (`ACCOUNTING_NOT_STARTED`). When it wrote journals,
`assignPeriods`, `repointStandInLines` and `promoteJournals` take only those
journals, by the ids `journalLegacyDocuments` returns. Then
`getCurrentAccountingPeriod` makes the period that holds today Active, as the
enable leaves it. Settings → Accounting shows the count (`hasLegacyDocuments`,
then `getLegacyDocumentCounts`) and a "Write missing journals" button.

`seedCompany` (ERP settings module, ported from `seed-company`) leaves a new company without a cutover, so it posts `Provisional`
journals until the activation wizard sets one. A subsidiary copies its `accountDefault`
ids and fixed asset class accounts from a company already in the group; the seeded
account numbers are only the fallback for a group with none.

## Safety

### Always
- MUST ensure journal entries balance — `postJournalEntry` validates total debits = total credits before posting.
- MUST use `rootSignMultiplier` logic for root account aggregation — Assets/Revenue add, Liabilities/Equity/Expense subtract.
- MUST scope chart of accounts by `companyGroupId` (shared across group) and transactions by `companyId`.
- MUST use `getOrCreateAccountingPeriod` before posting a Posted journal — it checks for closed periods and auto-creates missing ones. A Provisional journal (no cutover yet) has no period and creates none.
- MUST filter journal status with `GL_JOURNAL_STATUSES`, `DOCUMENT_JOURNAL_STATUSES` or `OPEN_ITEM_JOURNAL_STATUSES` — never `<> 'Draft'`, which counts Provisional and Superseded journals.
- MUST use `toStoredAmount` / `toDisplayDebit` / `toDisplayCredit` for amount conversion — respects account class sign conventions.

### Ask First
- Modifying the chart of accounts structure — affects all financial reporting.
- Posting depreciation — `postDepreciationRun` writes a journal per asset per month; only the latest posted run can be undone (Reverse Run).
- Changing fiscal year settings — impacts period boundaries and reporting.
- Generating intercompany eliminations — creates adjustment entries on elimination entities.

### Never
- Delete posted journal entries — MUST use `reverseJournalEntry` instead.
- Manually create a "Net Income" account — it is computed via `NET_INCOME_ACCOUNT_ID`.
- Bypass double-entry balance validation when posting journal entries.
- Write the cutover columns, or change a journal to `Posted` or `Superseded` outside `activate-accounting`. The enable is one-way; journal UPDATE RLS admits Draft and Posted only, and `check_posted_record_immutable` refuses any change to a Superseded journal or its lines.
- Delete an account any journal line or order / invoice / supplier quote line uses — those foreign keys are `ON DELETE RESTRICT` (`20261013005642`). The delete route disables Delete when `isAccountInUse` (`@carbon/database/account-import-reads`, across the group) is true. The chart lists active accounts only; there is no inactive-account view.

## Operations (`accounting.server.ts`)

Each is a `defineServiceFn` const in `accounting.server.ts`. The route calls it as `api.accounting.<name>(input)`; the API and MCP publish the ones tagged `@mcp` as `accounting_<name>`. It holds every check the route made. Routes validate the request, call one, and answer with `respond(request, result).redirect(…)` (a flash) or the result.

- Depreciation runs — `createDepreciationRun` (the picked month end, else the month after the last run; refuses a period not after the last run, a month that has not started, a period that has a run, and nothing to depreciate), `postDepreciationRun` (Draft only; refuses a run out of date with the assets or covered by a later posted run, and an asset class without its depreciation accounts), `recalculateDepreciationRun`, `repeatDepreciationRun` (a Posted run's period, for the assets no run there covers), `reverseDepreciationRun`.
- Revenue recognition and lease runs, by `type`: `createRevenueRecognitionRun`, `postRevenueRecognitionRun`, `repeatRevenueRecognitionRun` (the next month), `reverseRevenueRecognitionRun`, `recalculateRevenueRecognitionRun` (`recalculate-revenue-recognition-run`, run as the caller), `deleteRevenueRecognitionRun`. `createRevenueRecognitionRun`, `repeatRevenueRecognitionRun` and `createRunForPeriod` share `proposeRunAt`: a month that has not started and a period that already has a Draft of the type are refused before `propose-revenue-recognition-run` runs. The Draft refusal (and `createRunForPeriod`'s depreciation one) carries the Draft's id as its `cause`; `existingDraftRunId` reads it, and New Run, Repeat and the close page's Create Run open that Draft with the refusal as the flash.
- Periods — `lockPeriod`, `unlockPeriod`, `closePeriod` (`closePeriodWithChecklist`), `createRunForPeriod` (`run`: Revenue Recognition, Lease or Depreciation, for the period's end date; needs accounting create AND update). Close tasks — `closeTaskComplete`, `closeTaskSkip`.
- Journal entries — `createJournalEntry` (an empty Draft from the sequence), `saveJournalEntry` (every line needs an account and exactly one of a debit or a credit; `post` posts it after the save).
- Fixed assets — `registerFixedAsset`, `disposeFixedAsset` (scrap; Active or Fully Depreciated only), `purchaseFixedAsset` (a Draft PO with one Fixed Asset line; Draft or Under Construction only; `create: purchasing`), `sellFixedAsset` (a Draft SO line at net book value; Active or Fully Depreciated only; `create: sales`) — both delete the new order when its line fails, `takeFixedAssetOutOfService` (refuses Disposed, already out, and a unit on rent — `outOfServiceRefusal`, which the page's loader also asks), `returnFixedAssetToService`.
- `overrideExchangeRate` (refuses the base currency), `resetExchangeRate`. `updateCurrency` (pins the rate only when it was edited; `saveCurrencyWithRateOverride`).
- `moveAccount` (the parent must be a group of the chart, and not the account or one of its own accounts). `importChartOfAccounts` (`service.applyAccountImport`). `enableAccountingCutover` (`activate-accounting`). Neither of these two is tagged `@mcp`: they run from the import page and the activation wizard only.

A building block's own refusal ("Only open periods can be locked") is passed on with its message; a data-layer failure gets the route's old wording. Out of scope so far: the single-call actions (chart, cost center, dimension, payment term, project, asset class and fixed asset CRUD, journal post / reverse / delete, run deletes, period generate / delete, fiscal year, defaults, intercompany, report pins and views, the `invokeAssetTransfer` routes), the activation wizard's trial balance and fixed asset steps, and reimbursements and charges (invoicing). Each single-call action is still its own thin service function (`createCostCenter`, `postJournalEntry`, `deleteDepreciationRun`, …).
