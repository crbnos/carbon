# Supply Chain-Aware Carbon Emissions Framework

## Problem Statement

Current carbon tracking solutions focus primarily on emissions reporting rather than enabling operational decision-making within supply chains.

In real-world procurement environments, supplier selection directly impacts emissions, cost, and service levels. However, these dimensions are not integrated into a unified decision framework.

---

## Proposed Solution

This proposal introduces a supply chain-aware carbon framework to integrate emissions data into procurement and supplier management workflows.

---

## Key Enhancements

### 1. Supplier-Level Emissions Visibility
- Track emissions per supplier, region, and sourcing location
- Maintain carbon intensity benchmarks

### 2. Procurement Decision Support
Enable evaluation across:
- Cost
- Lead time
- Carbon emissions

### 3. Supplier Scoring Model
Extend supplier evaluation to include:
- Delivery reliability
- Cost efficiency
- Carbon footprint
- Sustainability compliance

### 4. Carbon-Aware Sourcing
- Recommend suppliers using multi-objective optimization
- Enable trade-off analysis (cost vs emissions)

---

## Business Impact

- Reduces supply chain carbon footprint
- Aligns procurement with ESG goals
- Improves transparency in supplier sustainability
- Supports better decision-making

---

## Future Scope

- Integration with lifecycle assessment tools
- Carbon pricing simulation
- Real-time supplier emissions tracking

---

## Conclusion

Embedding carbon awareness into procurement workflows transforms emissions tracking from passive reporting into an active decision-making capability for sustainable supply chains.
