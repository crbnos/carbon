After production, verify quality, preserve traceability, ship the finished units, and invoice the customer.

## Raise an issue

When an operator finds a defect, such as a short in the EPS wiring harness, create an Issue to record the <Term id="nonconformance">non-conformance</Term>. The issue moves from **Registered** to **In Progress** to **Closed** and links to the affected part and job operation.

## Workflows & actions

Each issue follows a configured workflow, such as <Term>8D</Term> or a containment-only response. The workflow creates required actions as independently tracked tasks:

<Figure illustration="issue-workflow" caption="An issue moves Registered → In Progress → Closed, spinning up its required-action tasks along the way." />

- <Term id="containment-action">Containment</Term>: prevent affected parts from proceeding. When applied to a process, it appears on the shop floor.
- <Term id="corrective-action">Corrective</Term>: identify and correct the root cause.
- <Term id="preventive-action">Preventive</Term>: prevent recurrence.
- Verification: confirm that the correction worked.
- Communication: update the customer.

<Callout tone="amber" badge="DISPOSITION" title="Held material doesn't move until someone signs off.">
A review determines the disposition of non-conforming assemblies: use as is, <Term>rework</Term>, <Term>scrap</Term>, or return to supplier. Carbon records the decision and related actions on the issue.
</Callout>

## Versioned procedures

Work instructions come from versioned procedures with **Draft**, **Active**, and **Archived** states, like <Term id="method">methods</Term>. Publishing a corrective revision applies it to future jobs while in-progress jobs retain their original revision.

<Callout tone="green" badge="REVISIONS" title="Change the procedure once; the floor follows everywhere.">
New jobs use the latest published version. In-progress jobs retain their assigned version, and each finished unit records the procedure revision used to build it.
</Callout>

## Traceability

Carbon stores the finished unit's <Term>genealogy</Term> as a graph of <Term id="tracked-entity">tracked entities</Term> and their activities. From one serial, you can trace backward to its components and material lots or forward from a lot to every unit that consumed it.

<Figure illustration="traceability-graph" caption="The genealogy of one finished robot, from serial number down to material lots." />

Open the record or scan its QR label to identify the material in the unit and every other use of the same lots.

<Screenshot src="/screens/ship-traceability-graph.png" label="Traceability graph" caption="The full as-built genealogy for SAT-1000 serial SAT1000-SN-0001, down to the aluminium plate and reaction wheels it consumed." ratio="wide" />

## Ship & invoice

Create a [shipment](/guides/order-to-cash) against the <Term id="sales-order">sales order</Term>. Carbon drafts lines from the outstanding quantity. If SHP000001 ships 2 of 3 ordered units, 1 remains outstanding. Post the shipment, then <Term id="invoice">invoice</Term> the 2 shipped units. Because shipping and invoicing both remain, the sales order stays **"To Ship and Invoice"**.

After the final unit ships, the sales order moves to **"To Invoice"** until that unit is invoiced; it then becomes **"Completed"**.

Posting submits the invoice. When accounting is enabled and the invoice produces journal lines, Carbon also creates a posted sales-invoice journal. Payment remains separate: applying a posted payment or credit creates settlements and moves the invoice to **"Partially Paid"** or **"Paid"**.
