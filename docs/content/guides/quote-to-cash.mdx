This guide follows a customer inquiry from quote creation through conversion to a sales order. The [next guide](/guides/order-to-cash) covers shipment, invoicing, and payment.

## One opportunity, many documents

In Carbon, a <Term id="rfq">request for quote</Term>, <Term>quote</Term>, and <Term>sales order</Term> link to one **<Term>opportunity</Term>**. The opportunity can reference each document through `salesRfqId`, `quoteId`, and `salesOrderId`; none is required.

<Figure illustration="opportunity-thread" caption="Three documents (RFQ, quote, order) hang off one opportunity, and any slot can be empty." />

You can therefore create a sales order directly, create a quote without an RFQ, or use all three documents. The opportunity records the relationship without enforcing a sequence.

<Callout tone="neutral" badge="NO RIGID CHAIN" title="Quotes are optional. The opportunity is the thread.">
A sales order does not require a quote, and a quote does not require an RFQ. Link the documents to one opportunity regardless of where the sales process starts.
</Callout>

## Build the quote

Create a quote and add one line for each requested item. Each line supports quantity-break pricing. The negotiated net unit price carries forward to the sales order.

A new quote is **"Draft"**. Each line progresses independently through **"Draft"**, **"In Progress"**, and **"Complete"**, allowing a multi-item quote to be priced incrementally.

<Screenshot src="/screens/quote-builder.png" label="Quote builder" caption="A multi-line quote with quantity-break pricing, each line worked up independently." ratio="wide" />

Finalizing the quote moves it to **"Sent"** and marks its lines **"Complete"**. Only a Sent quote can proceed to customer action.

## Send it to the customer

With digital quotes enabled, a **"Sent"** quote provides a private, unguessable share link that requires no login. The customer view excludes internal notes.

The customer can:

- Select **Accept Quote** to create a sales order, then optionally attach their purchase order as a PDF.
- Select **Reject Quote** to move the quote to **"Lost"**.

Carbon records either result on the quote.

<Callout tone="blue" badge="DIGITAL QUOTE" title="Acceptance is a first-class action, not an inbox.">
Acceptance converts the quote; rejection records the outcome. The customer portal can then display order and job status.
</Callout>

<Screenshot src="/screens/quote-digital-customer.png" label="Digital quote: customer view" caption="What the customer sees: line pricing, an 'Accept Quote' button, and a 'Reject Quote' link beneath it." ratio="wide" />

## When a quote doesn't land

Carbon records non-converted quotes as **"Expired"** after the validity date, **"Lost"** after customer rejection, or **"Cancelled"** after internal withdrawal.

To revise pricing or terms, create a new quote revision. Carbon increments the revision number and retains the previous terms.

<Callout tone="neutral" badge="REVISE, DON'T RESTART" title="Re-quoting bumps a revision and keeps the trail.">
Create a revision from a lost or expired quote to retain the complete negotiation history.
</Callout>

## Into a sales order

Internal conversion and customer acceptance both create a sales order. The order inherits the negotiated net unit prices and opportunity. Carbon moves the quote to **"Ordered"**, or **"Partial"** if only some lines convert. The new order opens at **"To Ship and Invoice"**.

Production, shipment, and invoicing use the sales order as their source.

<Callout tone="amber" badge="ORDER → JOB, NOT QUOTE → JOB" title="Jobs come from the order, never the quote.">
A quote cannot create a production job. A [make-to-order](/guides/order) sales-order line creates the job, so the sequence is quote → order → job.
</Callout>
