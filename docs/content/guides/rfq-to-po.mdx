This guide covers supplier solicitation and purchase-order creation. The [next guide](/guides/receive-and-bill) covers receipt, invoice matching, and payment.

<Figure illustration="rfq-fanout" caption="One purchasing RFQ fans out to a supplier quote each; the winning bid converts to a purchase order." />

## One RFQ, many suppliers

A <Term id="rfq">purchasing RFQ</Term> sends the same requested lines to multiple suppliers for comparison. A new RFQ starts in **"Draft"**.

Finalizing the RFQ creates one **Draft supplier quote** per supplier and links each quote to the request. Carbon can email each supplier a private response link. The RFQ moves to **"Requested"**.

<Callout tone="neutral" badge="LOOSELY COUPLED" title="RFQ and supplier quote are both optional.">
RFQs and supplier quotes are optional; you can create a purchase order directly. When used, junction records connect one RFQ to multiple supplier quotes and purchase orders.
</Callout>

<Screenshot src="/screens/rfq-supplier-comparison.png" label="Purchasing RFQ — supplier comparison" caption="One request, the same lines, fanned out to several suppliers to compare." ratio="wide" />

## Suppliers quote back

Each supplier quote has a private share link that requires no login. The supplier enters pricing, quantity breaks, and lead times. Submission moves the quote to **"Active"** and records the prices; rejection moves it to **"Declined"**.

<Callout tone="blue" badge="SUPPLIER PORTAL" title="The portal hangs off the quote, not the request.">
Each supplier responds through a separate quote. Compare the quotes and convert the selected one to an order.
</Callout>

<Screenshot src="/screens/rfq-supplier-portal.png" label="Supplier quote portal" caption="The supplier's view: enter unit price, quantity breaks, and lead time, then submit." ratio="wide" />

## Into a purchase order

Converting a supplier quote creates a <Term>purchase order</Term> with its supplier, lines, negotiated pricing, <Term>conversion factors</Term>, exchange rate, and supplier-interaction history. The order type is **"Purchase"** for items or **<Term id="outside-operation">"Outside Processing"</Term>** for subcontracted operations.

Carbon derives the purchase-order status from its lines. A finalized order starts at **"To Receive and Invoice"**. If its value exceeds an approval threshold, it moves to **"Needs Approval"** until approved.

<Callout tone="amber" badge="STATUS IS COMPUTED" title="A PO's status is derived from its lines, every time.">
Each line tracks whether it is fully received and invoiced. Carbon rolls those values into **"To Receive and Invoice"**, **"To Receive"**, **"To Invoice"**, or **"Completed"**.
</Callout>

<Screenshot src="/screens/rfq-purchase-order.png" label="Purchase order" caption="The order converted from the chosen supplier quote, sitting at 'To Receive and Invoice'." ratio="wide" />

## Send work out, too

Use an **"Outside Processing"** order for a job operation performed by a vendor, such as plating, heat treatment, or anodizing. The order links to the job operation instead of a stock item.

Posting an outside-processing receipt does not add inventory. It advances the job operation and posts the vendor charge to the job's [work-in-process](/guides/job-costing).

<Callout tone="blue" badge="OUTSIDE PROCESSING" title="A PO can buy an operation, not just an item.">
Receiving an outside-processing order advances the [make-to-order](/guides/order) operation and charges its cost to WIP.
</Callout>
