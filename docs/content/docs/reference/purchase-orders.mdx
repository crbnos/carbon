A **purchase order** commits the company to buy from a supplier. It can be created directly or from a <Term id="supplier-quote">supplier quote</Term>. <Term id="receipt">Receipts</Term> and <Term id="invoice">supplier invoices</Term> reconcile against its lines.

Each purchase-order line tracks received and invoiced quantities independently. The line closes only after both quantities are satisfied, allowing receiving and billing to advance separately.

## Line fields

<FieldTable>
  <Field name="Item" type="reference">What's being bought.</Field>
  <Field name="Purchase quantity" type="number">Ordered quantity.</Field>
  <Field name="Quantity received" type="number">Received so far; `quantity to receive` is the remainder.</Field>
  <Field name="Quantity invoiced" type="number">Billed so far; `quantity to invoice` is the remainder.</Field>
  <Field name="Conversion factor" type="number">Converts the supplier's purchase unit to the stock unit.</Field>
</FieldTable>

## Types

A purchase order is one of three kinds: a plain **Purchase**, a **Return** to the supplier, or **<Term id="outside-operation">Outside Processing</Term>** — sending parts out to a vendor for a production step, where the line links to a job operation.

## Status

The status is computed from the state of its lines, never set by hand.

## Dates

The order and every line carry three dates: the <Term id="purchase-order-delivery-requested-date">Requested Date</Term>, the <Term id="purchase-order-delivery-promised-date">Promised Date</Term> and the <Term id="purchase-order-delivery-actual-date">Delivery Date</Term>.

The order's dates are set on the **Shipping** card and in the properties panel. A line's dates are set in the **Dates** section of the line page, and the order summary shows each line's promised date as a **Promised** pill you can change in place.

A new line starts on the order's Requested and Promised dates. When you change an order date, every line whose date is empty or still the old order date takes the new one. A line you gave its own date keeps it.

<Callout type="info" title="The Delivery Date only reaches lines that received goods">
The Delivery Date records when goods arrived, so a new order Delivery Date moves only lines with a quantity received. Posting a receipt also sets each received line's Delivery Date to the day of the receipt, which replaces a date you corrected by hand.
</Callout>

Each date locks at a different point:

- **Requested Date** locks once the order is confirmed (from **"To Receive and Invoice"** onward).
- **Promised Date** stays editable until the order is fully received (**"To Invoice"**, **"Completed"** or **"Closed"**). A line's promised date also locks when the line is received complete, and such a line keeps its requested and promised dates when the order's dates change.
- **Delivery Date** can always be corrected.

## Troubleshooting

### "Cannot modify a confirmed purchase order." / "Cannot delete lines on a confirmed purchase order."
A purchase order is locked at **"To Receive"**, **"To Receive and Invoice"**, **"To Invoice"**, **"Completed"**, and **"Closed"** — from release onward. Only Draft (and pre-release) orders are freely editable. Reopen the order to make structural changes.

### "Cannot finalize: supplier is not approved (Active)"
The company requires approved suppliers, and this order's supplier is *Pending*, *Inactive*, or *Rejected*. Set the supplier's status to **Active** (or get it approved), then finalize again.

### "You are not authorized to finalize this purchase order"
The order belongs to a different company than the one the user is signed into. Switch to the owning company.

### "Receiving can only be closed or reopened on a released purchase order"
Short-closing a line only applies once the order is released (at "To Receive", "To Receive and Invoice", "To Invoice", or "Completed"). Release the order first.

### "This line cannot be received"
The line is a comment or G/L account line — only item lines carry a receivable quantity.

### "This line has no outstanding quantity to receive"
The line is already fully received; there's nothing left to short-close. Reopen receiving on the line if more goods are expected.

### "Receiving is already closed for this line" / "Receiving is not closed for this line"
The close/reopen action was applied twice. The line is already in the requested state — refresh the page to see the current state.

### "Cannot change the requested date of a confirmed purchase order."
A line's Requested Date (`purchaseOrderLine.requiredDate`) locks at the same statuses as the order. Reopen the order to change it, or record the supplier's new date as the Promised Date.

### "Cannot change the promised date of a received line." / "…of a fully received purchase order." / "…of a closed purchase order."
The line is received complete, the order is at "To Invoice" or "Completed", or the order was closed (a Closed order may still have lines with nothing received; reopen it to change the date). Line dates save one at a time through `updatePurchaseOrderLineDate`; the order's Receipt Promised Date through `updatePurchaseOrders`.

### "Failed to finalize purchase order"
Generic wrapper. Common underlying causes: no PO number sequence configured for the company, or a PDF/email step failing (separate "Failed to generate PDF" / "Failed to send email" messages). Ask for the full error text and check the supplier contact's email if sending failed.
</AgentContext>
