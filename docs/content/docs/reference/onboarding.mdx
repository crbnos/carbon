A new account remains in the `/onboarding` wizard until it belongs to a company. Onboarding is complete when the company has a **name** and at least **one location**. Submitting the Industry step creates both.

<Screenshot label="The setup wizard's Company step: name, address, base currency, and website fields" caption="Company details are saved for tenant provisioning on the next step." />

<Steps>
<Step>
**Theme.** Pick a color theme (cosmetic, stored in a cookie).
</Step>
<Step>
**Your profile.** First and last name, both required.
</Step>
<Step>
**Company.** Company name, full address, base currency, optional website.
</Step>
<Step>
**Industry.** How to start: a [demo-data template](/docs/platform/demo-data) for your industry, or a clean, empty company. Carbon staff also get a [backup](/docs/platform/backups) restore option. Submitting creates the company and everything a usable tenant needs.
</Step>
</Steps>

The **Plan** step appears only in Carbon Cloud and opens Stripe checkout. Self-hosted installations skip it.

<Callout type="note" title="Company provisioning">
Creating the company also creates a **"Headquarters"** location from its address, links you as an employee, and seeds the chart of accounts, posting accounts, numbering sequences, payment terms, and units of measure. Fiscal year, periods, and sequences use seeded defaults that you can change under [company settings](/docs/reference/company-settings).
</Callout>

## Where to go next

After onboarding, review [company settings](/docs/reference/company-settings), configure document numbering under [sequences](/docs/reference/sequences), add users under [people](/docs/reference/people), and use the [guides](/guides) for end-to-end workflows.

## Internals and troubleshooting

The completion gate is the layout loader (`routes/onboarding+/_layout.tsx:33-44`); step order is `utils/path.ts:2103-2109`; conditional steps at `_layout.tsx:49-53`. Theme validator `settings.models.ts:306-311`; profile `onboardingUserValidator` (`account.models.ts:12-17`); company `addressValidator` (`settings.models.ts:95-107`). Provisioning runs `provisionOnboardingCompany` (`services/onboarding.server.ts`) with the seed via `seedCompany` (`settings.service.ts:751-765`).

### Stuck in the setup wizard / keeps redirecting to /onboarding
The company lacks a name or a location — there is no `onboarded` flag. Complete the Company step, then submit the Industry step; the Industry step creates both at once.

### "Restore from a backup" isn't offered on the Industry step
Internal Carbon staff accounts only. Its absence for regular sign-ups is expected.

### The Plan step isn't showing
Cloud-only (app.carbon.ms). Self-hosted installs skip it and land at `/x`.
</AgentContext>
