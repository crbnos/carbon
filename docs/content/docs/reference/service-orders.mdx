title: Service orders
description: Send a technician to a customer's unit, charge time, parts and expenses to its work tree, decide who pays, and invoice the rest.

A **service order** sends a technician to one [customer asset](/docs/reference/customer-assets) at the customer's site. It holds a tree of work and collects every charge until you invoice it. Each charge decides on its own whether warranty, a contract or the customer pays.

Open the **Service** module. It has **Service Orders**, **My Visits**, **Schedule**, **Customer Assets**, **Service Templates** and **My Timesheet**.

## The service order

Click **Add Service Order** on the list, **Service Order** in the Create menu, or **New Service Order** on an asset. Carbon numbers the order `SVC…`. A unit can have only one open service order at a time.

<FieldTable>
  <Field name="Unit" type="reference" required>The customer asset, found by serial. It must be "In Service". The unit sets the customer, the site, the item and the serial of the order.</Field>
  <Field name="Service Location" type="reference" required>Your location that does the work.</Field>
  <Field name="Coverage Contract" type="reference">A <Term>contract</Term> of the unit's customer that pays for work outside warranty. If you leave it empty and the customer has exactly one Active contract, Carbon uses that contract.</Field>
  <Field name="Priority" type="enum">Low, Medium, High or Urgent.</Field>
  <Field name="Start Work From" type="enum">Where the first work tree comes from: **Empty**, **Service template**, **The unit's build history** or **The item's bill of materials**. See [the work tree](#the-work-tree).</Field>
</FieldTable>

The form also takes a **Contact** and the **Problem**. The order has the tabs **Details**, **Work**, **Visits** and **Billing**. Its contact, site and coverage contract can change only while it is "Draft".

## Status lifecycle

<StatusFlow entity="serviceOrder">
  <Status name="Draft">No live visit.</Status>
  <Status name="Scheduled">A visit is scheduled and none has started.</Status>
  <Status name="In Progress">A visit is traveling or on site, or completed with work left.</Status>
  <Status name="On Hold" branch>Set with **Put on Hold**. **Resume** returns the order to its visits' status.</Status>
  <Status name="Completed" accent>The technician answered **Work complete?** with yes, or you clicked **Complete**. The order is ready to invoice.</Status>
  <Status name="Closed">**Create Invoice** closes the order. With nothing left to bill, **Close** closes it.</Status>
  <Status name="Cancelled" terminal>Set with **Cancel**. It also cancels the order's open visits.</Status>
</StatusFlow>

<Callout type="info" title="Visits drive the status.">
You set On Hold, Completed, Closed and Cancelled. Carbon sets the rest from the visits, and cancelled visits do not count. **Reopen**, in the order's menu, returns a Completed order to its visits' status.
</Callout>

You can cancel an order only when every charge on it is reversed. Deleting the Draft invoice of an order moves the order from Closed back to Completed, and its billable items back to Unbilled.

## The work tree

The **Work** tab holds the order's tree of **nodes**. A node is a System, a Task or a Folder. Click **Add Node** or **Add Child Node** to add one. You can delete a node that has no charges.

**Start Work From** on the new order, or **Build Tree** on the Work tab, creates System nodes from one source:

- **The unit's build history:** the components recorded on the unit's serial. The new order keeps bought parts, because those are what service replaces.
- **The item's bill of materials:** the item's made components.
- **A service template:** every node of the template, with its planned materials, labor and other costs.

The new order builds two levels from a serial or an item. **Build Tree** lets you choose the depth, the parent node and whether to include bought components, and shows a preview first.

Click a node to open its drawer: the last service date of its serial or item, and its charges.

**Fixed price.** In the node drawer, enter a **Fixed Price** to bill the node and the work under it as one line. Leave it empty to bill each item.

## Charges

Charge a node from its drawer or from the visit page. Each charge posts at once.

- **Log Time** posts a timesheet entry on the order. Its billable item is priced at the service labor item's sale price.
- **Issue Part** takes a part from stock, by default from your own van. It is priced at the part's sale price, and it is written to the asset's [installed components](/docs/reference/customer-assets#installed-components). A return reverses one whole issue.
- **Add Expense** records a date, a description and an amount.

Service time shows read-only on My Timesheet and never goes through **Submit week**.

## Coverage

Carbon decides the coverage of each charge when it posts, from the charge date:

1. Time inside the asset's labor warranty is **Warranty**.
2. A part inside the asset's parts warranty, or inside the warranty of the installed component on the node, is **Warranty**.
3. An expense is never Warranty.
4. Any other charge is **Contract** when the order has a coverage contract.
5. Otherwise it is **Billable**.

A **Capitalized** coverage exists for refurbishment work on your own stock. A field visit does not create it.

| Coverage | Cost posts to | Billable item |
|---|---|---|
| **Warranty** | Warranty Expense | price 0, "(Covered by warranty)" |
| **Contract** | the contract's account, else Cost of Goods Sold | price 0, "(Covered by contract)" |
| **Billable** | Cost of Goods Sold | the sale price |

To change a coverage, click **Override** on a charge in the node drawer. The **Override Coverage** dialog needs a new coverage and a **Reason**. The cost moves to the new coverage's account. You cannot override a billed charge or a reversal.

<Callout type="warn" title="Set Warranty Expense before the first warranty charge.">
A warranty charge posts to the **Warranty Expense** account in **Accounting → Default Accounts**. Without it, the charge fails with "Set Warranty Expense in Account Defaults before you charge a service order".
</Callout>

## Billing and invoicing

The **Billing** tab lists every billable item by node, with items on no node last under "Other charges". Review each **Unbilled** item from its row menu:

- **Edit Price** changes the unit price.
- **Hold** keeps the item off the invoice. **Release** returns it to Unbilled.
- **Write Off** means the item is never billed. Its cost stays on the order. A reason is required.

**Create Invoice** is on a Completed order that has an Unbilled item. It drafts a sales [invoice](/docs/reference/invoices) of the Unbilled items, laid out by the tree, and closes the order. The order's id becomes the customer reference.

- **Itemized** (the default **Invoice Detail**): each top-level node is a heading, followed by one line per item.
- **By Node**: one line per top-level node at its subtree total.
- A fixed-price node is one line at its price. Its items follow at price 0, marked "Included in fixed price".
- Covered items appear at price 0, marked as covered by warranty or by the contract.

Tax on the draft is 0. The invoice does not move stock again: the part left stock when it was issued.

**Print Estimate**, on the Work tab and in the order's menu, makes a PDF of the planned work at sale prices. Labor uses the service labor item's price.

## Visits

A visit sends one technician to the unit in a planned window. On the order's **Visits** tab, click **Schedule Visit** and choose the **Technician**, **Planned Start** and **Planned End**. **Reschedule** moves a visit that is still Scheduled.

<StatusFlow entity="serviceVisit">
  <Status name="Scheduled">Planned. **Start Travel** or **Arrive** moves it on.</Status>
  <Status name="Traveling">The technician clicked **Start Travel**. Carbon records the time.</Status>
  <Status name="On Site" accent>The technician clicked **Arrive**. Carbon records the time.</Status>
  <Status name="Completed">The technician clicked **Complete** and answered **Work complete?**</Status>
  <Status name="Cancelled" terminal>**Cancel Visit**, from any open status.</Status>
</StatusFlow>

On **Work complete?**, **Yes, Work Complete** completes the order. **No, More Work** completes only the visit, and the order stays In Progress.

**My Visits** is the technician's phone page: their visits for today and the next 7 days, with a map link. A visit opens a one-column page:

- **Unit:** the item, serial, warranty badge and the last 3 orders.
- **Work:** pick a node, then **Issue Part**, **Add Expense** or **Add Time**. **Start Work** starts a clock on the device, and **Stop Work** opens Log Time with the elapsed hours.
- **Notes**, **Photos** and a **Signature** with the signer's name.

**Schedule** shows 7 days of visits by day and technician, filtered to **Not done** by default. You schedule visits from the order, not from this page.

## Service templates

A **service template** is a reusable work tree. Click **Add Service Template**, add nodes, then open a node to plan its materials, labor and other costs. An order started from a template copies its nodes and plan lines, so its estimate has prices.

## Permissions

Service uses its own `service` permissions: view, create, update and delete.

- A technician without `service` update works only their own visits. They can move the visit, charge it, write notes and sign until it is Completed or Cancelled. They cannot override a coverage.
- **Issue Part** also needs `inventory` update.
- **Create Invoice** also needs `invoicing` create.

## Settings

- **Settings → Invoicing → Service Labor:** the **Service Labor Item**, a Service item whose sale price prices time on charges, estimates and invoices.
- **Accounting → Default Accounts → Warranty Expense** (under Operating Expenses): the account for warranty charges.
- **Labor Warranty (months)** and **Parts Warranty (months)** on the item's **Sales** tab. See [customer assets](/docs/reference/customer-assets).
- **Van (default storage unit)** on the person's **Job**: where their service parts are issued from first. It must be at the job's location.

## Troubleshooting

### "A field visit needs a unit in service. This unit is …"
The chosen customer asset is not In Service (for example, Returned after its shipment was voided). Only an In Service asset can get a service order.

### A unit cannot get a second service order
A serial can have only one open service order (any status other than Closed or Cancelled). Close or cancel the open one first.

### "The coverage contract is not one of this customer's contracts"
The Coverage Contract belongs to another customer. Pick one of the unit's customer's contracts, or leave it empty.

### "This service order has 2 charges that are not reversed. Reverse every charge before you cancel the order, or complete the order and write off its billable items."
At least one charge on the order still stands. Return each part with **Issue Part** and **Return to stock**, then cancel. A coverage override does not reverse a charge: it moves the cost to a new charge. Time and expenses cannot be reversed from a service order yet. For those, complete the order, write off its unbilled items on the Billing tab, then Close it.

### "Set Warranty Expense in Account Defaults before you charge a service order"
The company has no Warranty Expense account default. Set it in Accounting → Default Accounts (Operating Expenses group). The same message names Cost of Goods Sold or Work in Progress when those defaults are missing.

### "The time is logged but did not post: ..." / "The expense is saved but did not post: ..."
The entry was saved but posting failed; the text after the colon is the posting error (often a missing account default or a closed accounting period).

### "Return the whole quantity of one issue" / "No open issue of this part to this node matches the return"
A service part return reverses exactly one earlier issue of the same item (and serial or batch) to the same node. Partial returns are refused.

### "You can charge only your own visits" / "This visit belongs to another technician" / "A completed visit takes no changes" / "A cancelled visit takes no changes"
The user lacks service_update, so they can only charge, sign, write notes on, or move their own open visit. Grant service_update, or have the visit's technician do it.

### "You do not have permission to change a coverage"
Coverage overrides need service_update.

### "This charge is already reversed or reclassed" / "The charge is already invoiced. Credit the invoice first."
Override applies only to an original charge that is not reversed, not reclassed and not Billed.

### "Only a refurbishment charge is Capitalized, and it stays Capitalized"
Overrides cannot move a charge to or from Capitalized.

### "Service order … is …. Only a Completed order is invoiced." / "Service order … has no unbilled charge. Close it instead."
Create Invoice needs a Completed order with at least one Unbilled billable item. With none, use Close.

### "Only an unbilled item can change"
Edit Price, Hold and Write Off apply only to Unbilled or On Hold items; a reversal row takes no action.

### "Choose a service item"
The Service Labor Item in Settings → Invoicing must be an item of type Service.

### "The van must be a storage unit at the job's location"
The Van on the person's Job tab must be a storage unit at the job's location.

### Labor bills at 0
The Service Labor Item is not set in Settings → Invoicing, or it has no sale price.

### Log Time / Start Work is disabled
The company has no labor category.

### Create Invoice is missing from the order header
The order is not Completed, it has no Unbilled item (then Close shows instead), or the user lacks service_update or invoicing_create.

### Issue Part is disabled
The user lacks inventory_update.

## Internals
- Each service order owns a Service project (projectType Service, costTreatment By Coverage, identifier = the order's readable id). Its elements are the nodes. Service projects are hidden from the Projects module, charge number pickers and dimension pickers.
- Readable id: sequence serviceOrder, prefix SVC, size 6 (SVC000001). Order types in the enum: Field Visit, Depot Repair, Refurbishment; only Field Visit is created.
- Status roll-up: rollUpOrderStatus (service.models.ts); allowed moves canMoveServiceOrder and canMoveVisit; saved by syncServiceOrderStatus (service.server.ts), which also sets the project status (On Hold → On Hold, Completed → Technically Complete, Closed/Cancelled → Closed, else Released).
- Coverage: resolveCoverage (packages/utils/src/service-coverage.ts). Capitalized charges post to Work in Progress and create no billable item.
- Charges: route service-order+/$id.charge.tsx with intent time / part / expense / reclass; server functions post-timesheet, post-project-issue, post-project-expense, reclass-project-coverage. Time posts Dr coverage account / Cr Labor Absorption ("Service Labor"); expenses Dr coverage account / Cr GR/IR ("Service Expense"); parts "Service Material". All carry the Customer dimension.
- Invoice: create-service-order-invoice (layout in plan.ts). post-sales-invoice skips stock and COGS on lines stamped by billable items. Deleting the Draft invoice runs releaseServiceOrderStamps.
- The Start Work clock is stored in the browser's localStorage (key `carbon:service-work:<visitId>`); clearing storage or switching devices loses a running clock.
- Visit photos: private bucket `${companyId}/service/${orderId}/${visitId}/`; signature `signature.png` in the same folder (PNG, at most 2 MB).
- The visit window picker uses the browser's time zone, not the company's.
- Estimate PDF: `/file/service-estimate/<id>.pdf` (ServiceEstimatePDF); not in the document template customizer.
- Routes: list `/x/service-orders`, order `/x/service-order/<id>`, visit `/x/service-order/<id>/visits/<visitId>`, My Visits `/x/service-orders/my-visits`, Schedule `/x/service-orders/schedule`, templates `/x/service-orders/templates`.
</AgentContext>
