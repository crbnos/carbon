**Training** manages required courses, employee-group assignments, and completion records. A training program contains written content or a PDF, and a quiz. Training taken outside Carbon is an external assignment with only a name, which an admin marks complete. Carbon tracks completion for each employee and period, sends weekly reminders for outstanding assignments, and allows managers to record manual completion. Training belongs to the [resources](/docs/reference/work-centers) area and uses the `resources` permission.

For example, create a "Lockout/Tagout" training with type **"Mandatory"** and frequency **"Annual"**, then assign it to the "Machinists" group. Each group member remains **"Pending"** for the current year until completion and receives a Sunday reminder while outstanding.

## The training catalog

Each training is a row in the `training` table (`packages/database/supabase/migrations/20251205021915_training.sql:18`). The create form requires only a **Name** (`apps/erp/app/modules/resources/ui/Training/TrainingForm.tsx:76`). Configure the remaining fields in the **Properties** panel (`apps/erp/app/modules/resources/ui/Training/TrainingProperties.tsx`), which saves each change through a bulk-update action.

<FieldTable>
<Field name="name" required>The training's title.</Field>
<Field name="type" type="Mandatory | Optional">Whether completion is required. Drives the badge on the list and, for mandatory trainings, the sense of "you must finish this."</Field>
<Field name="frequency" type="Once | Quarterly | Annual">How often the training must be re-taken. **"Once"** means complete it a single time, forever. **"Quarterly"** and **"Annual"** are *recurring* — completion is tracked per period and resets when a new period begins.</Field>
<Field name="status" type="Draft | Active | Archived">Lifecycle of the training itself, changed from the page header. See below.</Field>
<Field name="content">The written training material, stored as rich JSON (`content`). Employees read it before the questions.</Field>
<Field name="guidePath">The uploaded PDF. A training with a PDF shows the PDF to employees instead of the written content.</Field>
<Field name="estimatedDuration">A free-text estimate of how long the training takes.</Field>
<Field name="tags">Free-form tags for filtering the catalog.</Field>
<Field name="assignee">The person responsible for the training.</Field>
</FieldTable>

The status enum is a simple linear lifecycle on the training record — not to be confused with an employee's *completion* status further down.

Change the status from the page header: a Draft training shows **Publish**, an Active one shows **Archive**, and **Reopen** in the ⋯ menu moves it back to Draft. Each asks for confirmation.

<StatusFlow entity="trainingCourse">
<Status name="Draft">Being authored. Not yet meant for assignment.</Status>
<Status name="Active" accent>Published and in use. Assignments and reminders operate against active trainings.</Status>
<Status name="Archived" terminal>Retired. Kept for history, no longer assigned.</Status>
</StatusFlow>

<Callout type="note" title="Only a Draft training can change">
The content, the PDF, and the questions are editable only while the training is **"Draft"**; the server refuses a PDF change on any other status (`apps/erp/app/routes/x+/training+/update.tsx`). Employees can open and complete only an **"Active"** training. To change a published training, **Reopen** it first.
</Callout>

## Written content or a PDF

A training's content is one of two kinds, chosen with the **Write | Upload PDF** toggle on the **Content** card:

- **Write:** type the material in the editor.
- **Upload PDF:** drop one PDF, for shops that already have their training material as a document. **Replace** swaps it (`apps/erp/app/modules/resources/ui/Training/TrainingGuide.tsx`).

Employees see the PDF in a viewer when the training has one, and the written content when it does not. To go back to written content, choose **Write**; Carbon asks before it removes the PDF. Both change only while the training is Draft.

## Quiz questions

A training can carry a quiz. Each question is a row in `trainingQuestion` (`20251205021915_training.sql:229`), owned by one training, ordered by `sortOrder`, and marked `required` or not. Carbon supports five question types (`apps/erp/app/modules/resources/resources.models.ts:343`):

<FieldTable>
<Field name="MultipleChoice">Pick exactly one correct answer from a list of options.</Field>
<Field name="MultipleAnswers">Pick one or more correct answers from a list.</Field>
<Field name="TrueFalse">A boolean question with a `correctBoolean` answer.</Field>
<Field name="MatchingPairs">Match left items to right items, stored as JSON pairs.</Field>
<Field name="Numerical">Enter a number; graded against a `correctNumber` within a `tolerance`.</Field>
</FieldTable>

Questions are authored on the training detail page and can be reordered. The stored answer key columns differ by type: `options` and `correctAnswers` for the choice types, `correctBoolean` for true/false, `matchingPairs` for pairs, and `correctNumber` / `tolerance` for numerical (`20251205021915_training.sql:229`).

## Assigning training to groups

Trainings are assigned to **groups**, not individual employees. A `trainingAssignment` (`20251205021915_training.sql:93`) links one `trainingId` to an array of `groupIds`. Every employee in those groups receives the assignment.

A new assignment is **Internal** or **External**. An internal assignment picks a training from the catalog. An external assignment has only a **Name**, as below.

<Callout type="note" title="Assignments target groups, then expand to people">
The assignment stores `groupIds`, not employee ids. Carbon expands the groups to their members when it computes who owes the training, so adding a person to a group automatically enrolls them — you don't re-assign.
</Callout>

Click an assignment in the list to open its roster: each member with their current status, a completion date if they've finished, and a **"Mark Complete"** button (`apps/erp/app/routes/x+/resources+/assignments.$assignmentId.tsx`). **Edit Assignment** in the row menu changes its name or groups. Assigning an internal training also fires a notification to the newly-covered employees (see [notifications](/docs/reference/notifications)).

## External training

Some training happens outside Carbon, such as a forklift course from an outside provider. Create an **External** assignment with the course's **Name** and its groups. Carbon stores no training for it (`trainingAssignment.name`, `20261014164752_external-training-assignments.sql`), so it has no content, no quiz and no frequency. It is one-time: each employee is **"Pending"** or **"Completed"**.

- **No employee page and no notifications.** Employees are not notified or reminded, and they cannot open it.
- **Admin completes it:** on the roster, **Mark Complete** can attach the employee's certificate (one PDF, PNG or JPG of 10 MB or less). The certificate is optional. A phone photo in HEIC format is converted to JPEG on upload.
- **Proof:** a completed row with a certificate shows **View Certificate** on the roster and on the person's Trainings card.

The assignments list shows an **External** badge on these rows.

## Completion and status

Completion is recorded in `trainingCompletion` (`20251205021915_training.sql:156`) — one row per employee per assignment per period, with a `completedAt` timestamp and `completedBy`. For recurring trainings the row also carries a `period` string, and a unique constraint stops double-recording the same employee for the same period.

The **period** is the heart of the recurring logic (`20251206000000_training_assignments.sql`). Carbon derives the current period from the training's frequency:

<FieldTable>
<Field name="Once">No period (`NULL`). Complete it once and you're done for good.</Field>
<Field name="Quarterly">A string like `Q1-2026`. Completion in one quarter doesn't carry into the next.</Field>
<Field name="Annual">A string like `2026`. Resets at the start of each year.</Field>
</FieldTable>

An employee's status is *computed*, not stored — Carbon evaluates it from whether a completion exists for the current period and when the employee started. There are four possible values (`apps/erp/app/modules/resources/resources.models.ts:318`):

<StatusFlow entity="trainingAssignment">
<Status name="Pending">Assigned, in the current period, not yet completed. The default state.</Status>
<Status name="Completed" accent>A completion row exists for this employee and period.</Status>
<Status name="Overdue" branch>A recurring period has already ended and the employee never completed it.</Status>
<Status name="Not Required" terminal>The employee started after the period ended, so they were never on the hook for it.</Status>
</StatusFlow>

<Callout type="warn" title="New hires aren't retroactively overdue">
Recurring trainings check the employee's start date against the period. Someone who joined after a past period closed shows **"Not Required"** for that period, not **"Overdue"** — so onboarding a new hire doesn't instantly paint their record red.
</Callout>

A manager marks completion from the assignment's roster with **"Mark Complete"**; Carbon works out the current period on the server (`recordTrainingCompletion`, `apps/erp/app/modules/resources/resources.server.ts`). An employee already **"Completed"** or **"Not Required"** has no button — there's nothing to mark.

To undo a wrong completion, **"Remove Completion"** on the roster deletes it and the employee goes back to **"Pending"** (or **"Overdue"**). It needs the `resources_delete` permission, and it also deletes the attached certificate file. Any ability the training granted stays with the employee; remove it on their Abilities page if needed.

## The weekly reminder

Every Sunday at 21:00 UTC, a scheduled job finds outstanding training (`packages/jobs/src/inngest/functions/scheduled/weekly.ts`, cron `0 21 * * 0`). It selects **"Pending"** and **"Overdue"** assignments and sends one digest to each employee.

The notification is the `TrainingReminder` event (`training-reminder`) under the `Training` topic. Its email heading is **"Training reminder"** and the call-to-action button reads **"View training"** (`packages/notifications/src/index.ts`). It's the only *recurring* notification event in Carbon — so it needs a guard against nagging forever.

<Callout type="note" title="Reminders stop after five sends per period">
A recurring reminder is capped: once an outstanding training has had five successful reminder emails delivered for the current period, Carbon stops re-sending it. For quarterly and annual trainings the count is keyed to the period, so the cap resets when a new period begins. If a reminder "stopped coming," this cap is usually why.
</Callout>

Two one-time events announce assignments: **"Training assigned to you"** (`training-assignment`) and **"New training available"** (`resource-training-assignment`). See [notifications](/docs/reference/notifications) for delivery and preferences.

## Training can grant an ability

An **ability** is an employee's qualification for a process. A training can grant one: set **Grants Ability** in Properties, and completing the training adds that ability to the employee (`grant_ability_on_training_completion`, `packages/database/supabase/migrations/20260720121629_capacity-planning.sql:264`). The scheduler and the shop floor use abilities to decide who may run the process.

Training is one way to grant an ability; a manager can also add it by hand on the person's Abilities page. Removing a training completion does not remove the ability.

## Troubleshooting

Exact strings and preconditions for training assignment, completion, and reminders.

### "Training is required" / "Name is required"
An internal assignment needs a training, and an external one needs a name (`trainingAssignmentValidator`, `apps/erp/app/modules/resources/resources.models.ts`). The training is read-only when editing an existing assignment, so "Training is required" only bites on create.

### "At least one group is required"
An assignment must target at least one group; the `groupIds` array was empty. Add one or more groups in **"Assign to Groups"** before saving.

### "Training assignment is required" / "Employee is required"
The mark-complete action needs both `trainingAssignmentId` and `employeeId` (`apps/erp/app/modules/resources/resources.models.ts:333`). These come from the roster row; an empty value means the row didn't carry them — reload the assignment and try again from its roster.

### "Why didn't a reminder send?"
Not an error string — a common question. Checklist of preconditions for the weekly digest (`packages/jobs/src/inngest/functions/scheduled/weekly.ts`, cron `0 21 * * 0`, Sunday 21:00 UTC):
- The employee's computed status must be **"Pending"** or **"Overdue"**. **"Completed"** and **"Not Required"** are skipped.
- The reminder cap is five successful deliveries per training per period. If five have already gone out this period, sending stops until the next period (recurring) — this is the usual reason a reminder "went quiet."
- The employee must have the `Training` notification topic enabled for the channel; a user who muted training notifications won't be emailed.
- New hires who started after a past recurring period show **"Not Required"** for it and are intentionally never reminded about it.

### "Why can't I mark someone complete / assign a training?"
The action is gated by the `resources` permission — `resources_update` to mark complete and edit, `resources_create` to create a training or assignment (`apps/erp/app/modules/resources/ui/Training/TrainingAssignmentForm.tsx`). Without it the **"Mark Complete"** and **Save** buttons are disabled. (These permissions were `people_*` before migration `20251211012654_training-to-resources.sql`; the code now checks `resources`.)

### "This training is not currently active"
The employee or manager tried to complete a training that is **"Draft"** or **"Archived"**. Publish it (or **Reopen** and publish) first.

### "The certificate must be a PDF, PNG or JPG" / "The certificate must be 10 MB or smaller"
A certificate attached to an external assignment's **Mark Complete** must be of an accepted type and size (`validateTrainingCertificate`, `apps/erp/app/modules/resources/resources.models.ts`). Carbon checks the file's first bytes, so a file renamed to `.pdf` is refused. The dialog converts HEIC to JPEG.

### "This employee is not assigned this training"
**Mark Complete** was sent for a person outside the assignment's groups (`assignments.complete.tsx`). Add the person to one of its groups first.

### "The guide can only change while the training is Draft"
The PDF was changed on an Active or Archived training. **Reopen** it first.

### "A completed training re-appeared as Pending"
Expected for recurring frequencies. A **"Quarterly"** or **"Annual"** training tracks completion per period; when the new period starts (a new `Q?-YYYY` or `YYYY`), a fresh completion is required and the employee resets to **"Pending"** (`20251206000000_training_assignments.sql`). Only **"Once"** trainings stay completed forever.
</AgentContext>
