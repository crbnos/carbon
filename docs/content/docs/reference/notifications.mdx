---
title: Notifications
description: Assignments, approvals, expirations, and reminders reach the right people over in-app, email, and Slack, with per-user channel opt-outs by topic.
---

A <Term>notification</Term> tells one person that something needs their attention: a job was assigned to them, an approval is waiting, a gauge's calibration lapsed. Carbon fans each one out to up to three channels: the **in-app inbox** (the bell in the top bar), **email**, and **Slack**. In-app is always on; email and Slack are extras a user can mute per topic. One rule holds everywhere: Carbon never notifies you about your own action.

## What raises a notification

<FieldTable>
  <Field name="Assignments">Someone is put on a record (job, order, RFQ, invoice, issue, risk, procedure, picking list, stock transfer, dispatch, training). The assignee is the recipient.</Field>
  <Field name="State changes & responses">An approval was **requested**, **approved**, or **rejected**; a job **completed**; a change notice entered a new stage; a quote **expired**; a customer or supplier responded to a digital or supplier quote.</Field>
  <Field name="Expirations & reminders">Time-driven, raised by scheduled jobs: a **gauge calibration expired**, a **maintenance dispatch** was auto-created, or the weekly **training reminder** rolls up a person's outstanding trainings.</Field>
  <Field name="Workflow">A [workflow](/docs/reference/workflows) reached a **"Notify someone"** step, the only path where you compose the wording yourself. Lands under the **General** topic.</Field>
</FieldTable>

<Callout type="note">
Notifications are informational, never an approval step. Marking one read changes no record; the action lives on the underlying document, and the notification just points you there.
</Callout>

## Topics and channels

Every event maps to exactly one **topic**, the grouping key for the inbox and for muting: Approval, Job, Sales, Quote, Purchasing, Inventory, Quality, Maintenance, Training, Suggestion, Items, and General. Eleven are user-facing on the settings page; only **Items** has no toggle.

The channels differ in what they require:

<FieldTable>
  <Field name="In-app">The bell in the top bar. **Always written**, for every recipient, on every event — it cannot be turned off.</Field>
  <Field name="Email">A per-notification email with a **View** button. Sent only when the company's plan includes email notifications.</Field>
  <Field name="Slack">A DM in the connected workspace. Needs an active Slack integration *and* a matching Slack account for the recipient.</Field>
</FieldTable>

<Callout type="warn">
Email is **plan-gated**: companies without the `EMAIL_NOTIFICATIONS` plan feature skip the email channel entirely, regardless of the per-user switch. Slack has no plan gate — it only needs the integration. See [licensing](/docs/platform/licensing).
</Callout>

## Muting a topic

Each user controls their own email and Slack delivery under **Account → Notifications**: a grid with one row per topic, a column per channel, a switch in each cell. In-app has no switch.

<Screenshot label="Account → Notifications preferences grid: topic rows, Email and Slack columns, switches per cell" caption="Muting is per topic, per channel, per company. The in-app inbox always gets everything." />

The default is **on**; flipping a switch off mutes that one topic on that one channel, scoped to the current company. Muting Purchasing on Slack still leaves Purchasing emails, still leaves Slack DMs for Sales, and doesn't carry to another company you belong to. The Slack column appears only when Slack is connected; the email column always shows, with a note when the plan won't deliver it.

## Who receives an expiration or reminder

Assignments have an obvious recipient. The time-driven events resolve their own audience: a lapsed gauge calibration notifies a company-configured user list (set under [calibration](/docs/reference/calibration) settings), an auto-created dispatch notifies every employee on that work center, and the training reminder is a weekly per-person digest. There is no general "notification group" entity — only gauge-calibration expiry uses a configured list; everything else derives recipients from the record itself.

## The inbox

The bell opens the inbox: recent notifications newest-first with an unread count, each row carrying an icon, a short description, who it came from, and a timestamp. Click through to the record, or mark a row read.

## Internals and troubleshooting

Every notification starts as a `carbon/notify` event with a specific type; the full set is `NotificationEvent` (`packages/notifications/src/index.ts:7`), destinations are `NotificationDestination` (`index.ts:108`), and the fixed event→topic mapping is `getNotificationTopic` (`index.ts:114`). The topic string is stored on every row (`notification.topic`) and is what a user's opt-out row matches on. Preferences are opt-out rows, one per `(user, company, channel, topic)` in `notificationPreference`; no row means enabled. The fan-out always adds the in-app destination (`notify.ts:210`), drops muted recipients from email/Slack lists (`notify.ts:302`), and skips email entirely without the `EMAIL_NOTIFICATIONS` plan feature (`notify.ts:509`). Most assignments default to email + Slack; callers can override channels per dispatch.

### Why am I not getting email notifications?
Email is plan-gated — check the company's plan first. Then confirm the topic isn't muted for email and that the specific event defaults to the email channel.

### Why am I not getting Slack DMs?
Slack needs an active integration and a matching Slack account for the recipient; users without one are silently skipped. Also check the topic's Slack mute.

### I muted a topic but still get in-app notifications
Expected: in-app is always written and cannot be turned off. Mutes affect only email and Slack.

### I turned a channel on but it still doesn't deliver
On just removes the mute; it doesn't override the channel's preconditions (plan feature for email, integration + account for Slack).

### I did something myself and got no notification
The sender is filtered out of the recipient list. Self-assignments notify nobody.

### A recurring reminder stopped arriving
Repeating email notifications for the same document stop after a few successful deliveries (`MAX_NOTIFICATION_DELIVERIES`, `index.ts:90`); only the weekly training reminder is recurring. The in-app row stands until read.
</AgentContext>
