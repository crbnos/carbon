title: Notifications
description: Assignments, approvals, expirations, and reminders reach the right people over in-app, email, Slack, and browser push, with per-user opt-outs by topic for email and Slack.
---

A <Term>notification</Term> alerts one person to an assignment, approval, expiration, or other event. Carbon can deliver it through the **in-app inbox** (the bell in the top bar), **email**, **Slack**, and **browser push**. In-app delivery is always enabled, and browser push mirrors it in every browser where it is enabled; users can mute email and Slack by topic. Carbon does not notify users about their own actions.

## What raises a notification

<FieldTable>
  <Field name="Assignments">Someone is put on a record (job, order, RFQ, invoice, issue, risk, procedure, picking list, stock transfer, dispatch, training). The assignee is the recipient.</Field>
  <Field name="State changes & responses">An approval was **requested**, **approved**, or **rejected**; a job **completed**; a change notice entered a new stage; a quote **expired**; a customer or supplier responded to a digital or supplier quote.</Field>
  <Field name="Expirations & reminders">Time-driven, raised by scheduled jobs: a **gauge calibration expired**, a **maintenance dispatch** was auto-created, or the weekly **training reminder** rolls up a person's outstanding trainings.</Field>
  <Field name="Workflow">A [workflow](/docs/reference/workflows) reached a **"Notify someone"** step. This is the only notification with user-defined wording, and it uses the **General** topic.</Field>
</FieldTable>

<Callout type="note">
Notifications are informational. Marking one as read does not change the underlying record or complete an approval.
</Callout>

## Topics and channels

Every event maps to one **topic**, which controls inbox grouping and channel preferences: Approval, Job, Sales, Quote, Purchasing, Inventory, Quality, Maintenance, Training, Suggestion, Items, or General. The settings page exposes eleven topics; **Items** has no toggle.

The channels differ in what they require:

<FieldTable>
  <Field name="In-app">The bell in the top bar. **Always written**, for every recipient, on every event — it cannot be turned off.</Field>
  <Field name="Email">A per-notification email with a **View** button. Sent only when the company's plan includes email notifications.</Field>
  <Field name="Slack">A DM in the connected workspace. Needs an active Slack integration *and* a matching Slack account for the recipient.</Field>
  <Field name="Browser">An operating-system notification on each browser where the user enabled browser notifications, even when Carbon is closed. Clicking it opens the record. Sent for every notification, like the in-app inbox: browser push has no per-topic switch. Needs no setup: the Web Push keys are derived from the deployment's `SESSION_SECRET`.</Field>
</FieldTable>

<Callout type="warn">
Email is **plan-gated**: companies without the `EMAIL_NOTIFICATIONS` plan feature skip the email channel entirely, regardless of the per-user switch. Slack and browser push have no plan gate. See [licensing](/docs/platform/licensing).
</Callout>

## Muting a topic

Each user controls their own email and Slack delivery under **Account → Notifications**: a grid with one row per topic, a column per channel, a switch in each cell. In-app and browser push have no switch.

<Screenshot label="Account → Notifications preferences grid: topic rows, Email and Slack columns, switches per cell" caption="Muting is per topic, per channel, per company. The in-app inbox always gets everything." />

Each channel is enabled by default. Turning off a switch mutes only that topic and channel for the current company. For example, muting Purchasing in Slack does not mute Purchasing email, other Slack topics, or notifications in another company. The Slack column appears only when Slack is connected. The email column always appears and indicates when the plan does not support delivery.

## Browser notifications

Above the grid, the **Browser notifications** card turns them on for the browser you are using. Click **Enable** and allow the browser's prompt. The bell also offers **Enable browser notifications** at the top of the inbox until you enable them or choose **Not now** (it asks once more after 30 days, then stops). Browser notifications are a setting of the browser: once enabled on a computer or phone, whoever signs in there gets their own notifications, from every company they belong to. Signing out — or being signed out — stops the previous user's notifications at once, so a shared computer never shows someone else's. **Disable** turns them off for that browser, for everyone who uses it. On iPhone or iPad, add Carbon to the Home Screen first — Safari offers push only to Home Screen apps.

## Who receives an expiration or reminder

Assignment events notify the assignee. Time-driven events derive recipients as follows: an expired gauge calibration notifies the user list in [calibration](/docs/reference/calibration) settings, an automatically created dispatch notifies all employees at its work center, and the weekly training reminder sends each person a digest. Carbon has no general notification-group entity; only calibration expiration uses a configured list.

## The inbox

The bell opens the inbox: recent notifications newest-first with an unread count, each row carrying an icon, a short description, who it came from, and a timestamp. Click through to the record, or mark a row read.

## Internals and troubleshooting

Every notification starts as a `carbon/notify` event with a specific type; the full set is `NotificationEvent`, destinations are `NotificationDestination`, and the fixed event→topic mapping is `getNotificationTopic` (all in `packages/notifications/src/index.ts`). The topic string is stored on every row (`notification.topic`) and is what a user's opt-out row matches on. Preferences are opt-out rows, one per `(user, company, channel, topic)` in `notificationPreference`; no row means enabled. The fan-out (`packages/jobs/src/inngest/functions/notifications/notify.ts`) always adds the in-app destination, drops muted recipients from the email and Slack lists (step `filter-recipients-by-preference`), and skips email entirely without the `EMAIL_NOTIFICATIONS` plan feature. Browser push mirrors in-app: every recipient of the in-app row gets one `carbon/send-push` per browser where notifications are enabled, with no topic filter. Most assignments default to email + Slack; callers can override channels per dispatch.

### Why am I not getting email notifications?
Email is plan-gated — check the company's plan first. Then confirm the topic isn't muted for email and that the specific event defaults to the email channel.

### Why am I not getting Slack DMs?
Slack needs an active integration and a matching Slack account for the recipient; users without one are silently skipped. Also check the topic's Slack mute.

### I muted a topic but still get in-app notifications
Expected: in-app is always written and cannot be turned off, and browser push follows it. Mutes affect only email and Slack.

### I turned a channel on but it still doesn't deliver
Enabling a channel removes the mute but does not bypass its prerequisites: the plan feature for email, the integration and account mapping for Slack. Browser push has no mute; it needs browser notifications enabled in that browser.

### I enabled browser notifications but nothing arrives
Carbon does not notify you about your own actions, so a job you assign to yourself sends nothing. Test with a second account. If the card says notifications are blocked, allow them for Carbon in the browser's site settings. If Carbon sends a notification but no banner appears, the operating system is holding it: on macOS, open **System Settings → Notifications**, set your browser's alert style to **Banners** or **Alerts**, and turn off any Focus mode (Do Not Disturb, Work). Held notifications still wait in Notification Center.

### I did something myself and got no notification
The sender is filtered out of the recipient list. Self-assignments notify nobody.

### A recurring reminder stopped arriving
Repeating email notifications for the same document stop after a few successful deliveries (`MAX_NOTIFICATION_DELIVERIES`, `index.ts:90`); only the weekly training reminder is recurring. The in-app row stands until read.
</AgentContext>
