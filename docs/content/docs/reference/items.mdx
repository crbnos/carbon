---
title: Items
description: "The master record for everything you make, buy, or stock: parts, materials, tools, and more."
---

An **item** is the master record for a finished part, raw material, tool, consumable, fixture, or service. Orders, <Term id="method">methods</Term>, <Term id="job">jobs</Term>, and inventory reference it. The **part number** is its readable ID; Carbon uses a separate internal key.

Item type, replenishment system, and tracking type determine whether the item is made or purchased, whether it contributes to stock, and whether units require <Term id="serial">serial</Term> or batch identifiers.

## Fields

<FieldTable>
  <Field name="Part number" type="text">The readable id; with a non-zero revision it reads as `id.rev`.</Field>
  <Field name="Type" type="enum">*Part*, *Material*, *Tool*, *Consumable*, *Service*, or *Fixture*.</Field>
  <Field name="Replenishment system" type="enum">How it's sourced overall: *Buy*, *Make*, or *Buy and Make*.</Field>
  <Field name="Default method type" type="enum">The default per-line fulfillment: *Purchase to Order*, *Pull from Inventory*, or *Make to Order*.</Field>
  <Field name="Tracking type" type="enum">*Inventory*, *Non-Inventory*, *Serial*, or *Batch*.</Field>
  <Field name="Unit of measure" type="reference">The stock unit.</Field>
  <Field name="Active" type="boolean">Whether it can be used on new documents.</Field>
</FieldTable>

<Callout type="note">
**<Term>Replenishment system</Term>** is the high-level strategy; **<Term id="method-type">default method type</Term>** is the line-level default it allows. A *Buy* item defaults to purchasing; a *Make* item can be built to order or pulled from stock. The two are related but distinct.
</Callout>

## Tracking

The tracking type sets how units are counted. *Inventory* items are quantity-tracked stock; *Non-Inventory* items aren't counted; *Serial* items carry one record per unit; *<Term id="batch">Batch</Term>* items are lot-tracked with their own batch numbers and dates.

## Revisions

Each part revision is a separate item record with the same part number and a different revision. Creating a revision preserves the item used by existing jobs.

<Callout type="info">
An item's <Term>costing method</Term>, one of *Standard*, *Average*, *FIFO*, or *LIFO*, lives on its cost record, not the item itself, and decides how consumption is valued.
</Callout>
