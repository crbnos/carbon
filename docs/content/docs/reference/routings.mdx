---
title: Routings
description: The ordered operations and work centers a part flows through to get made.
---

A **routing** defines the ordered <Term id="operation">operations</Term> required to make a part and the <Term>work center</Term> assigned to each operation. A <Term id="job">job</Term> needs a routing for scheduling, run-time estimation, and costing.

## Fields

<FieldTable>
  <Field name="Operation" type="text">The step performed (its description, e.g. *Cut*, *Weld*, *Inspect*).</Field>
  <Field name="Work center" type="reference">Where the operation runs; supplies the rates used for scheduling.</Field>
  <Field name="Setup time" type="time">Fixed time to prepare the operation, independent of quantity.</Field>
  <Field name="Labor time" type="time/unit">Operator time per unit, in the operation's standard factor unit.</Field>
  <Field name="Machine time" type="time/unit">Machine time per unit, in the operation's standard factor unit.</Field>
  <Field name="Sequence" type="number">Order of operations; lower runs first.</Field>
</FieldTable>

## Per-revision routings

Routings are defined per item revision, so a design change doesn't rewrite history. Jobs built against
an older revision keep the routing they were released with.

<Callout type="info">
A routing with no operations can't be scheduled — the job will sit in **Planned** until at least one
operation exists.
</Callout>
