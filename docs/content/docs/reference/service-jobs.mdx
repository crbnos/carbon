A <Term>service job</Term> is the work on one customer unit, such as a returned drive at your repair bench or a site visit to a running machine. It holds the problem, one <Term id="service-coverage">coverage</Term>, the technicians' time, the parts and charges, and for billable work an estimate.

Open **Service → Jobs** in the ERP. The Service module also has **Schedule** (the dispatch grid) and **Customer Assets** (the [installed base](/docs/reference/customer-assets)). On the shop floor, technicians use **Service** in the [MES](/docs/reference/mes).

## Where a job comes from

| Origin | How | The new job |
|---|---|---|
| An [RMA](/docs/reference/rmas) line | Post the receipt of a **Return for Service** or **Return for Warranty** line | Open, at the receiving location, work site Depot. Billable for Service, Warranty for Warranty |
| The office | **Service → Jobs → New**, the **New Service Job** drawer | Open. The coverage you choose, or a suggestion from the asset |
| The shop floor or a site | MES **Service → New Job**: scan or pick a serial or a customer, type the problem | In Progress, assigned to you, with your clock running |

When you pick a **Customer Asset**, the job takes the asset's item and serial. If you leave **Coverage** empty, Carbon suggests Warranty while the asset's labor or parts warranty runs today, else Billable.

## Status

<StatusFlow entity="serviceJob">
  <Status name="Open">Waiting for a technician. **Start** moves it on; so does the first MES clock.</Status>
  <Status name="Awaiting Approval" branch>The estimate was sent. **Record Response** moves it to Open when the customer accepts, or to In Progress, marked not repairable, when they decline.</Status>
  <Status name="In Progress">Work is underway. Time and parts can change freely.</Status>
  <Status name="On Hold" branch>Parked with **Hold**. **Resume** returns it to In Progress.</Status>
  <Status name="Completed" accent>**Complete** posted the job's time. **Reopen** reverses that posting and returns the job to In Progress.</Status>
  <Status name="Invoiced">**Create Invoice** drafted the customer invoice. An invoiced job cannot reopen.</Status>
  <Status name="Cancelled" terminal>Closed unworked. **Cancel Job** is refused while the job has time entries or issued parts.</Status>
</StatusFlow>

A Completed, Invoiced or Cancelled job is locked: its time, parts, charges, estimate and fields no longer change.

The header shows one primary action, chosen by status: **Start**, **Send Estimate** or **Complete**, **Record Response**, then **Create Invoice** for a Billable job. Hold, Resume, Reopen, Print Estimate and Cancel Job are in the ⋯ menu.

## Coverage and cost

Coverage decides where the job's cost goes. Carbon posts the cost as the work happens: parts when they are issued, time when the job completes. The customer owns the unit, so nothing goes to work in progress.

| Coverage | Cost account | Invoiced? |
|---|---|---|
| **Billable** | Cost of Service, else Cost of Goods Sold | Yes |
| **Warranty** | Warranty Expense | No |
| **Contract** | Cost of Service, else Cost of Goods Sold | No |

Set **Cost of Service** and **Warranty Expense** in **Accounting → Default Accounts**. Without Warranty Expense, every Warranty posting is refused.

<Callout type="warn" title="Changing coverage moves the cost already booked.">
Change **Coverage** in the properties panel while the job is open. Carbon posts one journal that moves the cost of the parts already issued from the old account to the new one. Time has not posted yet, so it simply follows the new coverage at completion.
</Callout>

## Estimates

A Billable job can carry an estimate: labor lines (quantity in hours), parts and charges, each at a unit price. **Send Estimate** marks it Sent and the job Awaiting Approval; **Print Estimate** opens the PDF. While the estimate is Sent or Accepted, its lines are locked.

**Record Response** stores the customer's answer and the name of whoever gave it. On a Fixed Price job, acceptance sets the job's price to the estimate total.

<Callout type="info" title="Some jobs cannot complete without an accepted estimate.">
A Billable job needs an accepted estimate before **Complete** when it is Fixed Price or came from an RMA line. A job marked not repairable is the exception: it completes without one, so you can bill the evaluation time. Warranty and Contract jobs never need an estimate.
</Callout>

## Time

Every time entry names a **labor item**: a Service item whose unit cost is the cost per hour and whose sale price is the bill rate. Set the default in **Settings → Invoicing → Service Labor Item**; the MES clock and My Time use it.

- **On the job page**, add an entry under **Time** with the employee, labor item, date, hours and **Billable**.
- **In the MES**, tap **Start** on the job. **Stop** records the hours. You can run one clock at a time; a second **Start** is refused until you stop the first.
- **On My Time**, each service job you work on or are scheduled on has its own row below your [charge numbers](/docs/reference/projects#time). Drag a day's bar to set your total hours for that job. Service rows never go through timesheet submit or approval, and they lock when the job completes.

Time posts once, when the job completes: one journal that debits the coverage account and credits labor absorption, at hours × each labor item's cost. **Complete** is refused while a clock runs or a labor item has no cost.

## Parts and charges

**Issue Part** takes stock from the job's location. Its cost posts to the coverage account at once, because the part has left the shelf. **Return to Stock** on a part line puts everything still issued back and reverses its cost. In the MES, technicians add parts from the job page.

**Add Charge** adds a Service item at a price, such as a trip fee. A charge that was a real cost, like a hotel night, can name the line that paid it in **Paid By**: a line of a posted reimbursement or of a posted supplier invoice. The charge then shows that line's cost, and the job posts nothing for it, because the linked document already did.

## Invoicing

**Create Invoice** drafts a sales [invoice](/docs/reference/invoices) for a Completed Billable job and moves the job to Invoiced.

- **Time and Materials** bills the billable hours of each labor item at its sale price, then each billable part and charge.
- **Fixed Price** bills one line: the job's **Billed Item** at the accepted estimate total.

Posting the invoice records revenue only. The parts already left inventory when they were issued.

## The dispatch schedule

**Service → Schedule** shows a week for one location: technicians as rows, days as columns. Each cell lists the day's jobs with their planned hours and a total against the person's shift. A day marked absent is greyed out.

The **Backlog** panel lists the location's open jobs that nobody is scheduled on. Drag a job onto a cell to schedule it, drag a block to another cell to move it, or drag it back to the backlog to unschedule it. The department filter brings that department's people to the top.

## Troubleshooting

### "This job is closed. A Completed, Invoiced or Cancelled job cannot change."
The job is locked. Reopen a Completed job first; an Invoiced or Cancelled job cannot change.

### "This job needs an accepted estimate before it can be completed."
The job is Billable and Fixed Price, or came from an RMA line, and its estimate is not Accepted. Send the estimate and record the customer's response, or set Repairable to No.

### "The labor item … has no cost per hour. Set its unit cost first."
A time entry's labor item has no unit cost. Set the item's cost, then complete again.

### "Stop the running clock first."
A technician's MES clock still runs on the job.

### "Set the Warranty Expense account in Accounting → Default Accounts."
A Warranty job posted a part, its completion, or a coverage change with no Warranty Expense default.

### "A job with time entries or issued parts cannot be cancelled. Remove them first."
Delete the time entries and return the parts to stock, then cancel.

### "Your clock is running on … Stop it first." / "Your clock is already running on another job. Stop it first."
An employee can run one clock at a time (`serviceJobTime_open_clock_key`).

### "Set the default labor item in the ERP's invoicing settings before you start a clock" / "Set the service labor item in Settings → Invoicing first"
`companySettings.serviceLaborItemId` is empty.

### "This day has clock time on the job. Edit it on the job."
On My Time, the new total is below the day's clock hours. Clock entries are edited on the job page.

### "You can log time only on a job you are assigned to"
On My Time, a user without `service` update permission can write only to a job they lead or are scheduled on.

### "The labor item … has no sale price per hour. Set its unit sale price first."
Create Invoice found billable hours on a labor item with no sale price.

### "An invoiced job cannot be reopened."
Voiding the invoice does not move the job back from Invoiced.

### "This line already pays a charge on …"
A reimbursement or supplier invoice line pays one charge only.
