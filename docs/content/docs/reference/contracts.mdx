A **<Term>contract</Term>** bills a customer for services on a schedule: one-time fees such as implementation, and recurring charges such as a subscription or a support plan. Carbon plans the invoices and the monthly revenue from the contract's lines, drafts each invoice on its date, and recognizes the revenue month by month.

Contracts live under **Sales → Service Contracts**. Start one with **"Add Contract"** on the list, or with **"Create Contract"** in a sales order's menu ([from a sales order](#from-a-sales-order)).

## Setting up a contract

A new contract opens a five-step setup. **"Next"** on the first step saves it as a draft, and later steps save as you edit. **"Continue Setup"** on a draft's page reopens it.

<Steps>
<Step>
**Details.** The customer, name, contract type, currency, and the term: **"Start Date"**, **"Duration"** and **"Action on Completion"** ([renewal](#renewal)). Under **More Details**, **"Contract Close Date"** records when the deal was won, today by default.
</Step>
<Step>
**Products.** **"Add Products"** adds Service items as recurring lines at a rate of zero. Click a cell to set the **"Revenue Type"**, quantity, rate, discount and dates ([lines](#lines)).
</Step>
<Step>
**Invoicing.** **"Bill To"**, **"Invoice Contact"**, **"Invoice Address"**, **"Ship To"** and **"Payment Terms"**, which start from the customer's settings, then the [invoice schedule](#the-invoice-schedule).
</Step>
<Step>
**Revenue.** How each line earns its revenue, and the monthly [revenue plan](#revenue).
</Step>
<Step>
**Review.** A summary, and a linked list of anything that blocks **"Confirm"**.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Lifecycle

<StatusFlow entity="customerContract">
  <Status name="Draft">Terms, lines, invoices and the revenue plan are all editable. Nothing is invoiced.</Status>
  <Status name="Active" accent>Confirmed: the invoice schedule is fixed, invoices are drafted as they fall due, and revenue is recognized monthly. Changes go through Amend and Cancel.</Status>
  <Status name="Ended">The end date has passed and every planned invoice has been drafted. Read-only.</Status>
</StatusFlow>

**"Confirm"** checks that the contract has lines, that each is a Service item, and that each recurring line has a rate unit. Each line's invoices must add up to its total, and its revenue must equal what it bills. After that, terms and lines change only through [Amend](#amendments); only **"Contract Type"**, **"Invoicing"** and **"Notes"** stay editable. **"Delete Contract"** is for drafts only.

The daily billing job sets a contract to Ended once its end date has passed and nothing is left to draft. An open-ended contract runs until you cancel it.

## Lines

Each line is a **Service** item, **One-time** or **Recurring**, with a quantity, rate, discount, and its own start and end dates (an empty end runs to the contract's end). **"Edit Details"** opens the full line form.

- **One-time** lines bill their whole amount on the first invoice dated on or after their start date.
- **Recurring** lines bill every billing period at the rate **"Per"** Day, Week, Month, Quarter or Year. The rate unit is independent of how often you invoice: a rate per Day can be invoiced monthly.
- **"Discount Ends On"** ends a recurring line's discount, such as 20% off for the first year. Confirming turns it into an amendment that starts a full-price copy of the line the next day.
- **"Project"** defaults from the contract. It is written as the Project [dimension](/docs/reference/dimensions) on the line's revenue.

## The invoice schedule

The contract's invoicing terms cut every recurring line into billing periods:

- **"Frequency"**: Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly or Yearly.
- **"Align to calendar periods"**: off, periods count from the start date. On, they align to the week (from Monday), month, quarter or year, with a shorter first period.
- **"Billing Timing"**: *Advance* invoices a period on its first day, *Arrears* on its last.
- **"First Invoice"**: any invoice that would fall before this date moves onto it.

A period is priced in whole rate units, prorated by day. Months count from the period's start, so a period from 15 March to 14 April is exactly one month, and a calendar period from 15 to 30 November is 16 of 30 days. A rate of $10 per Day bills $310 for a 31-day month. Weekly invoices at a monthly, quarterly or yearly rate are the exception: each day is the yearly average, so every week bills the same amount ($3,000 per Month is $690.41 a week) and a 365-day year bills exactly twelve months.

Take a contract starting 1 November, invoiced Monthly in Advance: implementation $60,000 one-time, 10 seats at $40 per Month with 20% off, and support at $1,200 per Year. The 1 November invoice is $60,420 (60,000 + 320 + 100), and every later month is $420.

While the contract is a draft, the schedule is a grid with one row per invoice and one column per line. Change a date or an amount in place, **"Add Invoice"**, or **"Delete Invoice"** from a row's menu.

<Callout type="note" title="Editing never changes what a line bills in total">
Edits only move money between invoices. Three $20,000 invoices for the $60,000 fee are fine; invoices that bill $50,000 of it leave $10,000 in the **"Left to invoice"** row, and **"Confirm"** waits until it is placed.
</Callout>

Once edited, the schedule stops following the lines: a line you change afterwards leaves an amount to invoice. **"Reset Schedule"** plans it again, discarding your edits.

A fixed-term contract is planned to its end date, an open-ended one through the next billing period, rolled forward daily. **<Term id="billed-through">"Billed Through"</Term>** (under **Advanced** → **"Migrating from another system?"**) is for a contract moved into Carbon mid-term: periods ending on or before it read **Billed Externally** and are never invoiced.

## Invoicing

A daily job drafts every planned invoice dated on or before today as one sales invoice per contract, and **"Invoice"** on an active contract does the same on demand. Each line shows its billing period and the contract line's quantity, list price and discount.

What happens next follows the contract's **"Invoicing"** property, or the company default on **Settings → Invoicing** (**"Recurring Invoices"**):

- **Draft only**: the invoice waits for a person to post it.
- **Post**: posted automatically.
- **Post and email**: posted and emailed to the **"Invoice Contact"** with the PDF attached.
- **Post and send via Stripe**: posted and sent through your connected Stripe account with a payment link. The billing customer must be linked to a Stripe customer; when it is not, **"Confirm"** offers an existing Stripe customer with the same email or a new one, and needs permission to create invoices.

An invoice that credits a contract change or re-bills a voided invoice is held for review instead, and so is one with a sales-rule violation or a posting error. Held invoices carry a yellow **Needs Review** badge in the **Sales Invoices** list, which can be filtered on **Needs Review**, and the contract's salesperson (or its creator) gets a daily summary.

Voiding a posted contract invoice returns its periods to the schedule, and the next run bills them again on a held invoice. Deleting a draft contract invoice releases its periods too. Each drafted invoice carries the contract's **"Ship To"** address as its own ship-to.

## Revenue

Each line earns its revenue from its **"Go-Live"** date (else **"Revenue Start"**, else its start) to its **"Revenue End"** (else its end), by one of two <Term id="revenue-method">revenue methods</Term>: **Daily**, the same amount every day, or **Even Period**, the same amount every full calendar month with partial months prorated by days.

The **revenue plan** spreads what each line bills over those dates, one amount per calendar month. While the contract is a draft it is a grid with one row per month and one column per line: change an amount, **"Add Month"**, or **"Delete Month"**. Each line's revenue must equal what its invoices bill, so **"Confirm"** waits for an empty **"Left to recognize"** row.

<Callout type="note" title="An edited revenue plan stops following the invoices">
Until you edit it, the plan follows the lines and the invoice schedule. After your first edit, a later change to a line or an invoice leaves an amount to recognize. **"Reset Revenue"** plans it again.
</Callout>

**"Recognize Revenue From"** is for a contract moved into Carbon: earlier months read **Recognized externally**, and each line opens with what was billed less what was recognized elsewhere. Your own opening journal carries that balance.

### How revenue posts

Revenue follows the line, not the invoice. Each line keeps a balance of what it has invoiced less what it has recognized.

- **Billed ahead**: the invoice posts to <Term id="deferred-revenue">Deferred Revenue</Term>, not to Sales.
- **Earned first**: a month recognized before it is billed builds up <Term id="contract-asset">Contract Assets</Term>. The invoice that bills it clears Contract Assets first and defers only the rest.
- **The monthly run**: the **Revenue Recognition** run (**Accounting → Revenue Recognition**) recognizes every planned month that has started by its period end, out of Deferred Revenue as far as it covers and into Contract Assets for the rest.

A run proposed before a contract month fell due must be recalculated before it posts. In a foreign currency, the rate difference on a cleared balance posts to realized exchange gain or loss, never to revenue. Amendments, cancellation and renewal plan the revenue again from their date; a month already recognized never changes, and the difference lands on the next planned month.

## Amendments

**"Amend"** on an active contract changes, adds, or ends lines from a **"Change Date"**, with a **"Reason"**. **"Takes Effect"** decides how:

- **From the change date**: the old line ends the day before and its replacement starts on the date, prorated by day.
- **From the next billing period**: the change starts with the next billing period, so nothing is prorated.

Each change is an <Term>amendment</Term>. A period already invoiced past the change gets an **adjustment** on the next invoice: the billed amount times the days after the change, divided by the days billed. Raising the example's 10 seats to 15 on 12 March puts a credit of $206.45 and a charge of $309.68 on the 1 April invoice, for March's last 20 days.

The modal previews the adjustments and next invoices before you save. It suggests the **"Contract Type"** from the change in recurring value: up is *Expansion*, down is *Contraction*. The **Amendments** section lists every change, newest first.

## Cancellation

**"Cancel"** picks an **"End Date"**, by default the end of the current billing period, so nothing already invoiced is credited. Open lines end on that date, later planned invoices are removed, and the contract no longer renews.

When the date falls inside a period invoiced in advance, **"Credit unused time"** drafts a credit memo for the days after it. Posting it takes the credit off Deferred Revenue, and off Contract Assets for any part already recognized. It cannot be voided.

A month recognized after the new end date is reversed by a negative month in the next run. **"Revert Cancellation"** restores the previous end dates and renewal, until the end date passes and while that credit memo is still a draft.

## Renewal

**"Duration"** sets the term: 6 months, 1, 2 or 3 years, a custom end date, or open-ended. With **"Action on Completion"** set to **Renew**, the daily job extends the contract by one term on its end date, raising each continuing recurring line's rate by **"Renewal Uplift (%)"**, and records an *Existing* amendment.

## Contract types

Every contract and amendment carries a <Term>contract type</Term>: **New Sales**, **Existing**, **Expansion**, **Reactivation** or **Contraction**. A new contract is suggested as *Reactivation* when all the customer's earlier contracts have ended, otherwise *New Sales*.

## From a sales order

**"Create Contract"** moves chosen **Service** lines that are not yet invoiced into a new draft contract, copying the customer, bill-to, payment terms, currency and PO number. Each line starts as One-time; switch it to Recurring and choose its rate unit. It is unavailable on a cancelled or closed order.

The order counts those lines as invoiced, so invoicing the order bills only the rest. Deleting the draft contract, or one of its lines, hands them back.

## Troubleshooting

### "Add at least one line before confirming" / "LINE: only Service items can be on a contract" / "LINE: a recurring line needs a rate unit"
Confirm (`post-customer-contract` `confirm`) refuses a contract with no lines, a line whose item is not a Service item, or a Recurring line without a rate unit. Several problems are reported in one message, joined with "; ".

### "The edited invoice schedule no longer bills each line's total (LINE: AMOUNT not on any invoice). Fix the edits or reset the schedule."
A line changed after the schedule was edited, or a grid edit left an amount unplaced. The invoice grid shows the same amounts in its "Left to invoice" footer row, and the Review step lists them; **Reset Schedule** plans it again from the lines.

### "The billing customer is not linked to a Stripe customer. Link them before confirming a contract that sends its invoices via Stripe."
Confirm with the effective invoicing mode *Post and Send via Stripe* needs an `externalIntegrationMapping` row (`customer`, `stripe-connect`) for the bill-to customer (`invoiceCustomerId`, else `customerId`). The confirm modal creates it: for an unlinked bill-to it loads `api/stripe-connect/customer/by-customer/:customerId` (with the contract's `invoiceCustomerContactId` as the contact) and posts the choice (`stripeCustomerAction` `use-linked` / `link-existing` / `create`, `stripeCustomerId`, `stripeContactEmail`) with Confirm. The `confirm` route runs `linkStripeCustomerForBilling` (the same step the invoice post uses) before the server function, which then re-checks the mapping. A failed link is flashed as "Contract not confirmed — REASON" and the contract stays Draft; a link that succeeded is kept even when the confirm itself then fails. When the modal cannot offer a choice (Stripe Connect is not connected, or the customer has no email and none is entered) Confirm stays disabled; any other caller without a link gets this message from the server function.

### "Contract CON… is STATUS; only a Draft contract can be confirmed|edited|reset" / "… only an Active contract can be amended|cancelled|reverted"
The action does not apply in the contract's status. Draft: Confirm, schedule edits and revenue edits. Active: Amend, Cancel, Revert Cancellation, Invoice.

### "Only a Draft contract can be edited — use Amend" / "Lines can only be added or changed on a Draft contract — use Amend" / "Lines can only be removed from a Draft contract — use Amend" / "Only a Draft contract's terms can be edited — use Amend"
Service-level guards (code `CONTRACT_NOT_DRAFT`; these writers are also MCP tools). On an Active contract only Contract Type, Invoicing and Notes can change directly.

### "A contract line must be a Service item" / "This line does not belong to this contract"
`upsertContractLine` guards (`CONTRACT_LINE_NOT_SERVICE`, `CONTRACT_LINE_WRONG_CONTRACT`).

### "Add an invoice contact with an email to send invoices"
Setting Invoicing to *Post and Email* in the properties panel needs an invoice contact with an email (`CONTRACT_INVOICE_EMAIL_NO_CONTACT`).

### "Only a Draft contract can be deleted. Cancel it instead."
Delete is Draft-only.

### "The DATE invoice is STATUS; only a Planned invoice can be edited" / "A credited row cannot be edited" / "Installments total X; the line must still total Y" / "Choose a different invoice to merge into"
Schedule edits apply to Planned invoices of a Draft only, and a split must add up to the row's amount.

### "LINE is changed twice" / "LINE already ended on DATE" / "LINE is a one-time line that has been invoiced; it cannot be changed"
Amend refusals. A one-time line is changed or ended as a whole, so once invoiced it cannot change.

### "The contract already ends on DATE; choose an earlier end date" / "The end date can be at most one day before the contract starts (DATE)" / "Invoices exist — cancel on or after DATE"
Cancel refusals. An end date the day before the start cancels the contract back to nothing and sets it to Ended at once; that needs no invoiced period.

### "The cancellation has taken effect; it can no longer be reverted" / "The credit memo has been posted" / "Contract CON… is not cancelled"
Revert Cancellation works only while today is on or before the cancelled end date and the cancellation's credit memo is still Draft.

### "A contract cancellation credit cannot be voided"
`post-memo` refuses to void any memo with `customerContractId`.

### "Includes a credit for a contract change" / "Re-billing INV…, which was voided"
Automation hold reasons on a drafted contract invoice (`automationHoldReason`). Review and post it by hand. Under *Draft Only* nothing is held, since nothing is automated.

### "Stripe is not connected" / "No Stripe customer is linked"
`sendError` stamped by the *Post and Send via Stripe* send; the invoice stays posted and carries the Needs Review badge.

### "Contract invoices need the Deferred Revenue account mapped in the accounting defaults" / "Contract invoices need the Contract Assets account mapped in the accounting defaults" / "Contract invoices need the Sales account mapped in the accounting defaults"
`post-sales-invoice` posts a line with `customerContractLineId` to the line's position, not to Sales or a Service deferral. The three account defaults must be active leaves of the right class ("… account is invalid; expected an active CLASS leaf in this company group" otherwise).

### "Map the realized exchange gain|loss account (an active Revenue|Expense leaf) in the accounting defaults; this contract invoice clears a balance carried at another rate"
A foreign-currency contract invoice cleared a pool carried at a different base rate, so the difference is realized FX. Needed only when the difference is non-zero.

### "A contract line's revenue position changed while this invoice was posting; post it again"
Another writer (a recognition run, a credit memo, another invoice) moved the line's position between the read and the posting transaction. Posting again recomputes it.

### "Voiding a contract invoice needs the Deferred Revenue and Contract Assets accounts mapped in the accounting defaults"
The VOID negates the invoice's ledger movement and, when a pool would go negative (part was already recognized), reclasses it between the two accounts.

### "Contract credit memos need the ACCOUNT account mapped in the accounting defaults" / "The contract lines this memo credits add up to more than the memo amount"
`post-memo` refusals for a memo with `customerContractId`: it debits Deferred Revenue, then Contract Assets, then Sales for any uncovered part, plus realized FX.

### "Set the Deferred Revenue, Contract Assets and Sales account defaults before recognizing contract revenue"
`synthesizeContractRevenue` (revenue recognition run proposal or recalculation) found a due contract month but the account defaults are missing.

### "Revenue recognition run RUN is out of date with the revenue schedule; recalculate it before posting"
Among other causes: a contract month that started on or before the run's period end has no schedule row yet (a contract confirmed after the run was proposed), or a held contract row's plan month was replaced by an amendment. **Recalculate** the run.

### "The revenue plan no longer recognizes each line's billed total (LINE: AMOUNT not in any month). Fix the edits or reset the revenue plan."
Confirm refuses an edited revenue plan whose line totals no longer equal what the invoice schedule bills (details `revenueResiduals`). The Revenue step and the Review step show the same amounts as "left to recognize"; **Reset Revenue** plans it again.

### "Use the first day of a month (YYYY-MM-01)" / "Enter an amount for at least one line" / "Amount cannot be negative"
Revenue and invoice grid edits: a revenue row is a calendar month; Add Invoice and Add Month need an amount above zero; the grids accept no negative amounts.

### "Set the contract's start date first"
Add Products on a contract with no start date.

### "Cannot create a contract from a cancelled|closed sales order" / "Only Service lines can move to a contract" / "A line that is already invoiced cannot move to a contract"
Create Contract from a sales order refusals. Eligible lines are Service lines with nothing invoiced.
