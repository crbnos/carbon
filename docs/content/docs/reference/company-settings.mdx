Carbon scopes data and configuration to a **company**. The `company` record stores identity, address, currency, and logos. Its paired `companySettings` record stores feature flags and defaults. The app groups settings under **Company**, **Modules**, and **System**. Settings routes require employee access. **Billing** also requires company ownership in Carbon Cloud; **Companies** and **Backups** are internal-only.

A user can belong to multiple companies and switch between them with the company picker. Each company has independent settings, currency, and numbering. A company with a `parentCompanyId` is a subsidiary; see [intercompany accounting](/docs/reference/intercompany) for consolidation and elimination.

## Feature toggles and defaults

`companySettings` is a single row per company, edited across the **Settings → Modules** pages. These are the real column names:

<FieldTable>
  <Field name="accountingEnabled" type="boolean">Master switch for the ledger. When off, operations complete without posting journal entries.</Field>
  <Field name="timeCardEnabled" type="boolean">Turns on shop-floor time cards.</Field>
  <Field name="consoleEnabled" type="boolean">Enables MES console (shared-station) mode, where operators pin in with a 4-digit PIN.   <Field name="useMetric" type="boolean">Whether material units default to metric.</Field>
  <Field name="materialGeneratedIds" type="boolean">Whether new materials get an auto-generated readable id.</Field>
  <Field name="plmReleaseControl" type="text">The release-control policy for item revisions.</Field>
  <Field name="kanbanOutput" type="enum">What a Kanban card prints: `label`, `qrcode`, or `url`.</Field>
  <Field name="productLabelSize / shelfLabelSize" type="text">Default label sizes.</Field>
  <Field name="updateLeadTimesOnReceipt" type="boolean">Whether receiving recalculates supplier lead times.</Field>
  <Field name="purchasePriceUpdateTiming" type="enum">When purchase prices refresh: `Purchase Invoice Post` or `Purchase Order Finalize`.</Field>
  <Field name="maintenanceGenerateInAdvance / maintenanceAdvanceDays" type="boolean / number">Whether preventive maintenance is generated ahead of time, and by how many days (1–90).</Field>
  <Field name="samplingStandard" type="enum">The default inspection sampling standard.</Field>
  <Field name="enforceInspectionFourEyes" type="boolean">Requires a second person to sign off inspections.</Field>
  <Field name="qualityIssueTarget" type="number">The quality issue target used in reporting.</Field>

  <Field name="showCustomerReadableId / showSupplierReadableId" type="boolean">Whether customer/supplier readable ids are surfaced in the UI.</Field>

The row also stores **notification groups**, AP and AR email addresses, and default CC lists. See [notifications](/docs/reference/notifications) for delivery behavior.

<Callout type="warn" title="Accounting is a company-wide toggle, not per-document">
`accountingEnabled` gates the *entire* ledger: off means jobs, shipments, and invoices complete but nothing posts, with no per-transaction exceptions. Turning it on mid-stream doesn't backfill past activity.
</Callout>

## Base currency

Each company has one `baseCurrencyCode`. Carbon converts foreign-currency transactions at their exchange rate and stores posted amounts in the base currency. The value is chosen during onboarding and denominates the company's posting history. A subsidiary can use a different base currency from its parent; consolidation handles the conversion.

## Theme and tags

Carbon includes eight color themes: Modern, Brutal, Cherry, Apricot, Lemon, Mint, Blueberry, and Lavender. Theme selection is **per user** and stored in a browser cookie. Document templates have a separate theme setting. **Tags** are free-form labels scoped by company and table, so item and job tags have independent vocabularies.

## Internals and troubleshooting

Settings nav groups come from `useSettingsSubmodules.tsx` (internal gates in `internalOnlyRoutes`). Company fields are validated by `companyValidator` (`settings.models.ts:81`; `baseCurrencyCode` required at `:77`); subsidiaries by `subsidiaryValidator` (`:279`); themes by `themeValidator` (`:306`, definitions in `packages/utils/src/themes.ts`). Reads: `getCompany` (`settings.service.ts:230`, rewrites logo paths to public URLs), `getCompanies` (`:139`), `getEmployeeCompanies` (`:183`, filters supplier/customer-only memberships), `getCompanySettings` (`:283`). `companySettings` columns are in `packages/database/src/types.ts:6653`; each Modules page saves through its own small validator (`timeCardSettingsValidator`, `consoleSettingsValidator`, `materialUnitsValidator`, `materialIdsValidator`, …). Tags live in the `tag` table via `getTagsList` / `insertTag` (`shared.service.ts:929`, `:987`). Theme is set by a cookie action (`routes/x+/account+/theme.tsx`).

### I can't find the Companies or Backups settings page
Internal-operator-only (`internalOnlyRoutes`). Tenants create and switch companies through onboarding and the company picker.

### I can't see the Billing settings page
Billing needs company ownership *and* a Cloud environment; self-hosted editions and non-owners don't get it.

### Nothing posts to the ledger though jobs and invoices complete
`accountingEnabled` is off. Turn it on under Settings → Modules; it does not backfill past activity.

### My theme changed but a colleague still sees the old one
Theme is per-user (a cookie), not per-company. Document templates carry a separate theme setting.

### I can't change the base currency
By design: it's the denomination of the whole posted history, chosen at onboarding. Subsidiary/parent currency differences reconcile at consolidation, not by switching a base.

### A Modules toggle does nothing I expect
Flags are read by modules at runtime, and the feature may also be gated by plan/edition. Check licensing before assuming the toggle is broken.
</AgentContext>
