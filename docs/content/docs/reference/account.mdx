Everything on the **Account** screen is yours alone: your name and photo, the passkeys you sign in with, which topics reach you by email, the theme you look at all day. Changing them touches nothing for anyone else. Company-wide switches live under **Settings** and are gated by admin permissions; your account settings need no special permission at all. Open it from your avatar in the top bar.

## Profile

Profile is the one card everyone shares; the form itself warns *"This information will be visible to all users, so be careful what you share."*

## Signing in

Sign-in is a magic link, Google or Microsoft OAuth, or a passkey — there is no password. Which methods exist is a deployment-wide choice, not a per-user one.

<Screenshot label="Account → Security: passkey list with add/rename/remove, and the two-factor authenticator card" caption="Security holds everything you sign in with." />

**Account → Security** is where you manage your own credentials. Add a passkey through your browser's WebAuthn prompt to sign in with Face ID, Touch ID, or your device PIN; rename each one so you can tell devices apart; remove one to revoke that device. Each row shows when it was added and last used. The same screen holds the authenticator-app card — see [two-factor authentication](/docs/reference/two-factor) for setup, enforcement, and recovery. It also carries the **"Your devices"** card, which lists every live session on your account across ERP and MES and lets you sign out the ones that started after the browser you're using; see [your devices](/docs/reference/devices).

<Callout type="tip" title="Keep more than one way in">
A passkey is tied to a device. Register a second one up front, or you'll be falling back to the magic-link email when the first device goes missing.
</Callout>

## Notification preferences

**Account → Notifications** is your personal grid of topic × channel switches: in-app is always delivered, and the toggles govern email and (when connected) Slack. The default is on; a switch off is an opt-out for that one topic and channel. What raises each notification and how channels are gated is the [notifications reference](/docs/reference/notifications) — this page is where you tune it for yourself.

## Theme, language, and attributes

Your color theme is stored in a browser cookie, so it follows you per device and applies to nobody else; company-wide branding is an admin setting. Language is a preference Carbon carries for you rather than a live toggle on this screen. Your company can also define **user attributes** (a certification date, a shift preference) grouped into categories: whether one shows on your public profile depends on its category being public, and you can edit your own value only when the attribute is marked <Term id="attribute-self-managed">self-managed</Term>.

For the switches that shape the whole company, see [company settings](/docs/reference/company-settings) and [people](/docs/reference/people).

## Internals and troubleshooting

Sidebar sections come from `useAccountSubmodules.tsx:9`; `/x/account` redirects to Profile (`routes/x+/account+/_index.tsx:4`). Profile saves via `updatePublicAccount` (`account.service.ts:161`), self-scoped to the user's own `user` row. Photo uploads route through the `image-resizer` function. The passkey card renders only when `isAuthProviderEnabled("passkey")` (`security.tsx`); passkey names cap at 100 characters. Notification preferences: absent row = enabled (`notifications.tsx:130`); Slack column gated by `slackActive` (`notifications.tsx:47`); email gated by the `EMAIL_NOTIFICATIONS` plan feature (`notifications.tsx:49, :175`). Theme is a cookie (`theme.tsx`). Attribute self-editing is gated by the self-managed flag (`$userId.attribute.tsx:48`).

### Can't change your email from the Account screen
The field is deliberately read-only. Email is the sign-in identity and the notification address; changing it is an identity change handled outside self-service.

### There's no password to change
Sign-in is magic link, OAuth, or passkey. A password-change form exists in the codebase but isn't wired into any account route, so password management is not a live control here.

### The passkey card isn't showing
The `passkey` provider isn't enabled for the deployment. `AUTH_PROVIDERS` (default `email,google,azure`) decides which sign-in methods exist; it's deployment-wide, not per-user.

### There's no language picker
The per-user language preference exists in the account model, but the picker isn't surfaced on the Account sidebar in the current build; the interface follows the app's resolved locale.

### The Slack or Email notification column is missing
Slack's column needs the integration connected. Email preferences are stored regardless but only take effect when the plan includes `EMAIL_NOTIFICATIONS`.
