The **Account settings** window manages your profile, credentials, notification preferences, and display settings. These settings affect only your account and require no special permission. Company-wide configuration is under **Settings** and requires administrative access. Open **Account Settings** from your avatar menu; the window opens over the page you are on, so closing it returns you to where you were.

## Profile

Profile information is visible to other users. The form warns: *"This information will be visible to all users, so be careful what you share."*

<FieldTable>
  <Field name="First name / Last name" required>Your display name across Carbon.</Field>
  <Field name="Phone">Optional.</Field>
  <Field name="About">A short bio, capped at 160 characters.</Field>
  <Field name="Photo">Your avatar. Upload a photo, or pick a generated avatar with **Choose avatar**: choose a style (Croodles Neutral, Notionists, Lorelei, Loops, Pixel Art, Voxel Art, Voxel Bot, Planets and their neutral variants), then an avatar, and optionally a **Background** color (Reset returns to the style's own); Shuffle shows new ones.   <Field name="Email">Shown but **read-only**. Your email is your sign-in identity, so changing it is handled outside these self-service settings.</Field>

## Signing in

Carbon supports magic links, Google or Microsoft OAuth, and passkeys; it does not use passwords. Available methods are configured for the deployment, not per user.

<Screenshot label="Account → Security: passkey list with add/rename/remove, and the two-factor authenticator card" caption="Security holds everything you sign in with." />

Use **Account → Security** to manage credentials. Add a passkey through your browser's WebAuthn prompt to sign in with Face ID, Touch ID, or a device PIN. Rename passkeys to identify their devices, or remove one to revoke it. Each row shows when the passkey was added and last used. This screen also contains the authenticator-app settings. See [two-factor authentication](/docs/reference/two-factor) for setup, enforcement, and recovery.

<Callout type="tip" title="Keep more than one way in">
A passkey is tied to a device. Register a second passkey to retain access if the first device is unavailable; otherwise, use a magic link.
</Callout>

## Notification preferences

**Account → Notifications** contains topic-by-channel preferences. In-app notifications are always delivered. The toggles control email and, when connected, Slack. Each topic and channel is enabled by default; turn off a switch to opt out of that combination. The **Browser notifications** card enables or disables browser push for the current browser; like in-app, it has no per-topic switch. See [notifications](/docs/reference/notifications) for notification triggers and channel availability.

## Theme, language, and attributes

**Account → Appearance** sets the mode, text size, density and theme. **Appearance** in your user menu opens it too. At a MES console station with an operator pinned in, that menu sets only the mode (light, dark or system), since Account settings is the station's account. There are 2 types of theme. A color theme sets only the accent color. A gradient, solid or custom theme also colors the navigation, the module sidebar and the page. Your choices are stored in browser cookies, so they apply only to the current device. Company-wide branding is an administrative setting. Carbon stores a language preference, but this screen does not currently expose a language selector.

Your company can define categorized **user attributes**, such as certification dates or shift preferences. An attribute appears on your public profile only when its category is public. You can edit its value only when it is marked <Term id="attribute-self-managed">self-managed</Term>.

## Customize your navigation

You choose which modules and sub-modules appear in your navigation, and in what order. This is not part of the Account settings window: open it with **Customize** at the bottom of the left navigation, on a desktop screen.

<Steps>
<Step>Drag a module by its handle to move it. Its eye button hides it, and **Add module** brings a hidden module back.</Step>
<Step>Click a module to open its sub-modules. A sub-module's eye button hides it from the module's sidebar, and the eye button on a group name hides the whole group. The number next to the arrow counts the hidden sub-modules.</Step>
<Step>Drag a sub-module by its handle to change its place. A sub-module moves only inside its own group.</Step>
<Step>**Save** keeps every change at once. **Cancel**, or Esc, discards them. The sidebar on screen shows the result before you save.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot label="Customize open in the left navigation: the Sales module expanded to its sub-modules, one sub-module hidden with a crossed eye, and Save and Cancel at the bottom" caption="Hidden sub-modules stay in the list, dimmed, so you can bring them back." />

<Callout type="info" title="Hiding tidies the menu, it does not remove access">
A hidden sub-module leaves your sidebar, the phone menu, and the pages search offers. A direct link still opens it, and your permissions do not change. A module always keeps at least one sub-module: to hide all of them, hide the module itself. If the page a module opens on is hidden, the module opens on the next sub-module left inside it.
</Callout>

Your layout applies to you, in the current company, on every device. Settings is always shown and its sub-modules cannot be hidden.

For the switches that shape the whole company, see [company settings](/docs/reference/company-settings) and [people](/docs/reference/people).

## Internals and troubleshooting

Account settings is a modal from the `@carbon/account` package (`packages/account`), mounted once in the ERP, MES and starter shells and opened through `useAccountSettings().open(tab)`. Each pane reads an ERP API route with `useLoaderQuery` (`api+/account.profile.ts`, `account.notifications.ts`, `account.security.ts`), which also hold the actions; the MES and the starter reach them through their `/x/proxy` route. `?account=<tab>` on any `/x` URL opens it, and the old `/x/account`, `/x/account/<tab>` URLs redirect there (`routes/x+/account+/$tab.tsx`), so links in emails keep working. Profile saves via `updatePublicAccount` (`account.service.ts:161`), self-scoped to the user's own `user` row. Photo uploads are cropped and resized in the browser before upload (`prepareImageUpload` from `@carbon/files/media`). The passkey card renders only when `isAuthProviderEnabled("passkey")` (`packages/account/src/ui/SecuritySettings.tsx`); passkey names cap at 100 characters. Notification preferences: absent row = enabled (`packages/account/src/ui/NotificationSettings.tsx`); Slack column gated by `slackActive` and email gated by the `EMAIL_NOTIFICATIONS` plan feature (both from `api+/account.notifications.ts`). Appearance choices are cookies the host app's root action writes (`root.tsx`); the theme is one `theme` cookie, a theme name or `custom:<base>:<highlight>:<g|s>` (`packages/utils/src/theme-colors.ts`). Attribute self-editing is gated by the self-managed flag (`$userId.attribute.tsx:48`).

### Customize: where the layout is stored
Customize lives in the ERP rail (`PrimaryNavigation.tsx`, hidden on phones). Save posts every module row to `api+/module-preferences.tsx`, which upserts `userModulePreference` (one row per user, company and module: `position`, `hidden`, `hiddenSubmodules`, `submoduleOrder`). A sub-module's key is its link plus `?q=` when it has one (`submoduleKey`, `apps/erp/app/utils/submodule-visibility.ts`). A key that no longer matches a page is ignored, so a renamed page shows again. A new sub-module goes to the end of its group for a user who saved an order.

### Can't change your email from Account settings
The field is deliberately read-only. Email is the sign-in identity and the notification address; changing it is an identity change handled outside self-service.

### There's no password to change
Sign-in is magic link, OAuth, or passkey. A password-change form exists in the codebase but isn't wired into any account route, so password management is not a live control here.

### The passkey card isn't showing
The `passkey` provider isn't enabled for the deployment. `AUTH_PROVIDERS` (default `email,google,azure`) decides which sign-in methods exist; it's deployment-wide, not per-user.

### There's no language picker
The per-user language preference exists in the account model, but the picker isn't surfaced on the Account sidebar in the current build; the interface follows the app's resolved locale.

### The Slack or Email notification column is missing
Slack's column needs the integration connected. Email preferences are stored regardless but only take effect when the plan includes `EMAIL_NOTIFICATIONS`.

### The Browser notifications card is missing
The deployment's Web Push keys are derived from `SESSION_SECRET` (`getVapidDetails`, `@carbon/env/push.server`), so every deployment has them and the loader returns the public key. A browser without the Push API shows "not supported" instead of the **Enable** button. The subscription is one `pushSubscription` row per browser endpoint, owned by the signed-in user (`api+/push-subscription.ts`); `clearAuthCookies` (`@carbon/auth`) deletes it on sign-out through the signed `carbon-push` cookie.
</AgentContext>
