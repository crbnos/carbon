The **Account** screen manages your profile, credentials, notification preferences, and display settings. These settings affect only your account and require no special permission. Company-wide configuration is under **Settings** and requires administrative access. Open **Account** from your avatar in the top bar.

## Profile

Profile information is visible to other users. The form warns: *"This information will be visible to all users, so be careful what you share."*

## Signing in

Carbon supports magic links, Google or Microsoft OAuth, and passkeys; it does not use passwords. Available methods are configured for the deployment, not per user.

<Screenshot label="Account → Security: passkey list with add/rename/remove, and the two-factor authenticator card" caption="Security holds everything you sign in with." />

Use **Account → Security** to manage credentials. Add a passkey through your browser's WebAuthn prompt to sign in with Face ID, Touch ID, or a device PIN. Rename passkeys to identify their devices, or remove one to revoke it. Each row shows when the passkey was added and last used. This screen also contains the authenticator-app settings. See [two-factor authentication](/docs/reference/two-factor) for setup, enforcement, and recovery.

<Callout type="tip" title="Keep more than one way in">
A passkey is tied to a device. Register a second passkey to retain access if the first device is unavailable; otherwise, use a magic link.
</Callout>

## Notification preferences

**Account → Notifications** contains topic-by-channel preferences. In-app notifications are always delivered. The toggles control email and, when connected, Slack. Each topic and channel is enabled by default; turn off a switch to opt out of that combination. See [notifications](/docs/reference/notifications) for notification triggers and channel availability.

## Theme, language, and attributes

Your color theme is stored in a browser cookie, so it applies only to the current device. Company-wide branding is an administrative setting. Carbon stores a language preference, but this screen does not currently expose a language selector.

Your company can define categorized **user attributes**, such as certification dates or shift preferences. An attribute appears on your public profile only when its category is public. You can edit its value only when it is marked <Term id="attribute-self-managed">self-managed</Term>.

For the switches that shape the whole company, see [company settings](/docs/reference/company-settings) and [people](/docs/reference/people).

## Internals and troubleshooting

Sidebar sections come from `useAccountSubmodules.tsx:9`; `/x/account` redirects to Profile (`routes/x+/account+/_index.tsx:4`). Profile saves via `updatePublicAccount` (`account.service.ts:161`), self-scoped to the user's own `user` row. Photo uploads are cropped and resized in the browser before upload (`prepareImageUpload` from `@carbon/files/media`). The passkey card renders only when `isAuthProviderEnabled("passkey")` (`security.tsx`); passkey names cap at 100 characters. Notification preferences: absent row = enabled (`notifications.tsx:130`); Slack column gated by `slackActive` (`notifications.tsx:47`); email gated by the `EMAIL_NOTIFICATIONS` plan feature (`notifications.tsx:49, :175`). Theme is a cookie (`theme.tsx`). Attribute self-editing is gated by the self-managed flag (`$userId.attribute.tsx:48`).

### Can't change your email from the Account screen
The field is deliberately read-only. Email is the sign-in identity and the notification address; changing it is an identity change handled outside self-service.

### There's no password to change
Sign-in is magic link, OAuth, or passkey. A password-change form exists in the codebase but isn't wired into any account route, so password management is not a live control here.

### The passkey card isn't showing
The `passkey` provider isn't enabled for the deployment. `AUTH_PROVIDERS` (default `email,google,azure`) decides which sign-in methods exist; it's deployment-wide, not per-user.

### There's no language picker
The per-user language preference exists in the account model, but the picker isn't surfaced on the Account sidebar in the current build; the interface follows the app's resolved locale.

### The Slack or Email notification column is missing
Slack's column needs the integration connected. Email preferences are stored regardless but only take effect when the plan includes `EMAIL_NOTIFICATIONS`.
