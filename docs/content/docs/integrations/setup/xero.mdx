Connect Xero to send Carbon's customers, suppliers, invoices, bills, payments and
journals to your Xero books, and bring payments made in Xero back into Carbon.

**You need:** permission to change Settings in Carbon, a Xero login that can
connect apps to your organisation, and the same base currency on your Carbon
company as on your Xero organisation.

<Diagram name="connect" />

## Connect Xero

<Steps>
  <Step>
  ### Press Install in Carbon

  Go to **Settings → Integrations** and press **Install** on the **Xero** card.

    <Screenshot src="/screens/setup-xero-install-card.png" label="Carbon Settings → Integrations, the Xero card with its Install button" />
  </Step>
  <Step>
  ### Allow Carbon in Xero

  Sign in to Xero, pick **one** organisation and allow access. Carbon links the
  first organisation in Xero's list. If you connected other organisations to Carbon
  before, that list can start with one of them. After connecting, open the Xero
  integration and check the name next to **Connected to**.

  {/* Replace with /screens/setup-xero-consent.png */}
  <Screenshot label="Xero's consent screen for Carbon, with the organisation picker and the Allow access button" ratio="tall" />
  </Step>
  <Step>
  ### Finish in Carbon

  You land back in Carbon. Choose what syncs and run the first sync, as shown on
  the [Accounting](/docs/integrations/accounting) page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Carbon can do in Xero

When you allow access, Xero lets Carbon:

- create and update contacts (your customers and suppliers)
- create and update invoices, bills and credit notes, and create and read payments
- create bank transactions and manual journals
- read your chart of accounts and tracking categories, and create items
- read your trial balance, when you turn on accounting in Carbon

<details>
<summary>The exact Xero scopes, for your Xero admin</summary>

```

accounting.reports.trialbalance.read
```

## Something went wrong?

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| *Company base currency not configured* | Set the base currency on your Carbon company, then connect again. |
| *Currency mismatch: Your Carbon company uses …* | Your Carbon company and your Xero organisation use different base currencies. Make them match, then connect again. |
| *No Xero connections found* | Xero didn't return an organisation for your login. Connect again and pick one. |
| *Invalid state parameter* | Carbon couldn't match this sign-in to one you started: it took over an hour, or finished in another browser or Carbon account. Start again from **Settings → Integrations**. |
| Carbon asks you to reconnect when you pull the trial balance while turning on accounting | Your connection is older than the trial balance permission, or it has expired. Connect Xero again. |

## Running Carbon on your own servers?

Skip this on Carbon Cloud. The Xero app is already set up.

On your own servers, you create the Xero app once:

1. Create an app at [developer.xero.com](https://developer.xero.com), with this
   redirect URI: `https://<your Carbon address>/api/integrations/xero/oauth`.
2. Set its client ID and client secret as `XERO_CLIENT_ID` and `XERO_CLIENT_SECRET`
   in Carbon's environment.
3. For live updates from Xero, add a webhook to
   `https://<your Carbon address>/api/webhook/xero` for contacts and invoices, and set
   its key as `XERO_WEBHOOK_SECRET`.
4. Restart Carbon. Then run Xero's "Intent to receive" check on the webhook; Xero
   sends no updates until it passes.
