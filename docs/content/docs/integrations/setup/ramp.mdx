Connect Ramp to keep purchase orders, bills and card spend in sync between Ramp
and Carbon.

**You need:** permission to change Settings in Carbon, and a Ramp login with the
**Admin** or **Business Owner** role.

<Diagram name="connect" />

## First, one question: who keeps your books?

Ramp lets only one accounting system connect to it. So before you connect, Carbon
asks which system that is.

<Diagram name="ramp-modes" />

- **Carbon is my accounting system.** Card charges and bills from Ramp flow into
  Carbon's books, and reimbursements arrive as drafts for you to review. Once the
  card liability account is set, Carbon also sends your chart of accounts to Ramp so
  your team can code spend there.
- **Another system posts my ledger.** Your other accounting system stays connected
  to Ramp and keeps posting the spend. Carbon only reads back which bills were paid.

In both modes, Carbon sends purchase orders and draft bills to Ramp, and creates
their vendors there. Two things to know:

- **Each draft bill is sent once.** If you change the invoice in Carbon afterwards,
  update the bill in Ramp too.
- **Suppliers need contact details.** To create a vendor in Ramp, a supplier needs a
  contact email and a country on one of its locations (plus a state for US
  suppliers). Without them, that supplier's bills don't reach Ramp. Carbon doesn't
  update a vendor in Ramp when the supplier changes later.

## Connect Ramp

<Steps>
  <Step>
  ### Press Install in Carbon

  Go to **Settings → Integrations** and press **Install** on the **Ramp** card.

    <Screenshot src="/screens/setup-ramp-install-card.png" label="Carbon Settings → Integrations, the Ramp card with its Install button" />
  </Step>
  <Step>
  ### Answer the question

  Pick **Carbon is my accounting system** or **Another system posts my ledger**,
  then press **Continue**.

    <Screenshot src="/screens/setup-ramp-mode.png" label="Carbon's How will you use Ramp? dialog with the two choices" />
  </Step>
  <Step>
  ### Allow Carbon in Ramp

  Sign in to Ramp as an Admin or Business Owner, and allow access.

  {/* Replace with /screens/setup-ramp-consent.png */}
  <Screenshot label="Ramp's consent screen for Carbon, listing the access requested" ratio="tall" />
  </Step>
  <Step>
  ### Finish in Carbon

  You land back in Carbon. Open the Ramp integration and choose what syncs under
  **Sync**. Everything is on to start with.

  If you picked **Carbon is my accounting system**, also fill in the accounts under
  **Accounts**. Card spend can't sync until **Card liability account** is set.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Something went wrong?

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| *Another system is connected to Ramp as its accounting system* | Your other accounting system already holds Ramp's one spot. Uninstall Ramp in Carbon, then connect again and pick **Another system posts my ledger**. Or disconnect the other system in Ramp, then open the Ramp integration in Carbon and save its settings. |
| *Ramp denied the connection* | Ramp refused access, for example because it was cancelled. Connect again and allow access, signed in as an Admin or Business Owner. |
| *The Ramp connection expired* | Carbon couldn't match this sign-in to one you started: it took over an hour, or finished in another browser or Carbon account. Start again from **Settings → Integrations**. |
| A bill didn't reach Ramp, and **Sync Activity** says the supplier *needs a contact email*, *a country* or *a two-letter state* | Add the missing detail to the supplier in Carbon. The bill is retried on the next sync. |
| *Ramp connected but setup didn't finish* | Open the Ramp integration and save its settings to finish setup. If that fails too, uninstall Ramp and connect again. |

Picked the wrong answer to the first question? Uninstall Ramp in Carbon and
connect again.

## Running Carbon on your own servers?

Skip this on Carbon Cloud. The Ramp app is already set up.

On your own servers, you create the Ramp app once:

1. In Ramp's developer settings, create an app and turn on the **refresh token**
   grant.
2. Open **Set scopes** and turn on every scope in the first list above (it includes
   the second). If one is missing, Ramp refuses the connection with
   `invalid_scope`, and Carbon shows *Ramp denied the connection*.
3. Add this redirect URL, where your Carbon address is the one in `ERP_URL`:
   `https://<your Carbon address>/api/integrations/ramp/oauth`.
4. Set `RAMP_CLIENT_ID` and `RAMP_CLIENT_SECRET` in Carbon's environment, then
   restart Carbon. Until then the Ramp card says **Not Configured**.

<Screenshot src="/screens/setup-ramp-scopes-1.png" label="Ramp's Set scopes dialog, top half, with Carbon's scopes switched on" />
<Screenshot src="/screens/setup-ramp-scopes-2.png" label="Ramp's Set scopes dialog, scrolled down, with the rest of Carbon's scopes switched on" caption="Ramp's Set scopes dialog. Check your switches against the lists above rather than copying this picture." />

Carbon tells Ramp where to send updates by itself when you connect. Ramp needs to
reach your Carbon address over the internet for that. If it can't, Carbon still
catches up every hour.
