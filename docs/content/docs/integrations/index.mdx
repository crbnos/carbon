Carbon provides first-party integrations with third-party applications. Enable and configure them per company under **Settings → Integrations**.

Integrations are an **Enterprise Edition** capability. On [Carbon Cloud](https://app.carbon.ms), they require the **Business** plan. Self-hosted deployments require a commercial license. See [Licensing](/docs/platform/licensing). **Email** and **Exchange Rates** are available on every edition and plan.

## How a connection works

Each integration has a system-wide **definition** and a per-company **activation**.

- The **definition** specifies required fields such as API keys, OAuth tokens, and mapping options.
- The **activation** is a `companyIntegration` record: an `active` flag plus a `metadata` object holding
  credentials and settings. Carbon validates this metadata against the definition before activation.

Connections are established one of three ways, depending on the app:

| Style | Apps | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Provider settings | Email | Pick a provider and fill in its fields. |
| API key | Linear, Paperless Parts, Exchange Rates | Paste a key (and any options). |
| OAuth | Xero, Jira, Onshape, Slack | Authorize Carbon with the provider; tokens are stored for you. |

<Callout type="note">
Disconnecting sets `active = false` but retains stored credentials. To remove credentials, clear the integration settings before disconnecting.
</Callout>

## What's available depends on your deployment

<Callout type="warn">
Available integrations depend on deployment configuration. **Xero**, **Jira**, and **Onshape** appear only when their OAuth clients are configured server-side; see [Environment variables](/docs/platform/self-hosting/environment-variables). **Slack** is unavailable in controlled (ITAR) environments.
</Callout>
