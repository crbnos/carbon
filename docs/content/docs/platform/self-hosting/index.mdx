---
title: Self-hosting
description: Run the whole Carbon stack on your own infrastructure, a single VPS or your own AWS account, instead of Carbon Cloud.
---

Self-hosted Carbon runs the ERP and MES applications, Supabase data plane (Postgres, Auth, REST, Realtime, Storage, and Edge Functions), Redis, and Inngest on infrastructure you control. [Carbon Cloud](https://app.carbon.ms) is the managed alternative.

<Callout type="warn" title="Self-hosting runs Community Edition (CE)">
Self-hosted Carbon uses **AGPLv3** by default. If users access a modified version over a network, you must offer them its source. A commercial license is required to use Business features under `packages/ee` or to keep modifications private from users. See [Licensing](/docs/platform/licensing).
</Callout>
