Demo data populates a company with a coherent industry dataset covering items, BOMs, routings, customers, suppliers, orders, production, quality, maintenance, training, accounting, scheduling, and material planning.

Use demo data to explore, demonstrate, or test Carbon with realistic records. Apply it under **Settings → Demo Data** or select **Use a demo template** during sign-up.

## The templates

Each template provides linked records across sales, purchasing, production, quality, and accounting:

<FieldTable>
  <Field name="Aerospace &amp; Satellite">A satellite manufacturer, from RFQs through built and inspected flight hardware.</Field>
  <Field name="Robotics OEM">A robotics maker building assemblies to order.</Field>
  <Field name="Precision Manufacturing">A machine shop quoting and cutting precision parts.</Field>
  <Field name="Motor Assembly">An automotive supplier assembling motors.</Field>
</FieldTable>

Seeded dates are relative to the application date and include recent orders, jobs in progress, and upcoming due dates. Parts include thumbnails, and each template includes a 3D assembly.

Templates include normal and exceptional states: lost and expired quotes, partially shipped orders, paid and partially paid invoices, voided receipts, closed accounting periods, and succeeded, failed, and skipped workflow runs.

Related stock movements and payment settlements keep quantities and invoice balances consistent. Posted invoices, payments, memos, receipts, and shipments include journal entries for financial reports.

Jobs span every work center over three weeks and include active and recently completed operations.

The signed-in user receives a job, shift, assignments, and an open time card. Templates also configure sales and storage rules, purchase approvals, and customer portals. They do not add uploaded files, so [Documents](/docs/reference/documents) remains empty.

<Callout type="note" title="A template is not a backup">
Carbon generates demo data from current dataset definitions rather than a stored backup. To copy another company's actual data, use a [backup](/docs/platform/backups).
</Callout>

## Apply a template

Under **Settings → Demo Data**, select **Apply** for a template. Carbon first snapshots current company data, then replaces it with the template. The operation continues if you leave the page and reports each phase.

After applying the template, Carbon runs MRP and scheduling in the background to populate Material Planning, Demand Forecasts, Scheduling, and Priorities. If this run fails, the demo data remains and the regular three-hour MRP run retries the work.

<Callout type="warn" title="Some companies can't take demo data">
Carbon cannot apply a template when the company contains records that cannot be deleted:

A company with intercompany customers or suppliers fails with "Seed: this company trades with other companies in its group (N intercompany customer/supplier record(s)). Demo data can only be applied to a company without intercompany partners."

A company with posted card transactions fails with "Seed: this company has N posted or voided card transaction(s), which cannot be deleted. Demo data can only be applied to a company without posted card transactions."
</Callout>

<Screenshot src="/screens/platform-demo-data.png" label="Settings → Demo Data, the Apply a demo template card listing the four industry templates with their Apply buttons" caption="Applying a template snapshots the company first, then seeds the industry story." ratio="wide" />

When it finishes, the **"Demo data applied"** card asks you to decide:

- **"Keep"** accepts the demo data and permanently deletes the saved copy of your previous data, so it can't be reverted afterwards. Carbon asks you to type **"I understand, keep the demo data"** to confirm.
- **"Revert"** puts back exactly what was here before.

Only one demo-data change can be active per company. Application is atomic: if it fails, Carbon writes no rows and displays the error.

<Callout type="tip" title="Revert works even after a hiccup">
If a revert seems stuck, the card says so after a few minutes and offers "Retry revert". The saved copy of your data is kept until the revert succeeds, so retrying is always safe.
</Callout>

## Demo data at sign-up

The same templates power onboarding. When anyone creates a new company, the data step asks **"How would you like to start?"**:

<FieldTable>
  <Field name="Use a demo template">Sets up sample customers, suppliers, parts and orders for the industry you pick. They appear shortly after you finish.</Field>
  <Field name="Restore from a backup">Sets the new company up from an uploaded [backup](/docs/platform/backups) of another Carbon company environment. Carbon staff only.</Field>
  <Field name="I don't need data">Starts with a clean, empty environment.</Field>
</FieldTable>

Choosing a template then asks **"Which best describes your company?"** and seeds the matching industry. See [Getting started](/docs/reference/onboarding) for the rest of the wizard.

<Callout type="info" title="Who can see the Demo Data page">
Anyone with permission to update settings can open Settings → Demo Data.
</Callout>

## Troubleshooting

### The Demo Data page isn't in my Settings menu
The page needs permission to update settings. Ask an admin to grant it to your role.

### I applied a template and want my old data back
Use "Revert" on the "Demo data applied" card. Carbon saved a copy of your data immediately before applying, and the card stays open until you choose Keep or Revert — reverting puts back exactly what was there before.

### The apply or revert looks stuck
It keeps running even if you leave the page. If it's been more than a few minutes, the card shows "This is taking longer than expected" and offers "Retry revert" — safe to click, because the saved copy of your data is kept until the revert succeeds.

### Can't apply a second template
Only one demo-data change can be open per company. Resolve the pending one first — Keep or Revert on the review card (or Dismiss if it failed).

### Applying failed with an error
Applying is all-or-nothing: a failure writes no rows and your data is unchanged. The card shows the job's error next to "Failed"; Dismiss clears it, and you can apply again.

### Applying failed with "Seed: this company trades with other companies in its group"
Expected refusal. The company has customers or suppliers linked to another company in its group (intercompany partners), which the demo-data wipe cannot remove. Nothing was written. Apply the template to a company without intercompany partners.

### Applying failed with "Seed: this company has N posted or voided card transaction(s)"
Expected refusal. Posted and voided card transactions cannot be deleted, so the demo-data wipe cannot clear the company. Nothing was written. Apply the template to a company without posted card transactions.

### Material Planning, Scheduling or Priorities are empty right after applying
Planning runs as a separate background step once the demo data is committed: MRP for the company, then scheduling for every location with Ready, In Progress or Paused jobs. Give it a moment. If that step failed, the demo data stays and MRP fills in on its regular run every three hours.

### The Webhooks, API keys or Audit log screens are empty after applying
Expected. Templates never seed credentials, webhooks or audit history, and applying a template leaves any existing webhooks and API keys in place.

### The Documents list is empty after applying a template
Expected. Templates seed records, not files: no uploads are added, so Documents and file attachments stay empty. Part thumbnails and the 3D assembly ship with the app rather than as uploaded files.

### The demo dates look recent — is that real activity?
Yes, by design: every seeded date is relative to the day the template was applied, so the company always looks live with recent orders and jobs in progress.
</AgentContext>
