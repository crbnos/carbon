## A customizable sidebar

The sidebar can be customized, so you can reorder modules and hide the ones you don't use.

<Accordion title="Improvements">

- Shelf-life history and an inventory history menu.
- Reorder line items on documents.
- Filter the inventory table by storage unit.
- Expand or collapse every level of a BOM explorer at once.
- Traceability search disambiguates readable ids with job and tracking context.
- Hide empty work centers on the schedule.
- Audit logs snapshot related records.
- New fields on CSV imports.
- Email notifications are a plan feature, shown as such in settings.
- Onboarding's plan selection and model viewer were refined.
- Academy: Historical Costs, Purchase Invoices, Purchasing Planning, and Supplier Pricing videos.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Fixes">

- Make-to-order jobs issue to inventory and create cost of goods sold on shipment.
- Unlinking a job from a sales order asks for confirmation.
- Released job material operations validate correctly.
- The BOM explorer popover no longer disappears too quickly.
- Realtime reconnects when the tab becomes visible again.
- Notification links validate job parameters.
- Sidebar scrollbars fixed.

</Accordion>
