The line could not see what each station was short, and nobody could tell how many of an item could be built now. This release maps what each station needs, follows each serial unit from the pick to the finished good, and shows how many of an item can be made today.

<Callout type="warn" title="A change to know">
Label templates set on a printer were not carried over. Set them again in `Settings → Printing → Label Templates`, per [label type and media size](/docs/reference/printing#label-templates).
</Callout>

## Line feed and material requests

The MES **Line feed** page shows each station in routing order as Short, Bring, Waiting or Ready. A station shows its raw shelf, kit rack and station stock, and moves parts in one step. Operators ask for parts from a searchable list, and **Material requests** shows every open request at the location, live.

## Serial travelers

One serial traveler label follows a unit from the pick to the finished good. [Scanning](/docs/reference/mes#serial-units-on-later-operations) a kit or sub-assembly label claims it into the next unit that needs it. Turn on **Tracked parts must be scanned in** in `Settings → Production` to refuse a completion until every serial and batch part is scanned.

## Scrap and issue from any operation

**Scrap parts** is on every MES operation view. One [scrap](/docs/reference/scrap) can cover loose parts, a kit's contents, chosen serials or part of a batch, and asks for a replacement. Scanning an item label [issues](/docs/reference/mes#issuing-material) one piece per scan, never more than the job needs, and only from stock at the job's location.

## How many can be made now

A made item's Inventory tab shows its **Buildable Quantity** through every level of the bill of materials: on hand, ready to finish, waiting on parts, and the components that limit more. It also lists the jobs in production that are short of parts. The same answer is in the API and MCP.

## Delivery date check on sales orders

Set **Delivery Date Check** in `Settings → Sales` to check a promised date on a [sales order line](/docs/reference/sales-orders#delivery-date-check) against lead time, stock or capacity. A date that fails shows the earliest date that works, with a button to use it. Keeping the date anyway is recorded, and can start a workflow on the Business plan.

## Operation time analysis

`Production → Time Analysis` compares an item's operations in box plots, by setup time per run or by labor or machine time per unit. Drill into one operation by method version, month and operator. Runs far outside the usual range are set aside, with a toggle to include them.

## Correct and close the books

- [Import journal entries](/docs/reference/import-export#journal-entries) from a CSV as Draft or Posted. Each journal imports whole or not at all.
- **Change GL Account** on a [posted invoice line](/docs/reference/invoices#changing-a-posted-lines-account) instead of voiding the invoice, and [reopen a closed period](/docs/reference/period-close#reopening).
- Pay suppliers from a credit card account, and Ramp card bills post as a supplier payment from the card.
- Payments post on their payment date, and Draft payments show as pending on invoices and aging.
- **Adjust for Rounding** on a purchase invoice matches the total the supplier charged.

## Supplier stages, shipping dates and quote requests

[Suppliers](/docs/reference/suppliers-and-customers) get a Stage (Prospect, Sampling, Qualified, Production) beside their status. Purchase orders record [shipped and expected receipt dates](/docs/reference/purchase-orders#shipping-details). **Request Quote** on an item drafts one RFQ per supplier for the part or its whole bill of materials.

## Admin force delete

**Force Delete (Admin)** on a purchase order, invoice, receipt or payment asks for a reason and the document number. A second Admin approves it. Carbon then unapplies payments and reverses stock and recognized revenue before it deletes the document.

## Label templates and document printers

[Label templates](/docs/reference/printing#label-templates) are now set once per label type and media size, not per printer. Each printer has a [DPI setting](/docs/reference/printing#printer-routes), so labels print at their real size, and five more [label sizes](/docs/reference/printing#label-sizes) are available. Packing slips and sales invoices print to a location's Documents printer.
