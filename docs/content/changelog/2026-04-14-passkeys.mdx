AI clients can now work with your Carbon data, and nobody needs a password to sign in.

## The Carbon MCP server

The [MCP server](/api/mcp) lets Claude, Cursor and other AI clients work with your data through Carbon's tools.

## Passkeys

Passkeys arrive for passwordless sign-in. Register one from your [account](/docs/reference/account), sign in with Face ID, Touch ID or a security key, and rename or remove passkeys you no longer use.

## Console mode

Console mode runs a shared shop-floor device without a personal session. See [MES](/docs/reference/mes).

## Five new languages

Carbon is now available in Hindi, French, Polish, Portuguese and Russian, and the login page is translated too.

<Accordion title="Improvements">

- Method types are now named for what they do: Pull from Inventory, Purchase to Order, and Make to Order (previously Pick, Buy, Make).
- CSV import tools.
- A Recalculate unit cost button on the item master.
- Purchased lead time is updated from a weighted yearly average.
- API key expiration dates are validated more strictly.
- Active time cards count toward duration calculations.
- Risk updates carry more detail.

</Accordion>

- External notes show on the digital quote.
