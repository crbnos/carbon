One company group, several sets of books, and rules that stop a bad inventory transaction before it posts.

## Multi-entity accounting

Multi-entity accounting lets a company group run several legal entities with their own books, with [intercompany](/docs/reference/intercompany) trade between them.

## Storage rules

A [storage rule](/docs/reference/storage-rules) runs when an inventory transaction posts. It checks the line against a condition you define and either blocks the transaction, or warns and lets the user continue. Rules attach to [items](/docs/reference/items) or [work centers](/docs/reference/work-centers).

## Shelf life

[Shelf life](/docs/reference/shelf-life) is tracked on items and batches.

## Traceability explorer, rebuilt

The [traceability explorer](/docs/reference/traceability) has been rebuilt for large genealogies, and dates across the app now follow your locale.
