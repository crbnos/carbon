Multi-entity accounting lets a company group run several legal entities with their own books. A [storage rule](/docs/reference/storage-rules) runs when an inventory transaction posts: it checks the line against a condition you define and either blocks the transaction or warns and lets the user continue; rules attach to items or work centers. Shelf life is tracked on items and batches.
