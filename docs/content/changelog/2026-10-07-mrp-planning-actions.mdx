MRP used to make one suggestion, Order, worked out again on every page load and then thrown away. A planner could not dismiss it, hand it to someone, or see that an order already placed should move. This release turns that suggestion into a list of work.

## Planning actions

[Planning](/docs/reference/planning#planning-actions) now writes seven kinds of action after every MRP run: Order, Make, Expedite, Defer, Cancel, Increase and Decrease. The change actions are worked out against your open purchase order lines and jobs.

Each row in the purchasing and production planning grids lists its actions with one button each:

- **Apply** changes supply that is not yet committed.
- **Review on PO** or **Review on Job** opens supply that is.
- **Dismiss** hides an action, and it stays hidden on later runs until the suggestion changes.
- **Assign to Me** sets the owner, and MRP keeps a hand-set owner on later runs.

Every action has a [responsible employee](/docs/reference/planning#planning-ownership), taken from the item, then its item group, then the location, then the company default. A [reschedule tolerance](/docs/reference/planning#reschedule-tolerance), 7 days by default, keeps small date differences out of the list. Both are set under `Settings → Planning`.

## Forecasts no longer double count

A [demand forecast](/docs/reference/forecast) of 10 and a sales order for 10 used to plan 20. Open sales order lines and job materials now consume the forecast for the same part and location: the same week first, then back up to 4 weeks, then forward 1. Only what is left drives demand. Change the reach on the Forecast Consumption card under `Settings → Planning`.

## Planning horizon and run time

A long planning window buried next week's work. The [planning horizon](/docs/reference/planning#planning-horizon) is a date per row, and a row shows only the actions and suggested orders due by it. MRP still plans the whole window. Set a company default under `Settings → Planning` and override it on an item's planning tab.

MRP runs every 3 hours by default. You can now [run it once a day](/docs/reference/planning#when-it-runs) at an hour you pick, on your company's time zone.

The weekly projection in both grids is one Stock Availability strip in place of 48 columns, with new Latest Order Date, 1st Negative On Hand and Item Group columns.

## Date range filters

Date columns across Carbon filter to a range, with a From and a To picker. Leave either side empty for "on or after" or "on or before". Both ends are inclusive, and the filter lives in the URL, so it works with saved views and links.

It is on [jobs](/docs/reference/jobs), [sales orders](/docs/reference/sales-orders), [quotes](/docs/reference/quotes), [purchase orders](/docs/reference/purchase-orders), [invoices](/docs/reference/invoices), [payments](/docs/reference/payments), journal entries, [receipts](/docs/reference/receipts), [shipments](/docs/reference/shipments), [picking lists](/docs/reference/picking), [issues](/docs/reference/issues), gauges, change notices and holidays, among others. The [batch builder](/docs/reference/batching#building-a-batch)'s Custom due range can be open-ended too.

## Job due dates in the MES

The [MES](/docs/reference/mes) showed a sub-assembly operation's scheduler target as the job's Due Date, and that target was weeks too early. The scheduler counted the sub-assembly's lead time on top of its own operations. That double count is gone. The MES now shows the job's due date, with the operation's own target beside it as "Operation needed by".

Schedule cards that are past due, or in a batch that finishes after a job's due date, turn red. Cards due today or tomorrow are orange.

## Release jobs in bulk

Select jobs in the Jobs list and choose **Release Jobs** from the actions menu. Draft and Planned jobs in the selection are [released](/docs/reference/jobs), and any job Carbon could not release is named.

## Revisions keep their setup

A new [item](/docs/reference/items) revision started with default planning and no suppliers. It now inherits the source revision's batch size, scrap percent, lead time, reorder policy per location, item group, preferred supplier, and supplier parts with their price breaks. Costs, sale price and stock are not copied.

## Self-hosting

Sign-in was broken on [self-hosted](/docs/platform/self-hosting) builds behind a proxy that ends TLS. Carbon now treats the host of `ERP_URL` and `MES_URL` as an allowed origin, so set both.

- Assets are compressed once at build and served from memory. The ERP's app bundle went from 19,236 to 61,323 requests a second in our test.
- Carbon checks its whole [environment](/docs/platform/self-hosting/environment-variables) at startup and reports every problem together, grouped, without printing a secret. No deployment has to change what it sets.
- Migrations and seeding run on a smaller `bootstrap` image, which replaces the `ops` image.
